# Green Gallows — Blackwood / Xylon revision
Date: 17 November 2026

## What was saved
The project’s existing season Markdown and HTML tracker were updated together. This bundle contains the revised copies, exact pre-revision backups, this change log and the validation results. The earlier Four-Room Audit describes the previous draft; it has not been rewritten to make its findings disappear.

## Pilot changes
- Felix recognises Vykra methods at the Narrows and uses the constable’s informant “birds.” A ticket-office porter and the hired-driver trail explain his arrival at Blackwood.
- Grise’s inventory names the missing Lambrick purse. Miriam’s lie, Jennifer’s observation of three tickets and the compromised driver give Grise evidence of the escape.
- Miriam’s birthday conversation includes a real admission that Alice saved her. Her rare request, and the promise of tomorrow, give Alice a reason to choose not to follow.
- Jamie’s failed demonstration advice receives an observable response; Nathaniel then chooses actual professional recognition from the Chancellor’s men over following his brother.
- Two keys remain distinct: Byron’s copied iron Guard key opens the core stair; Ajax’s copied brass service key opens the Blackwood cabinet. The generator’s return bar is a separate component.
- Miriam performs the pilot blackout. Works control can reverse this trip. It is not the permanent darkness caused by releasing Morgana.
- The driver’s eavesdropping supplies the midnight rendezvous. Jennie uses the familiar vehicle, a prepared likeness and silence, rather than a broad new shapeshifting power.
- Xylon is delivered through the kiss. Miriam discards both blades, resists and is forced over the rail. The episode ends on her fall and Jamie alone with Eldgard.
- Locket activation is placed in the closing cross-cut, beside Miriam’s fall. The river transfer is shown in Episode 2, rather than leaving a magical arrival unexplained for a quarter-hour.

## Directly affected episodes
### Episode 2
The continuous opening is the first night / dawn of 3 November (48 days remaining): river transfer, Felix’s rescue, death check, the Harding death announcement and Nathaniel–Byron grief, then Alice’s morgue vigil and the resurrection. The escape is into daylight. Wren is reached after sunrise. The 47-day card marks 4 November before the main coffin/funeral sequence.

Felix distinguishes pigment before the river from frost afterward. He saw resistance, not Jennie’s unveiled face, and he does not possess knowledge the scene never gave him. His existing informants already supplied the Bounds address.

### Episode 4
Ajax sold Jamie an unrecorded copy of the Blackwood cabinet key for four hundred guineas; Lambrick’s two hundred bought the original access. Official keys would leave a family requisition trail. Ajax did not perform the pilot cut.

The dock confrontation still reveals “your brother paid me,” but now connects Miriam’s Blackwood task to Jamie’s plan. Nathaniel’s choice costs Alice a witness too.

The Lost One’s kind memory of Jamie is explicitly from the real lovers’ first carriage encounter. The corresponding line is planted in Episode 1. Felix’s proposed testimony follows the facts he witnessed. Winter’s kiss remains the separate frost-producing poison used to murder him.

### Episode 7
Alice accepted Grise’s contract two mornings after the river, not the following dawn. A recovered memory describes the carriage, the false face and Miriam discarding her weapons. It establishes an impostor, not Jennie’s identity: Miriam never saw Jennie after the carriage escaped. The exact request to the frost child remains withheld until Episode 10.

Absolute language that would now falsely make Miriam cowardly in every meaningful situation is narrowed to the sisters’ childhood wound.

### Episode 8
The Lost One’s deliberate submission to the cage is no longer equated with Miriam voluntarily choosing death at the bridge.

### Episode 9
Grise admits ordering the Xylon interception. Jennie admits wearing Jamie’s face; Jennifer admits finding the driver. Jennie’s regret does not erase her choice not to stop. Alice needs their help but demands signed statements and forbids borrowed faces that night.

### Episode 10
Jennie’s service and message-running refer to the Xylon kiss, not a poured drink. Miriam’s last request preserves love for Alice without rewriting the forced jump as voluntary sacrifice.

Alice supplies the twins’ statements at Westminster; the Record names Miriam, the method and those responsible. Helping defend London is not a pardon. Legal consequences remain for investigation; forgiveness is not presumed.

## New device limits chosen for continuity
1. Xylon compels movement; it does not create frost. Its command ends with Miriam’s first cardiac arrest and is spent. The Lost One’s later movements and mortality do not restart it.
2. Xylon-compelled conduct cannot satisfy the willing Tithe or freely given release price.
3. Jennie’s face-veil is prepared for one likeness and short-lived. It cannot copy voice, height, memory, blood or cold immunity. She remains silent during the deception.
4. The reversible Blackwood trip, Ajax’s separate Lantern Night outer-feed attack and the finale’s permanent extinction remain distinct events.

These are fictional narrative mechanics, not real-world toxicology.
