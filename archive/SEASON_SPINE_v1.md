# The Green Gallows — Season Spine v1

Draft foundation for the rebuilt season. Built from `REBOOT_Locked_Decisions.md`. Changes first, incidents second: each episode is defined by what it changes, not by what happens in it.

## The promise

**We follow Nathaniel Harding as he hunts his brother's killer, choosing the people in front of him over the family that made him, until he must decide whether London's light is worth a child in a freezer.**

Under it: **Alice Bounds learns her sister is dead from the thing that loves her like one, until she must let it choose.**

The audience's pleasure: an investigation, a forbidden intimacy, and dread that keeps climbing.

**Clock card:** THE LIGHT HAS 49 DAYS.

## Character ladders

Want → the compromise that seems reasonable → what success costs → the choice they postpone → the final action that shows who they've become.

**Nathaniel.** Wants his father's respect and a seat in the work → hides the Frost Killer while hunting his brother's killer, lying to his father and to Nixon → loses Nixon, then Alice's trust, then London's light → postpones choosing between the family and the girl in his workshop → opens the pod and puts out the light.

**Alice.** Wants Miriam's killer, and wants to keep "Miriam" → takes Josephine's money to spy on Nathaniel → loses the sister illusion and Nathaniel's trust on the same night → postpones choosing between holding on and letting go → offers the Lost One the ship, and lets her choose.

**The Lost One.** Wants to free her mother → wears Miriam and lets Alice believe → learns she was a tool too: her mother sent her to imprint the boys → postpones choosing between her mother and Alice → stays.

**Nixon.** Wants to belong to the family that raised him → hunts the Frost Killer and keeps Eldgard's secrets, including Jamie → loses Nathaniel → postpones choosing between obeying and knowing → breaks his uncle out of Newgate with Ada's blood on his cuffs, then holds the Guard for London instead of the Hardings.

## The four threads

**Nathaniel and Nixon.** Ep 2: Nixon gets the keys and the father Nathaniel wanted, and is told Jamie lives. Ep 3: Nathaniel lies to Nixon about the Frost Killer, and Nixon lies to Nathaniel about Jamie. Ep 5: Nathaniel finds Jamie and learns Nixon knew. That's the first break. **Ep 8, the point of no return:** Nixon leads the raid on the workshop and takes the Lost One. Hours later he learns what the Hardings did to Hargrave, and understands what he just did to her. Ep 9: they fight on the same side, but not as friends. Ep 10: changed, not restored.

**The two imprints.** Ep 1 cold open: 1780, the crash. Ep 3 cold open: a 1873 fragment, a child's room and green light at the window, which could be a dream. Eps 3–5: Nathaniel's memory flickers whenever he's near the Lost One. **Ep 6: imprint reveal #1.** The full 1873 night in Nathaniel's memory: the creature, his father, the swinging lantern. His genius was planted, and his father erased him. **Ep 9: imprint reveal #2.** The cold open shows the same night from the offspring's side: Jamie's room first. Morgana tells the Lost One she did exactly what her mother asked. Same night, two readings.

**The sisters.** Ep 2: Alice gets "Miriam" back. Eps 3–6: closeness. The counting game, oranges, the fence. Ep 7 cold open: the Bounds girls under the bed, counting. **Ep 7: the break.** Alice's hunt for Miriam's killer leads to Jennie, then Grise, who tells her what Miriam paid to join the Vykra: their stepfather, drowned at sixteen. The Lost One confirms it from Miriam's memory and confesses on Miriam's behalf, and that's how Alice knows. Miriam would never have told her. "You're not her." "Then why do I have her? Why do I love you so much?" Alice walks out. **Ep 8 morning:** she comes back. "Nine is the one where we get up." Ep 10: Alice offers her the ship. She stays.

## The episodes

### 1. The Exhibition
**Change:** the secret gets out. **POV:** Nathaniel and Jamie.
- Jamie's initiation, days earlier: the stair, the vault, Morgana in the pod. "Where is my child?"
- The Exhibition at the Works: Nathaniel's gauntlet unveiled, his proudest night. Nixon home from the marshes a hero. Ada polishing lamps and straightening Nixon's collar: "love."
- Cross-cut: Jamie at Harding House breaks the ornament; the offspring tears through him and out of the window. Miriam waits at the bridge; Jennie's knife; the river.
- **Heaviest moment:** Miriam, drowning: "Go back for my sister."
- **Tag:** the lamps of the Exhibition flicker. In the dark, Morgana: "My child is out."

### 2. The Wake
**Change:** two grieving siblings get missions. **POV:** Alice.
- The Lost One walks out of the river and finds Alice. Alice gets her sister back.
- Jamie's public funeral, closed coffin. Nathaniel takes a shard of strange glass from his brother's room and vows to find the killer.
- Grise gives Alice her first contract: get close to Nathaniel Harding. Paid by Josephine.
- Eldgard makes Nixon his right hand and gives him the keys.
- **Tag:** Eldgard shows Nixon the locked room at the top of the house. "He's alive. Nathaniel must never know."

### 3. The Frost Killer
**Change:** Nathaniel takes a side. **POV:** Nixon and Nathaniel.
- The Lost One kills a man who attacks Alice. Her cold, by touch. London names a Frost Killer, and blames Jamie's death on it.
- The Guard hunts her through the Christmas market. Ajax hunts too, for Lambrick, who has heard the killer leaves frost like gallowglass.
- Nathaniel finds the sisters and hides them in his workshop. His tech can manage her cold. First lie to Nixon.
- **Tag:** the Lost One studies Nathaniel's face: "I know you."

### 4. The Hearing
**Change:** the light's failure goes public. **POV:** Lambrick, Tamara, Nathaniel.
- Lambrick attacks the Harding Charter in Parliament with yield figures smuggled out by the Works' chief engineer. The lamps flicker in the chamber on cue.
- Tamara properly introduced: the codebreaker, the engagement as a merger on both sides.
- Nixon visits Marsh in Newgate. The madman pinches his chin and whispers Hargrave. Nixon walks away not understanding.
- Nathaniel and Alice trace the shard to the Works. The romance warms; Alice reports to Josephine and hates it.
- **Tag:** Eldgard to Josephine: when the mother dies, they use the child. The audience knows the stakes before the characters do.

### 5. The Locked Room (midseason)
**Change:** the family's lie cracks. **POV:** Nathaniel.
- Byron's slip points them upstairs. The heist into Harding House during the Lambrick engagement dinner: Alice's Vykra skills, Nathaniel's tech, the Lost One's cold.
- Nathaniel finds Jamie alive, frost in his veins.
- The Lost One touches Jamie's hand: a flash of 1889, and of something older.
- Nixon catches them. Nathaniel realises Nixon knew. The first break.
- **Tag:** Nathaniel to Nixon: "How long?" Nixon can't answer.

### 6. The Remembering (aftermath)
**Change:** Nathaniel learns what his father did to him. **POV:** Nathaniel and Byron. **No set piece.**
- Nathaniel at Jamie's bedside. **Imprint reveal #1:** the full 1873 night. His genius was planted; his father erased him.
- Byron confesses to tending Jamie: "You were always enough."
- Nathaniel and Alice: the night they finally stop circling.
- **Tag:** Eldgard orders the East End cut to keep the West lit. The green dies street by street across the East.

### 7. The Girl Who Isn't
**Change:** every love Alice has turns out to have been bought. **POV:** Alice.
- The frozen East End: bodies in doorways. Byron, who went to help, among them. The Hardings blame the Frost Killer; mobs form. The massacre repeats, smaller.
- Alice's hunt reaches Jennie, then Grise: Miriam's entry price was their stepfather.
- The Lost One confirms and confesses. "You're not her." "Why do I love you so much?" Alice walks out.
- The same night, Nathaniel learns his mother hired Alice. Two loves break for the same reason.
- Lambrick's coal is the only heat left. Eldgard's terms: Nathaniel marries Tamara, the East gets coal.
- **Tag:** Nathaniel: "Tell my father yes."

### 8. The Wedding
**Change:** the child is taken; everyone picks a side. **POV:** Nixon.
- Morning: Alice goes back to the Lost One. "Nine is the one where we get up." Then she goes to the wedding to tell Nathaniel the truth: paid at first, never after.
- **Nixon's point of no return:** he leads the Guard raid on the workshop and takes the Lost One for Eldgard.
- The wedding. Nixon hands Tamara the ring properly. Tamara has cracked Alistair's book and gives Nixon the Hargrave pages.
- Nixon to Ada: "Did you know?" "Forgive them? Love, I forgave them before you were born."
- At the altar Nathaniel learns she's been taken, and says "I will" anyway, because the East is freezing. He leaves the wedding breakfast to go and get her.
- **Tag:** the Lost One in the Works, face to face with her mother's pod. Morgana: "My child."

### 9. The Break-out
**Change:** the truth is spoken aloud. **POV:** Nixon and the Lost One.
- **Opening:** Ada works out the break-out plan and goes to report Nixon. The servants' stair. The lamp smashes.
- Nixon breaks Marsh out of Newgate with her blood on his cuffs. Marsh is his uncle.
- Nathaniel, Alice and Nixon storm the Works.
- **Imprint reveal #2:** Morgana tells the Lost One she did what she was told: both boys, both seeds. The Lost One's crisis: was her love for Alice a plan as well?
- Eldgard's confession. Nixon: "Did you love me, or did you keep me?" Eldgard: "Yes."
- **Tag:** Jennie tells Alice who paid for Miriam: Josephine.

### 10. Lights Out
**Change:** the light ends. **POV:** Nathaniel and Alice.
- Morgana is dying. Eldgard moves to put the Lost One in the pod.
- Alice confronts Josephine and doesn't kill her.
- Nathaniel uses the knowledge planted in 1873 to open the pod. "Us" at the lock: both of them choose it.
- Morgana goes free. Her vengeance: Eldgard. London goes dark.
- The ship. Substantial damage across the city, then retreat. Morgana, too weak to fight, boards it and vows hell on this hellhole.
- In the dark, Lambrick's coal is king: Nathaniel's righteous act has handed London to his nemesis. Tamara opens the depots to the East for nothing.
- Nixon leads the Guard for London, not for the Hardings.
- Alice offers the Lost One the ship. The Lost One looks up at it, then back at Alice, and stays. Her hand finds Alice's in the cold.

## Supporting arcs in one line each
- **Eldgard:** the aura's most comfortable servant. Confesses, then dies at Morgana's hand.
- **Josephine:** fully awake for every terrible thing she does. Paid for Miriam. Spared by Alice. Fate after the finale to be decided.
- **Tamara:** from merger piece to the person who holds the book and the depots.
- **Lambrick:** wants the light dead, then wants to own her. Ends the season winning: London runs on coal.
- **The chief engineer:** Nathaniel's mentor, right about the light, wrong about everything else. Lambrick's door into the secret.
- **Ada:** seeded from Ep 1. Dies Ep 9 still believing.
- **Byron:** tends Jamie in secret. Dies in the freeze the house made.
- **Grise and Jennie:** the knife every faction rents. Jennie killed Miriam and turns in Ep 9.
- **Jamie:** in the coma all season. Never wakes to explain the plot.
