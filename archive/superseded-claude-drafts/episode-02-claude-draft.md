# Episode 2 — The Wake

**Clock:** THE LIGHT HAS 48 DAYS → 47 · 3–4 November 1889

**Cold open:** None. Straight in: the river at dawn.

**Change:** Two grieving siblings get missions.  
**Governing question:** What do you do with grief when somebody hands you a job?  
**POV:** Alice  
**Set piece:** The river gives Miriam back; Jamie's funeral in the rain  
**Heaviest moment:** The Lost One sees the half orange on the pillow and remembers it was saved for her.  
**Tag:** Eldgard takes Nixon to the top of the house: "He's alive. Nathaniel must never know."

## Scenes

### 1. The foreshore

*Act One · The Thames foreshore below Blackwood Bridge. Dawn, 3 November.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Reveal, Setup

Low tide. Mudlarks working the shingle with sacks and sticks: children and old women, barefoot in November, picking coal and copper out of the mud. **PEG**, ten, in a man's waistcoat to her knees, finds a red wool mitten, and then a hand.

Around the woman lying in the shallows, the mud has frozen in a perfect circle six feet across, white as a plate. Every mudlark stops.

She sits up. **THE LOST ONE**, in Miriam's body exactly as the river left it: the dark braid soaked and heavy, the charcoal coat black with water, one red mitten on, lips blue, a knife-wound under her ribs sealed shut with a crust of frost that glitters like sugar. Her eyes are Miriam's brown with something green moving at the back of them, like light under ice. She looks at her own hand as if it were a tool she has never used. She breathes out, and the breath falls as snow.

She knows one thing. A face. She doesn't know what a face is for.

> **PEG:** *(backing away)* You was dead, miss. You was dead, I saw.
>
> **THE LOST ONE:** *(the first word, in Miriam's voice, with no idea what it means)* …Sister?

**Exit state:** Before: Miriam is a corpse in the river. After: something wearing her is walking into London with one face in its head.

**Craft:** Open on wonder and dread, not exposition. Peg is a witness the Guard will find in Episode 3, so the foreshore has a consequence.

### 2. The boarded window

*Act One · Harding House. Morning.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Turn, Setup

The household in black by eight o'clock. Mirrors covered, clocks stopped at a quarter to midnight. Byron has done it all before dawn and looks a hundred years old. Eldgard tells Nathaniel in the hall, standing: a burglar, the study window, the cold. Jamie is with the undertaker already.

Nathaniel goes into the study anyway. The window is boarded; the carpet is soaked where frost has melted, and the room smells of thunder. Under the desk, missed by whoever swept, a shard of glass the colour of a frozen pond. It's warm. It hums against his palm like something alive, and for half a second he sees green light at a window that isn't this one.

He puts it in his waistcoat pocket.

> **NATHANIEL:** I want to see him.
>
> **ELDGARD:** No. You'll remember him whole.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** He was fine. He was *fine*, at nine o'clock he—
>
> **ELDGARD:** Nathaniel. *(his hand on his son's shoulder: the first time in years)* Go and lie down.

**Exit state:** Before: Nathaniel is a grieving brother. After: he is a grieving brother with evidence nobody knows he has.

**Craft:** Give the lead his mission on the morning of the loss, through an object (atlas lesson 2: the hero turns a vast mystery into a thing in his pocket). The father's touch, withheld at lunch yesterday, arrives now as a lie.

### 3. Half an orange

*Act One · The Bounds room, then the Shadwell laundry. Morning.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Turn

Alice wakes alone. The other half of the orange is still on Miriam's pillow, untouched, going dry at the edges. The dawn boat has sailed. She doesn't know there was a boat.

At the laundry the mangles roar. Grise tells her flatly that her sister has run off with a gentleman and forty guineas of the guild's money. Behind Grise, Jennie works the far mangle with a bandaged hand, and doesn't look up.

> **ALICE:** She'd never go without me.
>
> **GRISE:** They all say that about the ones who did, dear.
>
> **ALICE:** *(to Jennie, across the steam)* You were out last night. Your hand.
>
> **JENNIE:** Mangle.
>
> **ALICE:** You've never caught your hand in a mangle in your life.

**Exit state:** Before: Alice expected her sister home. After: she's been told Miriam abandoned her, and she's noticed Jennie's hand.

**Craft:** Plant the clue in plain sight on day one (Weeks): the audience knows what Jennie's hand means; Alice half-knows and files it. It pays in Episode 7.

### 4. What an orange is

*Act Two · London, morning: Blackfriars to Gin Lane.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Setup

The Lost One walks into the city in a wet coat and one red mitten. Everything is too loud. A tram bell, *ding-ding*, makes her flinch like a struck dog. The lamps are the worst: every one she passes hums, and in the hum there's a voice she knows better than her own.

A costermonger holds out an orange to the pretty girl who looks half-drowned. She takes it in both hands like a jewel and holds it to her face to breathe it. She doesn't know what it is. Miriam's body does: her mouth waters. That frightens her more than the trams.

At a corner she puts her palm on a lamppost to steady herself. Frost runs up the iron, *tik-tik-tik-tik*, all the way to the glass, and the glass sings to her: *Child?* She snatches her hand away. A horse rears. She runs.

> **COSTERMONGER:** Go on, love, it's an orange, it won't bite you.
>
> **THE LOST ONE:** *(very serious)* Thank you for the orange.

**Exit state:** Before: the Lost One is a blank. After: we've seen her two pulls: her mother in the lamps, and Miriam's body remembering things she doesn't.

**Craft:** Whimsy earns the grim (Rule 8). The geography test: she walks from the river toward Gin Lane because the only face she has lives there, and the lamps nearly turn her toward Tyburn instead. A move with a reason and a cost.

### 5. Mim

*Act Two · Gin Lane, in the fog. Noon.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Turn, Reveal

Alice is coming home from the laundry with her fists in her pockets when she sees her: at the end of the lane, under the dead lamp, swaying, soaked. The red mitten.

Alice runs. She hits her sister so hard they both nearly go down, and holds on, and keeps holding on. Miriam is cold, wrong-cold, cold like a stone in a river, and Alice holds on harder.

Upstairs, she peels the wet coat off and sees the wound: a knife's width under the ribs, sealed with frost like sugar-glass. Her hands shake. The Lost One looks at the wound with mild curiosity, as if it were on someone else.

> **ALICE:** Mim. *Mim.* Where were you, where were you—
>
> **THE LOST ONE:** *(finding the word in the body, the way you find a step in the dark)* Ali.
>
> **ALICE:** You're freezing. Where have you *been*?
>
> **THE LOST ONE:** The river.
>
> **ALICE:** Who did this to you?
>
> **THE LOST ONE:** *(a fragment surfacing: a face in the rain)* …A scar. Here. *(she touches her own left eyebrow)*

**Exit state:** Before: Alice had lost her sister. After: she has her back, wounded and strange, and a clue that points straight at Jennie's eyebrow.

**Craft:** The reunion gets room. The audience knows it's not Miriam; Alice doesn't. Every warm beat between them now plays as dramatic irony, which is the engine of their arc until Episode 7.

### 6. Closed coffin

*Act Two · Harding House, Josephine's sitting room. Afternoon.* · Thread: Jamie / the locked room · Turn, Setup

Josephine dresses for a funeral she has arranged for a son who is breathing two floors above her. Her maid pins the black veil. When the maid goes, Eldgard comes in and sits on the edge of a chair that was never meant for a man his size.

They talk like generals: the coffin, weighted with ballast; the undertaker, paid; the rector, told the face was too disfigured to view. Then she asks about the thing.

> **JOSEPHINE:** And the thing that came out of that glass?
>
> **ELDGARD:** It'll want its mother. It'll come close. When it does, we take it back.
>
> **JOSEPHINE:** Nathaniel was in the study this morning.
>
> **ELDGARD:** Did he take anything?
>
> **JOSEPHINE:** He's his father's son. He wouldn't tell me if he had.
>
> **ELDGARD:** Then he needs watching.
>
> **JOSEPHINE:** *(pinning the veil, not looking at him)* I'll see to it.

**Exit state:** Before: Josephine was a grieving mother. After: she has taken charge of watching her surviving son, and we know she will use a stranger to do it.

**Craft:** Causality, not convenience: Josephine's decision here is why Alice gets the contract in the next act. Two parents planning a fake funeral in calm voices is protection as control in its coldest form.

### 7. The knot

*Act Two · The Shadwell laundry, the back room. Evening.* · Thread: The Vykra / the contract · Turn

A second letter in plain black wax lies on Grise's table. She doesn't show it to anyone. She sends for Alice.

Grise lays it out like a laundry bill. Miriam stole a purse and a strongbox's worth of trust. Miriam is home, and sick, and sick girls can't work. Grise can collect from Miriam the old way, or Alice can work it off.

The job: a young man of good family who needs watching, discreetly, by a girl he'd look at twice. Nathaniel Harding. Alice has seen his picture in the papers, under the word MOURNING. She thinks about the frost on her sister's ribs, and puts out her hand for the gloves. The knot is already stitched inside them. Grise had them made yesterday.

> **GRISE:** Forty guineas and a purse. Your sister's, or yours.
>
> **ALICE:** You'll leave her alone.
>
> **GRISE:** I'll leave her alone as long as you're useful. *(tenderly, pulling the glove on for her)* There. Like it was made for you.
>
> **ALICE:** It was.
>
> **GRISE:** Every girl thinks she's the one who gets out, dear.

**Exit state:** Before: Alice wanted in to be seen. After: she's in to protect her sister, owned by the woman Miriam tried to keep her from, and pointed at Nathaniel Harding.

**Craft:** Give the want a better reason than the one she started with (guide §2). Joining the Vykra now costs her Miriam's one wish, and she does it for Miriam.

### 8. One post

*Act Three · The Bounds room. Night.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Setup, Turn

Strangeness piling up. The Lost One doesn't eat. When Alice is frightened the kettle freezes on the stove, *crk*, a skin of ice on boiling water. When Alice plays the old game to calm her, *one post*, the Lost One just looks at her, blank, and Alice's heart drops through the floor. Then something arrives behind the green in her sister's eyes and she says *two post*, and Alice laughs too loudly and calls it the fever.

Late, the Lost One sees the dried half-orange on the pillow. She picks it up. And she remembers: not as herself, but as Miriam, a girl who left her sister on a birthday.

> **ALICE:** One post.
>
> **THE LOST ONE:** *(a terrible pause; then)* …two post.
>
> **ALICE:** *(too bright)* See? See. It's the fever.
>
> **THE LOST ONE:** *(holding the dried half-orange)* You saved me half.
>
> **ALICE:** *(breaking)* You said tomorrow.

**Exit state:** Before: Alice could believe Miriam was simply ill. After: she has seen the gaps and chosen not to look at them, and the Lost One has felt her first human grief.

**Craft:** The heaviest moment of the episode is an object, not a speech. Alice's denial is a choice we watch her make, so Episode 7's 'You're not her' becomes a reckoning with her own refusal.

### 9. The second ledger

*Act Three · The Harding Works, the control room. Night.* · Thread: The light / Morgana · Turn

Eldgard reads the yield over Pike's shoulder: down eleven points since midnight before last, and not coming back. Pike asks, carefully, what got out of the house that night. Eldgard tells him nothing got out of the house.

After Eldgard goes, Pike opens a second, plain ledger from his coat and copies the yield in a smaller hand. Then he sits looking at the speaking-pipe for a long time.

> **ELDGARD:** She's grieving. It'll pass.
>
> **PIKE:** Who is, sir?
>
> **ELDGARD:** *(a beat; correcting himself without hurry)* The machine, Silas.
>
> **PIKE:** Machines don't grieve.
>
> **ELDGARD:** Then call it a fault.

**Exit state:** Before: Pike was a frightened man with a secret. After: he's a frightened man keeping a second set of books.

**Craft:** The engineer's turn gets one wordless step per episode (atlas: dormant threads need a visible unfinished condition). Eldgard's slip, 'she', is the aura's comfort cracking for a second.

### 10. The funeral

*Act Three · St Bride's, Fleet Street, and the churchyard. Rain. 4 November.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Turn, Setup

**THE LIGHT HAS 47 DAYS.** London's quality in black under a hundred green lamps; the Lambricks in the third pew; the Guard in dress grey. Nixon is a pallbearer. The coffin is very heavy.

At the graveside Eldgard puts Nixon at his right hand. Nathaniel is at the edge again, between a cousin and the rain. The congregation sings the Lantern Hymn. He can't bear it. He walks out mid-verse.

In the lane by the church a flower-girl in a too-big coat is lighting a tallow stub under a dead lamp, her knuckles split and bleeding. Alice, in costume, on the job. Nathaniel doesn't know that. He gives her his handkerchief without thinking: the only thing he does all day that nobody told him to.

> **ALICE:** You'll want that back.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** Keep it.
>
> **ALICE:** You're him. The brother.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** Was it the hymn? You came out too.
>
> **ALICE:** I'm selling flowers.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** *(looking at her empty tray)* Not very well.
>
> *(He goes back in. She looks at the handkerchief: a monogram, N.H. She puts it inside the glove, against the knot.)*

**Exit state:** Before: Alice had a target. After: she has his handkerchief inside her Vykra glove, and he's the only person all day who didn't want anything from her.

**Craft:** The meet-cute is a lie for one of them and a kindness from the other; both are true. The handkerchief against the knot is the romance and the contract in one image.

### 11. A house loses its nerve

*Act Three · The Lambrick carriage, leaving St Bride's.* · Thread: Lambrick / coal · Setup

Lambrick, Tamara and, on the jump seat, **AJAX FINK**, his face stitched cheekbone to jaw in black thread like a boot, still smiling. The carriage stinks of wet wool and cigar.

> **LAMBRICK:** A house that loses its heir loses its nerve.
>
> **TAMARA:** Or finds a better one.
>
> **LAMBRICK:** *(to Ajax)* The girl who did your face. Find her. And find out what Harding's chief engineer drinks.
>
> **AJAX:** Whisky and guilt, by the look of him.
>
> **LAMBRICK:** Then buy him both.

**Exit state:** Before: the Lambricks were guests. After: they've chosen their two targets: the girl who cut Ajax and the man who reads the yield.

**Craft:** Give the antagonist an unmet need (atlas lesson 2): Lambrick can't buy what's inside the Works with coal. He needs a person who can still refuse. Tamara's line marks her as the cleverest person in the carriage.

### 12. The script

*Act Four · Nathaniel's workshop, Clerkenwell. Night.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Reveal, Setup

His own place, rented with money he won't explain: a converted bell-foundry with a crane hook in the roof, benches drowning in brass, a cot he never sleeps on, and a window full of the green city. Establish it well: the season will live here.

Under his loupe the shard has no grain at all, like no glass ever poured in London. Etched into one face, finer than a hair, a line of script in no alphabet he knows. He stares at it. And reads one character of it, aloud, before he can stop himself. He doesn't know how. The lamp on his bench dips.

> **NATHANIEL:** *(alone, frightened)* …*Hold.*

**Exit state:** Before: the shard was evidence. After: it's evidence that is somehow talking to him.

**Craft:** Imprint flicker, second dose. Lore changes behaviour (atlas lesson 9): from now on he won't show the shard to anyone.

### 13. With the light

*Act Four · Harding House, the housekeeper's pantry. Late.* · Thread: Nixon & the kept Hargraves · Setup

Ada's pantry is a chapel of brass: lamps on every shelf, glass chimneys drying on a rack like a choir of bells, the smell of rottenstone and spirit. She's pressing Nixon's dress uniform, still damp from the churchyard. Before she lights the pantry lamp she touches her lips to its glass. Nixon has seen her do it his whole life and never asked.

> **NIXON:** Do you think he suffered?
>
> **ADA:** I think he's with the light, love. They all go to the light in this house.
>
> **NIXON:** You say that like it's a place.
>
> **ADA:** *(smoothing his collar)* You were born for this house. Never forget it.

**Exit state:** Before: Nixon is grieving. After: he has been told, again, by the woman who raised him, that he belongs to the house.

**Craft:** Ada's faith gets one line per episode until it can break the audience's heart. The irony is total: Jamie is with the light, frozen upstairs.

### 14. The top of the house

*Tag · Harding House, the study, then the top-floor stair.* · Thread: Jamie / the locked room · Reveal, Turn

The boarded study. Eldgard gives Nixon a ring of keys: the house, the cellars, the Works gates. Lantern-Captain of the household. Everything Nathaniel has wanted his whole life, handed across a desk to someone else, on the day of his brother's funeral.

Then Eldgard takes a lamp and walks him up. Past the family floors, past the servants' floor, up a stair Nixon has never been allowed on. A door. Byron inside on a hard chair with a tallow candle, the same white gloves. In the bed, Jamie: frost veining his throat like marble, his breath a thread of white.

> **ELDGARD:** My family looks after its own.
>
> **NIXON:** *(staring)* We buried him this morning. I *carried* him.
>
> **ELDGARD:** You carried sand. He's alive. Nathaniel must never know.
>
> **NIXON:** Sir, he's— Nat would want—
>
> **ELDGARD:** That's why.

**Exit state:** Before: Nixon was Nathaniel's best friend. After: he is the keeper of the lie that would destroy that friendship.

**Craft:** The tag changes the board: the season's central friendship now has a bomb in it, and the audience is holding the fuse. Dread for three episodes, not a surprise in one.
