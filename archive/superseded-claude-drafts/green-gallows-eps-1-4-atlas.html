01 / The architecture

Rupture. Grief. Shelter. Exposure.

The Rings of Power block runs establish → commit → redirect → collide. Ours runs the same muscle at our pace: the secret breaks out, two families grieve and take on missions, Nathaniel takes a side, and the failing light goes public. Every thread is aimed at one workshop in Clerkenwell when Episode 5 begins.

06 / Dread or surprise

Label every secret.

If the audience knows and the character doesn't, the scene runs on dread. If nobody knows, it's built for surprise. If everyone knows and nobody acts, it's a fuse. Weeks's rule: the reader usually knows more, and that's what keeps the pages turning.

08 / The collision

Every arrow points at the workshop.

The atlas's planning pass starts at the end of Episode 4 and works backward. Our collision isn't a battle; it's six people who each want something that runs through one rented bell-foundry in Clerkenwell. Each brings a different kind of pressure into Episode 5.

Lambrick

knows where the creature is and that Nathaniel hides her: leverage on both houses.

Nixon

has frost on his fingers and an order to find out.

Josephine

wants the shard, through a girl who has started lying to her.

Morgana

wants 'the boy who knows', through the girl he shelters.

Eldgard

wants the child for the pod, and the traitor in the Works.

Byron

has heard the plan, and he loves Nathaniel.

09 / What we borrowed

The mechanisms, not a scene quota.

From the Rings of Power room, as reported by Payne and McKay and read in the atlas, and from Brent Weeks's long-fuse secrets. Each card says where it shows up in our four episodes.

Rings of Power room

A governing question per episode

Each episode above opens on one question. Threads that don't complicate it are held, with a one-line condition so they stay alive (see Jamie in Eps 3–4).

Rings of Power room

Give the threat an unmet need

Eldgard can't summon the child; Lambrick can't buy the core; Morgana needs a boy who knows. Each need runs through someone who can still refuse.

Rings of Power room

Design scenes by exit state

Every scene card has a before / after. Scenes whose before and after matched were cut (Felix's Narrows inspection, the Blackwood generator, the face-veil prep).

Rings of Power room

Travel tests relationships

The Ring mains escape: the route forces trust, and the engineer's impossible knowledge is the cost.

Rings of Power room

Dread over surprise

Josephine's veil, Pike asked to hunt himself, Jamie upstairs: the audience holds the fuse for weeks.

Rings of Power room

Institutions do dramatic work

The Charter hearing puts the Works under Crown inquiry. Nixon's 'not Newgate' order is illegal. Both constrain the next move.

Rings of Power room

Track the price of compression

Episode 2 is grief before plot. No new reveal lands on the same beat as the funeral.

Rings of Power room

No convenient triggers

The driver's fare book, Ada's runner, Ajax's man on the stair, Byron overhearing: every collision has a visible path.

Brent Weeks

Secrets with long fuses

Black wax (Ep 1) → Ep 9. The painted-out child (Ep 1) → Ep 8. 'Bring me the boy who knows' (Ep 4) → Ep 9.

Brent Weeks

Every POV holds a secret

Nathaniel's hands, Alice's contract, Nixon's keys, the Lost One's mission, Pike's ledger, Ada's faith.

Brent Weeks

Reveals that re-colour

'I know you' turns every reflex of Nathaniel's in Ep 3 into memory. Ada's 'I know who it belongs to' plays differently on rewatch.

Brent Weeks

Keep them turning pages

Every tag changes the board: Pike hears the light, Nixon sees Jamie, 'I know you', the plan for the child.

10 / The pilot, revised

What changed in Episode 1.

Kept from the version you liked: the Narrows heist and takedown, Alice cutting Ajax, the laundry, the photograph's count of eight, the birthday orange and 'Not tonight', the cravat, the three berths, the Exhibition and 'Toys after supper', Josephine and Byron's one line. Changed or added:

  • New cold open: the crash in the Harding woods, c. 1780, with a Hargrave boy crossing himself (plants the massacre).
  • New: the Harding luncheon. Nixon takes Nathaniel's chair; the engagement is dropped over grouse; Jamie floats treason and the table stays calm (the aura); the chandelier dips; Ada's 'love'.
  • New: the initiation. The audience sees the pod and Morgana in Ep 1, and Jamie's immunity to the aura.
  • New: the gauntlet shop with Pike. Nathaniel's genius is visibly strange (imprint seed), and the chief engineer is introduced.
  • The photograph now puts Nixon in the frame and Nathaniel at the edge; Alistair's portrait has a painted-out child.
  • Cut: Felix, the Blackwood generator and both keys, the lockets, Xylon, the face-veil, Cole, the 1489 cold open, Jennifer.
  • The coach scene now tells Miriam about 'a child in a box', so the audience knows what Jamie will free.
  • New: Ada on the servants' stair sees Jamie come home.
  • The climax is now the ornament breaking (intimate horror), the city dimming, a Vykra knife duel on the bridge, and 'Go back for my sister' underwater.
  • New ending: 'As of tonight he's dead': the coma lie decided on screen. Tag: Pike hears the light speak.
