# Episode 1 — The Exhibition

**Clock:** THE LIGHT HAS 49 DAYS · 2 November 1889

**Cold open:** c. 1780. The crash in the Harding woods.

**Change:** The secret gets out.  
**Governing question:** What does it cost the Hardings to keep one night perfect?  
**POV:** Nathaniel and Jamie, with the Bounds sisters below  
**Set piece:** The Narrows takedown; the ornament breaking; the bridge  
**Heaviest moment:** Miriam, drowning: "Go back for my sister."  
**Tag:** Alone in the Works control room, the chief engineer hears the light speak: "My child is out."

## Scenes

### 1. The burning furrow

*Cold open · The Harding woods, c. 1780. Night, hard frost.* · Thread: The light / Morgana · Setup, Reveal

A furrow a quarter-mile long, ploughed through the beeches and still steaming. Trees snapped like kindling, sap boiling out of them, *tssss*. At the end of it, half-buried in black earth, something that is not a ship anyone has a word for: a hull the colour of a frozen pond, ribbed like a ribcage, humming one low note that makes the teeth ache.

ALISTAIR HARDING, about thirty, comes down the furrow with a lantern. Threadbare scholar's coat, patched at both elbows; a nobleman's shirt gone grey with washing; a satchel of brass instruments he never paid for, *clink-clink* at his hip. Ink on his fingers, mud to the knee, and the starved, bright face of a man who has failed at everything he has ever built. Behind him, holding torches and keeping well back, three HARGRAVE woodcutters from the village at the edge of the estate. One of them, a boy of twelve, crosses himself.

The lantern finds a hand in the wreck. Long-fingered, pale blue, frost feathering the nails. It moves. And a voice speaks out of the hull, not in any tongue at all but somehow in perfect, gentle English, inside his head:

**VOICE:** *Help me.*

Alistair looks at the hand. Then past it, deeper in the hull, where something small is glowing like a coal in snow. He doesn't take the hand. He lifts the lantern higher, to see better what he's found.

**MATCH CUT TO:** the same lantern-flame, green now, in a Mayfair street lamp, 1889. Every lamp on the street burns. The camera drops through an iron grating, past the Ring mains glowing in their pipes, and up through a second grating onto **Gin Lane**, where every third lamp is dark and women sell tallow from trays chalked **FOR WHEN IT GOES**.

**TITLE CARD: THE LIGHT HAS 49 DAYS.**

**Exit state:** Before: the audience assumes a Victorian period drama. After: they know the light began with a creature asking for help, and a man choosing not to give it.

**Craft:** Weeks's three-layer secret starts here: the public believes iron, the audience now knows *something*, and no character on screen will learn the whole of it for weeks. The Hargrave boy crossing himself is a plant for Episode 8.

### 2. The Narrows

*Act One · A riverside warehouse with no sign, Wapping. Noon, but rain has made it dusk.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Setup

Rain hammers the skylight, *tak-tak-tak-tak*. Seven green lamps light a smugglers' floor. Crates stamped with the Harding lantern crest, the Works stamp chiselled off every one; one open in straw, its crystal throwing cold green up into the faces around it: **core-cut gallowglass**, the kind the Works never sells.

AJAX FINK, 35, sits on a crate eating an apple, *crnch*. Good teeth, a bad smile, a camel coat too fine for the room. His right arm is cased to the elbow in black iron, all pistons and rivets, and it hisses when he flexes it, *hss*. Across from him MR DE GRAAF, an Antwerp smuggler in sealskin, holds a jeweller's loupe to a crystal.

On the skylight, rain sheets off a shape lying flat on the glass. **MIRIAM BOUNDS**, 24: tall and long-limbed, dark hair braided tight under a wool cap, a charcoal coat with Vykra red stitched inside the collar, soft boots worn thin at the toe, a knife flat against her spine, red wool mittens tucked in her belt for later. The calm face of a woman who has already counted the exits. Her lips move: *One. Two. Three…* Seven men. Seven lamps.

Two roofs back, a second, smaller shadow crouches in the rain. Miriam doesn't see it.

> **DE GRAAF:** Last crate went grey by Rotterdam. Dull as river stones. My buyer says the light *dies* once it's far from London.
>
> **AJAX:** Then don't dawdle. Seventy.
>
> **DE GRAAF:** Fifty. And I don't ask why your glass gets homesick.
>
> **AJAX:** *(cheerful)* Everybody in London's selling Harding light, Mr de Graaf. I'm just the only one honest about the price.

**Exit state:** Before: the glass is a commodity. After: we've heard the first clue that it is bound to something in London, and we know a Lambrick man is selling stolen Harding light.

**Craft:** Worldbuilding with a job (atlas lesson 6): the 'homesick glass' line is a rule disguised as a haggle. It changes what Lambrick will want once he understands it.

### 3. The takedown

*Act One · The Narrows.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Turn

Almost no dialogue. Speed, shadow and light. Miriam snaps a vial of Vykra **snap-glass** and drops it through a broken pane, *KRAK*: green-white light, seven men blind for one second, and in that second she drops.

Every lamp she kills changes the frame. In the green we see her; in the black we only hear her. *Thump. Shhk. BLAM*, a blind shot, the muzzle flash catching her one frame, already elsewhere. She counts under her breath, the old count: *"Four post. Five post."* A guard's lantern rifle hits the open crate of core-cut glass and it bursts, **KA-WHOOOMPH**, a column of green fire, shadows the height of houses.

One lamp left, and Ajax under it. *Hssssss.* Her knife skids off the iron, *KLANG, KLANG.* The iron hand closes on her throat and slams her into the wall safe; her boots leave the floor.

Behind him the second shadow drops without a sound. **ALICE BOUNDS**, 20: small and wiry, hair chopped short with a knife, a borrowed coat two sizes too big, knuckles wrapped in rag, wolf's eyes. She doesn't count or plan. Her blade opens Ajax from cheekbone to jaw, *shhk*. He goes through the river window, *KSHHH*, and a splash.

Miriam empties the safe: Grise's strongbox into the satchel, a purse stamped with the **Lambrick** crest into her coat. Alice sees which pocket. We see her see it.

> **AJAX:** *(blood through his fingers)* I'll remember you.
>
> **MIRIAM:** *(hoarse)* What are you doing here?
>
> **ALICE:** Helping. He nearly *had* you, Mim.
>
> **MIRIAM:** I had it under control.
>
> **ALICE:** Your feet weren't touching the floor.
>
> **ALICE:** Who goes first?
>
> **MIRIAM:** Me. Always me.

**Exit state:** Before: Alice is the kid sister who isn't allowed in. After: she has saved Miriam's life, marked Ajax, and seen Miriam steal from her own guild.

**Craft:** Action in phases that change the problem (guide §8): darkness is Miriam's weapon until the burst crate takes it away, and then the fight is lost until Alice arrives. 'Who goes first? Me. Always me.' is the sisters' whole wound in five words, and it lands in Episode 7.

### 4. The gauntlet shop

*Act One · Harding Works, the gauntlet shop, high above the vats. Afternoon.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Setup

The shop's windows look down a hundred feet onto the core housings: brass domes the size of chapels, humming, cast with the Works motto **PROVIDENCE AND IRON. EST. 1783.** A chalkboard lists districts against dark hours: *Barking 3. Ilford 2. Essex (whole) 4.* Somebody has underlined Essex twice.

**NATHANIEL HARDING**, 21: lanky, ink-stained to the wrist, sleeves rolled, brass goggles pushed up into hair that needs cutting, a waistcoat buttoned wrong. A face that hasn't learned yet to hide how much it wants things. He fires his leap-gauntlet prototype, *kshhhh-THOOM*, crosses the shop in one jump and lands in the coal bin.

BYRON, 64, the Harding butler, picks soot out of his hair. Silver-haired and straight-backed, an old soldier's moustache, white gloves somehow still white.

The chief engineer **SILAS PIKE**, 50s, comes up the iron stair with a ledger under his arm. Spare and grey, pince-nez on a ribbon, a leather apron over a good suit worn shiny at the cuffs, the burn-scarred hands of a man who came up from the furnace floor. He looks at the gauntlet's coupling, frowns, then looks closer. Nathaniel has solved a heat-bleed problem the Works has failed at for ten years, with a spiral nobody taught him.

> **PIKE:** Where did you learn to wind it like that?
>
> **NATHANIEL:** I didn't. It just… looked right.
>
> **PIKE:** *(a long beat, closing his ledger)* Then don't show your father. He'll want to know where it came from too.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** Tonight he sees it. In front of everyone. He'll *have* to look.
>
> **BYRON:** Then I'll brush you twice. *(a beat)* And, sir, if he doesn't look—
>
> **NATHANIEL:** He'll look.

**Exit state:** Before: Nathaniel is a talented second son. After: we've seen his genius is strange even to the Works' best engineer, and that Pike is hiding something in his ledger.

**Craft:** Imprint #1 is planted as character, not lore: the audience reads it as talent until Episode 6. Byron lights himself down the servants' stair with a tallow candle, not a green lamp, and nobody asks why.

### 5. The luncheon

*Act One · Harding House, the dining room, Mayfair. One o'clock.* · Thread: Nixon & the kept Hargraves · Turn, Setup

A long table under a green chandelier, every crystal polished to a hard shine. The Hardings eat lunch the way other families take communion. Grouse, claret, a newspaper folded face-down by Eldgard's plate: **THE END OF THE GREEN? ESSEX DARK FOURTH NIGHT.**

**ELDGARD HARDING**, 58: broad and grey and very still, a perfectly cut black coat, a wolf-crest signet that was his grandfather's. He doesn't raise his voice because he has never needed to. **JOSEPHINE HARDING**, 50: black silk the colour of a closed piano, grey-streaked hair coiled tight as rope, a jet brooch she has never explained, kid gloves at table. She moves family the way she moves furniture, with two fingers. **JAMIE HARDING**, 25: tall and immaculate, cravat already crooked from pulling at it, grey eyes that haven't slept in three days. He has barely touched his plate.

The doors open. **NIXON HARGRAVE**, 23, Lantern-Captain, home from the eastern marshes and still in his greatcoat: broad through the shoulders, close-cropped dark hair, a weather-cut face older than his years, a fresh scar through one eyebrow, mud dried grey on his boots and a soldier's careful way of standing in rooms too good for him. Eldgard rises, actually rises, and embraces him. Then he pulls out a chair at his own right hand. Nathaniel's chair. Nathaniel arrives late from the Works, soot on his cuff, and finds it taken.

Behind them all, **ADA HARGRAVE**, head housekeeper, about sixty: small and upright in black bombazine, a chatelaine of keys at her waist that chimes when she moves, iron-grey hair under a lace cap, hands red-knuckled from forty years of brass polish, and the serene face of a woman in church. She serves Nixon first and touches his shoulder as she passes. Nobody else at the table gets touched.

Mid-course, the chandelier dips. Every crystal gutters at once, a long breath of half-dark, *whmmmm*. Everyone freezes except Eldgard, who keeps cutting his grouse. The light comes back. Ada is already at the sconce, steady-handed.

> **ELDGARD:** Nixon held the Essex line nine nights with tallow and rifles. Nine. While the papers wrote our obituary.
>
> **JOSEPHINE:** *(to Nathaniel, mildly)* You're in Nixon's place, darling. Sit by Jamie.
>
> **JAMIE:** He's in Nathaniel's place, Mother. It's a different sentence.
>
> **JOSEPHINE:** Lord Lambrick brings Tamara tonight. Nathaniel, you'll take her in to supper.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** Why me?
>
> **JOSEPHINE:** Because you're engaged to her.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** Since when?
>
> **ELDGARD:** Since Tuesday.
>
> **JAMIE:** Congratulations, Nat. You've been sold. *(raising his glass)* Coal and light.
>
> **JAMIE:** *(lightly, to Nixon)* What would happen, Nix, if it just went out? All of it. One night.
>
> **NIXON:** *(the soldier's answer)* People would freeze, sir. I've seen it. Children first.
>
> **ELDGARD:** *(still cutting)* Eat your soup, James.

**Exit state:** Before: Nathaniel came to lunch to tell his father about the gauntlet. After: he has lost his chair to Nixon, learned he's engaged, and watched his brother float treason over grouse, and nobody but Nathaniel noticed how calm the table stayed.

**Craft:** Power inside intimate relationships, shown through seating, touch and who keeps eating. The calm after Jamie's 'what if it went out' is the aura: nobody reacts as they should, and nobody notices that they don't. It plants Nixon as the replacement son, Ada's 'love' economy, and the failing light in a single scene.

### 6. The study

*Act One · Harding House, the study. After lunch.* · Thread: The light / Morgana · Setup

Eldgard's study is the house's memory: a brass model of the Ring under a glass dome, its green veins all running to one point at Tyburn; a portrait of Alistair over the fire; a telegram on the blotter, *YIELD 61. CROWN ENQUIRES.* And on the desk, among paperweights, the **ornament**: a palm-sized fist of glass the colour of a frozen pond, caged in brass, warm when everything else in the room is cold. It hums. If you put your ear to it, it hums back.

Jamie stands at the desk. His father pours two drinks.

> **ELDGARD:** Now you know what we are. Tonight you smile for them anyway.
>
> **JAMIE:** You keep it on your desk.
>
> **ELDGARD:** Where I can see it.
>
> **JAMIE:** *(looking at the ornament, not at him)* Does it know where it is?
>
> **ELDGARD:** Drink with me, James.

**Exit state:** Before: the ornament is a paperweight. After: the audience knows it is the object in the room that matters, and that Jamie has been changed by something three nights ago.

**Craft:** Dread over surprise (atlas lesson 5): we now watch every scene in this house knowing what is on the desk. Jamie leaves his drink untouched, and his pocket watch, wound and ticking, beside the telegram.

### 7. The initiation

*Act One · The Harding Works, the core. Three nights earlier: Jamie's 25th birthday.* · Thread: The light / Morgana · Reveal, Turn

A stair cut into the old gallows-field clay, lamplit green, going down further than a stair should go. Iron doors set with plates of pond-coloured glass that match the ornament. Eldgard walks ahead with a lantern. Jamie follows in evening dress, his birthday cravat still perfect.

The vault. In the middle of it, a pod: a long shell of frozen glass, ribbed like a ribcage, braided with Works cable as thick as a man's arm, every cable running up into the dark to feed London. Inside, frost-blurred, a figure much taller than a person, long-limbed, pale blue, a face we only half see. **MORGANA.** Her eyes are closed.

Eldgard puts his hand on Jamie's shoulder, the painted father's hand from every portrait.

> **ELDGARD:** Do you feel that? The quiet? That's the house. It means you're home.
>
> *(Jamie feels nothing at all. He looks at his father's relieved face, and lies.)*
>
> **JAMIE:** Yes.
>
> *(Morgana's eyes open. Her voice is in his head, his own language, as gentle as a nurse.)*
>
> **MORGANA:** *Where is my child?*
>
> **ELDGARD:** She talks. Don't answer her. Not ever.

**Exit state:** Before: Jamie was raised to inherit the light. After: he knows the light is a mother in a freezer, and that her child is somewhere in his father's keeping.

**Craft:** The reveal changes action immediately (atlas lesson 9): Jamie's whole night tonight is caused by this question. The aura is dramatised as a feeling everyone else has and Jamie doesn't, a rule we can see working without a single line of exposition.

### 8. The laundry

*Act One · The Shadwell laundry. Late afternoon.* · Thread: The Vykra / the contract · Setup

Steam pours from the vents. Forty women at the mangles; the room smells of lye and hot iron. **MOTHER GRISE**, 70: tiny and immaculate in dove-grey, a lace collar pinned with a mourning brooch, white hair in a coronet, little round spectacles, and hands softened by sixty years of other people's laundry. She looks like everyone's grandmother. She counts gold like a priest counts beads.

She checks the strongbox against an inventory. The Lambrick purse is missing. Miriam says it burned. Grise lets the lie sit. In the yard a laundress slaps a wet strap onto a trestle and a buckle rings, *tink*, and Alice is across the yard before she knows she's moved. Miriam looks away.

Then, after the sisters go: **JENNIE**, 19, at the far mangle. Lean, still, mousy hair pinned flat, a scar through the left eyebrow, sleeves rolled on forearms corded from the mangle wheel. She never blinks first. A boy delivers a letter to Grise, sealed in plain black wax, no crest. Grise reads it, and smiles at nothing.

> **GRISE:** Ajax Fink, across the face. Nobody's touched that man in years. You have talent, girl. The Vykra would receive you.
>
> **MIRIAM:** She's not interested.
>
> **GRISE:** I asked her.
>
> **MIRIAM:** And I'm answering. Keep your hands off her.
>
> **GRISE:** Every girl thinks she's the one who gets out, dear. It's what makes them good.
>
> **GRISE:** *(later, to Jennie, folding the black-wax letter)* A good family wants its son kept home. Follow Miriam. See where she spends my purse.

**Exit state:** Before: Grise suspects. After: a nameless family has paid to 'keep a son home', Jennie is on Miriam, and Grise has her eye on Alice.

**Craft:** The anonymous client is a long fuse (Weeks): the audience will forget the black wax until Episode 9, when Jennie names Josephine and the scene re-reads itself. Twins merged into Jennie: one knife, one face, one guilt.

### 9. The photograph

*Act Two · Harding House, the long gallery. Dusk.* · Thread: Nixon & the kept Hargraves · Setup

Three generations of Hardings in oil, and every portrait is the same picture: the eldest son holds up a brass lantern, his father's hand on his shoulder, younger sons at the edges, half out of frame. Alistair's portrait hangs first. Look closely at the dark varnish beside his knee and there is the ghost of a painted-out child, a small figure overpainted brown. Ada dusts that frame herself and nobody else is allowed to.

A PHOTOGRAPHER fusses under a black cloth, his flash-tray heaped with powdered gallowglass. Josephine arranges: Jamie at his father's left with the lantern, Nathaniel behind. Then Eldgard says, *"And Nixon."* Nixon, uncomfortable in borrowed evening dress, is put at Eldgard's right. Nathaniel is moved to the very edge.

*Fssshhhhh.* The powder catches green. **A count of eight.** *One.* Nobody blinks. *Three.* Josephine's glove tightens on Jamie's sleeve. *Four.* Somewhere in the bones of the house Nathaniel hears a woman crying. *Five.* His eyes move; nobody else's do. *Six.* The lantern trembles in Jamie's hand and he holds it higher. *Seven.* The crying is under the floor now. *Eight.* It stops.

> **JOSEPHINE:** You're taller, Nathaniel. It's composition, not a verdict.
>
> **JAMIE:** It's always a little bit of a verdict.
>
> **ELDGARD:** And Nixon.
>
> **PHOTOGRAPHER:** *(emerging pink)* Lovely. Very natural.

**Exit state:** Before: Nathaniel was behind his brother. After: he's at the edge of the frame, behind his father's ward too, and he's the only one who heard the crying.

**Craft:** A family portrait as a power map. The count of eight plants the counting motif on the Harding side before we ever see the Bounds sisters count. The painted-out child pays off in Episode 8.

### 10. Tomorrow

*Act Two · The Bounds room, Gin Lane. Evening.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Setup, Turn

One room above a chandler's: a cot they share, a cracked mirror, a kettle ticking on a stove that eats coal they can't afford. Through the window children skip under the lane's one working lamp: *Green light, green light, who's in the cellar tonight?*

Miriam does her hair, takes it down, does it again. Alice binds her split knuckle.

Then Alice asks if she knows what today is. Miriam stops. She digs in her coat for an orange stolen off a cart and tosses it over. Alice sets it down without peeling it. Miriam leaves her hair unfinished and sits beside her, a hand over the bandage.

> **MIRIAM:** Whatever Grise offers you, say no.
>
> **ALICE:** She said I had talent.
>
> **MIRIAM:** She says that when she's found a use for someone.
>
> **ALICE:** Third time with the hair. Who is he? Some toff?
>
> **MIRIAM:** *(a smile she can't stop)* …Oh. Ali. Happy birthday. Tomorrow. You choose where.
>
> **ALICE:** Somewhere you can't get called away from.
>
> **ALICE:** You didn't have it under control.
>
> **MIRIAM:** No.
>
> **MIRIAM:** *(at the door, seeing Alice reach for her coat)* Please, Ali. Not tonight.
>
> **ALICE:** Tomorrow.
>
> **MIRIAM:** Tomorrow.

**Exit state:** Before: Alice was going to follow. After: Miriam has admitted, for the first time, that she needed her, and asked her to stay; Alice gives her that.

**Craft:** The whimsy beat (Rule 8) carries the whole tragedy: a birthday orange, a promise of tomorrow. Alice's choice not to follow is earned by trust, so it can't read as convenience (atlas lesson 8).

### 11. The cravat

*Act Two · Harding House, Nathaniel's dressing room.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Setup

Nathaniel at the mirror, losing a fight with a cravat. The gauntlet schematic folded on the dresser like a love letter; his sketches of the Ring pinned to the wall, every line running to Tyburn, a question mark where they meet. Jamie comes in, takes the cravat out of his hands and ties it for him, the way he has since Nathaniel was six.

Jamie's eyes go to the question mark. Whatever he came to say, he doesn't say it. He pulls his brother into a hug that goes on a beat too long.

> **JAMIE:** You're showing him tonight. He won't ask. Show him anyway, in front of everyone. He can't say no in front of everyone.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** You'd make a terrible Harding.
>
> **JAMIE:** I'm the only one who's had the training.
>
> **JAMIE:** Nat. If you could leave, would you?
>
> **NATHANIEL:** Leave what? The work's here.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** What's that for?
>
> **JAMIE:** Your cravat was crooked.
>
> **JAMIE:** *(at the door, not turning)* The work isn't worth you. Remember I said it.

**Exit state:** Before: the brothers are allies in the old game of getting Father's attention. After: Jamie has said goodbye without saying it; Nathaniel thinks it was a joke.

**Craft:** It's the last thing his brother says to him awake. That line changes meaning three times: a joke now, a mystery at the funeral, an accusation in Episode 5.

### 12. Three berths

*Act Two · The steamship office, Wapping. Night.* · Thread: The Vykra / the contract · Setup

A clerk stamps three Southampton tickets for the dawn sailing. Miriam pays with coins from the Lambrick purse and checks every date twice. Three. She is buying Alice's berth as well as her own.

Across the street, under a dead lamp, Jennie watches the purchase through the window. When Miriam climbs into a waiting hired carriage, Jennie crosses to speak to the driver about nothing in particular, and leaves a coin in his hand.

> **CLERK:** Three, miss? Party of three?
>
> **MIRIAM:** Three.

**Exit state:** Before: Miriam's plan was private. After: the Vykra know she's leaving, and the driver has been bought.

**Craft:** Information has a path (guide §6 skeptical read): Jennie learns the rendezvous by a visible, simple purchase, so no one knows anything the scene never gave them.

### 13. A child in a box

*Act Two · A hired coach at the Blackwood Bridge stair. Rain.* · Thread: The light / Morgana · Reveal, Setup

Rain drums on the roof. Jamie climbs in and kisses Miriam, and for a moment neither of them is anything but young. She lays the three tickets in his hand. He sees the third and doesn't need it explained.

Then he tells her what he's doing tonight. The family will all be at the Works for the Exhibition. The house will be empty. On his father's desk there is a thing made of glass, and inside it something has been kept alive, in the dark, for sixteen years.

> **JAMIE:** You bought hers.
>
> **MIRIAM:** I'm not leaving without her.
>
> **JAMIE:** I know.
>
> **MIRIAM:** What's in the box?
>
> **JAMIE:** A child. Of a sort. They've kept it in a box since I was nine.
>
> **MIRIAM:** Who keeps a child in a box?
>
> **JAMIE:** My father.
>
> **MIRIAM:** And you'll let it out. And then?
>
> **JAMIE:** The bridge. Midnight. I'll come in this carriage myself.
>
> **MIRIAM:** Don't send somebody for me.
>
> **JAMIE:** I'll be in it. Then we fetch Ali.
>
> **JAMIE:** *(finding Nathaniel's gauntlet note in his own pocket, almost laughing)* Nat'll fly before he learns to walk. He's the only one of us worth saving.

**Exit state:** Before: the audience knew about a mother. After: they know there's a child, that Jamie means to free it tonight, and that the driver's listening hatch is open behind them.

**Craft:** Jamie's plan is half heroism, half cowardice: he'll free the child and leave the mother. That flaw is what makes him a character instead of a saint. His kindness about Nat is the memory the Lost One will carry out of him.

### 14. The fare book

*Act Two · A lane off Blackwood Bridge.* · Thread: The Vykra / the contract · Turn

At the next corner the driver hands Jennie a scrap torn from his fare book: **BLACKWOOD. UPPER STAIR. MIDNIGHT.** He didn't understand a word about boxes. He understood the time.

> **DRIVER:** The Harding boy. She wants him in the carriage, not a messenger.
>
> **JENNIE:** *(paying him the rest)* Then send nobody.

**Exit state:** Before: the Vykra knew Miriam was leaving. After: they know exactly where and when.

**Craft:** A ten-second scene that removes coincidence from the climax.

### 15. The Exhibition

*Act Three · The Harding Works, the great hall. Night.* · Thread: Lambrick / coal · Setup

Two hundred guests under a thousand green chandeliers in the hall the Works builds its engines in, swept and hung with silk for one night. Glass cases line the walls, every one telling the story the family wants told: **ALISTAIR HARDING'S FIRST LAMP, 1783**; a scale model of the Ring, every green vein meeting at Tyburn; a framed share certificate from the year the Hardings bought the gallows field. A broadsheet passes hand to hand: **ESSEX DARK AGAIN. HARDING SAYS "A FAULT."**

**LORD HECTOR LAMBRICK**, 55, arrives: heavy and pink, soot in the creases of his knuckles no valet has ever got out, a diamond pin like a wet eye, the friendliest smile in London. With him **TAMARA LAMBRICK**, 19: sharp-chinned and quick-eyed, in grey silk two seasons out of fashion on purpose, ink on her middle finger, a cipher wheel hidden inside her fan.

Across the hall, Pike, in his good suit, catches Lambrick's eye for exactly one second. Lambrick doesn't nod. Then a footman bends to Lambrick's ear: *the Narrows. Fink's been cut. By a girl.* Lambrick's smile stays where it is. His hand closes on his glass.

> **TAMARA:** I'm told we're to be very polite to each other.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** For how long?
>
> **TAMARA:** Forty years, I think.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** I can manage polite.
>
> **TAMARA:** *(reading his soot-blackened cuff)* Can you manage clean?
>
> **LAMBRICK:** *(to Eldgard, tapping the case over Alistair's lamp)* My grandfather kept a lawn at Lambrick, the greenest in the county. He used to say grass only stays green while somebody keeps paying to water it. It won't stay green for ever, Eldgard. Nothing does.

**Exit state:** Before: Lambrick was a rival. After: we've seen he has a man inside the Works and has just lost a crate of stolen light to a girl with a knife.

**Craft:** Every faction in one room, each with a different objective (guide §7): Lambrick wants proof, Pike wants out, Tamara wants to be seen, Eldgard wants one perfect night. The lawn line is the season's theme in a coal baron's mouth.

### 16. Before they sail

*Act Three · The Shadwell laundry, a back room.* · Thread: The Vykra / the contract · Turn

Jennie lays the fare-book scrap on the table, and her report: three tickets, the dawn boat. Grise looks at the tickets for a long time. A Vykra girl running off with a Harding heir and the guild's money is leverage walking out of the door.

She sets down a pair of knotted gloves, and a knife wrapped in oilcloth. Jennie looks at them. Then she takes the gloves.

> **GRISE:** Before they sail.
>
> **JENNIE:** She taught me to hold a knife.
>
> **GRISE:** Then she'll know you're holding it properly.

**Exit state:** Before: Jennie was watching. After: she has the order and the knife.

**Craft:** The one line of protest is the seed of her turn in Episode 9. Plant doubt early so the turn is earned (canon note).

### 17. Toys after supper

*Act Three · The Exhibition: the speech.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Turn

Eldgard takes the gallery stair and the hall goes quiet. He promises new lines to the east and light for another hundred years. Then: *"My son and heir. James."*

Jamie says the family story perfectly, exactly as he promised his brother he would. We've seen the cold open; the room hasn't. The applause is the first lie of the series, and only we know it. Jamie smiles into it like a man swallowing glass.

Then Nathaniel, on his brother's advice, steps forward in front of everyone with the gauntlet strapped to his arm.

On the gallery Josephine watches her elder son's hand go to his breast pocket for the third time. Byron at her shoulder. Jamie slips out through a service door. Nathaniel takes one step after him, and his father's hand closes on his arm: *"The Board of Trade, Nathaniel."* A grey man asks about the leap apparatus. Nathaniel looks at the service door closing. Then he answers the question.

> **JAMIE:** A hundred years ago our ancestor Alistair Harding, an inventor the Royal Society laughed at, found that the iron under Tyburn could be grown into light. Every lamp in this hall is his gift, and our promise.
>
> **ELDGARD:** *(smiling for the room, at the gauntlet)* Not tonight. Toys after supper.
>
> **JOSEPHINE:** That boy is planning something.
>
> **BYRON:** Then he takes after you, madam.

**Exit state:** Before: Nathaniel could follow his brother. After: he chose his father's recognition, and his brother walked out of the door alone.

**Craft:** Nathaniel's first choice of the season costs him the one thing he'll spend the season trying to recover. Make the cost his own choice, not bad luck.

### 18. The servants' stair

*Act Three · Harding House, the servants' stair. Eleven at night.* · Thread: Nixon & the kept Hargraves · Setup

The house is dark but for one lamp coming up the back stair: Ada, on her nightly round, polishing as she goes, lighting each landing's glass with a taper and a whispered word we can't hear. She meets Jamie coming the other way, in evening dress, rain on his shoulders, hours early. He has one of Nathaniel's spanners in his hand.

She looks at the spanner. She looks at him.

> **ADA:** You're home early, Master James.
>
> **JAMIE:** Go to bed, Ada.
>
> **ADA:** I've the second landing still.
>
> **JAMIE:** *(gently)* Please.
>
> *(She goes down. At the turn she stops, and stands, and listens.)*

**Exit state:** Before: Jamie's plan relied on an empty house. After: someone who reports everything has seen him.

**Craft:** Seed Ada from Episode 1 (canon requirement). This is the same choice she made about her brother. Here it's small; in Episode 9 it kills her.

### 19. Half an orange

*Act Three · The Bounds room, Gin Lane.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Turn

Alice has her coat on and her hand on the bolt. The warehouse is still with her: Miriam's boots off the floor, the iron hand. She lifts the latch. Stops on *Please, Ali. Not tonight.* Following now would mean refusing the first piece of trust Miriam has ever given her.

She closes the door. Bolts it. Takes off the coat. She peels the orange, eats half, and leaves the other half on Miriam's pillow.

**Exit state:** Before: Alice could still go to the bridge. After: she has chosen to believe in tomorrow.

**Craft:** A silent decision with an exit state. The half orange becomes the most painful prop in Episode 2.

### 20. The ornament

*Act Four · Harding House, the study. Quarter to twelve.* · Thread: The light / Morgana · Turn, Reveal

Jamie locks the study door behind him. The ornament sits where it always sits, humming. He sets Nathaniel's spanner to the brass cage. *Krrk. Krrk.* The cage gives. He lifts the glass, and it is warm, and it is beating, and he brings it down on the edge of the desk.

**KSSSHHH.** Not a shatter but an exhale. Light the colour of deep ice floods the room, the fire in the grate goes out flat, frost races across the carpet in ferns. The light is starving. It goes for the only living thing in the room. It goes *into* him.

Horror, close and silent. Frost blooms up Jamie's throat and over his jaw; his breath comes out in white blades. He feels it trying to put him on like a coat, trying to find room in him, and he fights it the only way he can: he thinks of her. *The bridge. The bridge. Midnight. She's waiting.* The light takes the thought like a hand taking a key.

It tears out of him. The window bursts outward, *KSHHH-HHH*, glass spraying into the rain, and something bright goes down Mayfair like a falling star going the wrong way. Jamie lies on the frozen carpet, eyes open, frost to the heart, his breath a thread.

> **JAMIE:** *(through ice, to the light)* Not me. Not me. She's at the bridge. Go. *Go.*

**Exit state:** Before: the child was a prisoner. After: it is loose in London, carrying Jamie's thought of Miriam, and the heir of House Harding is frozen on his father's floor.

**Craft:** The set piece is one man and one object in one room: intimate horror, cheap to shoot, expensive to feel. The rule that the living fight back is shown, not said.

### 21. Every lamp at once

*Act Four · The Exhibition / London.* · Thread: The light / Morgana · Turn

At the Works, every chandelier in the great hall dips at once and holds at half-light, *whmmmmm*, a thousand crystals breathing in. Across London every lamp does the same: Mayfair, Gin Lane, a tram on the Strand, a hospital ward. For three seconds the whole city is a candle someone has cupped a hand around.

Under the hall, under the floor, Nathaniel hears a woman scream. Not crying now: a scream, rising. He turns to his father.

A Harding footboy, soaked, red-faced from running, is at Eldgard's elbow with a note in Ada's hand. Eldgard reads it once. He folds it. He doesn't look at Josephine; he simply walks, and she is already walking beside him.

> **GUEST:** *(laughing nervously)* Essex again?
>
> **NATHANIEL:** *(to no one)* Can't you hear that?

**Exit state:** Before: the escape was private. After: London has felt it, the mother has felt it, and Eldgard knows.

**Craft:** Make the rule visible on a city scale: the light dims with Morgana's grief. It's the first public sign of the engine and seeds the Charter hearing in Episode 4.

### 22. Blackwood Bridge

*Act Four · Blackwood Bridge, the upper stair. Midnight.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Turn

Midnight. Miriam waits at the upper stair in her red mittens, three tickets inside her coat, the river black and loud below. The hired carriage turns onto the bridge exactly as promised and stops. The door opens.

Jennie steps out.

The two women look at each other across the wet cobbles. Neither of them pretends. It is a Vykra duel in the rain: two students of the same woman, the same feints, the same economy. Miriam is better, and Miriam is tired, her throat still purple from the iron hand. She disarms Jennie once. Jennie has a second knife; Grise always gives two.

The blade goes in under Miriam's ribs. Miriam's hand closes on Jennie's wrist and holds it there, close enough to talk.

> **MIRIAM:** Who paid?
>
> **JENNIE:** Does it matter?
>
> **MIRIAM:** It'll matter to Ali.
>
> *(Jennie pulls the knife. Miriam steps back, once, twice, into the rail, and over.)*

**Exit state:** Before: Miriam was leaving. After: she's in the river, and Jennie is standing on the bridge not calling for help.

**Craft:** Replace the old poison-and-disguise machinery with one knife and two women who know each other. 'Who paid?' is the question that drives Alice's season. Jennie watches the water too long: seed her doubt.

### 23. Go back for my sister

*Act Four · Under the Thames.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Turn, Reveal

Black water, cold enough to stop a heart. Miriam sinks with her coat spreading around her like a dark flower, blood threading up from her side. The red mittens drift. Her lips move: *one post, two post.*

Then light, coming down through the water toward her. Ice-blue, shapeless, reaching. Something that has been in a box for sixteen years and has just come through London on a dead man's thought, looking for this exact face. It arrives seconds too late to save her. Not too late to hear her.

She sees it. She isn't afraid of it. She's past afraid. She gives it the only thing she has left.

> **MIRIAM:** *(a mouthful of bubbles; we read it on her lips and hear it in her head)* Go back for my sister.
>
> *(Her eyes close. The light goes into her. Under the water, they open again: green.)*

**Exit state:** Before: the child was looking for a body. After: it has one, and a promise, and the face of the girl it must find.

**Craft:** The heaviest moment gets silence: no score, a held shot. It explains, ahead of time, why the Lost One goes to Alice in Episode 2, with no coincidence.

### 24. As of tonight

*Act Four · Harding House, the study. Half past twelve.* · Thread: Jamie / the locked room · Turn, Reveal

The study is a frozen ruin: ferns of frost up the walls, the window gaping, rain blowing in over shards of the ornament. Byron kneels by Jamie with a tallow candle, holding his wrist, counting the pulse aloud. Ada stands in the doorway with her lamp. Eldgard comes in, takes it all in, and goes straight to the window to look out into the dark, where the thing went.

Josephine goes straight to her son.

> **BYRON:** He's alive. Barely.
>
> **JOSEPHINE:** Then send for—
>
> **ELDGARD:** No one. *(turning from the window)* He let it out. If he wakes, the first thing he'll say is what's under the Works.
>
> **JOSEPHINE:** He's our son.
>
> **ELDGARD:** As of tonight he's dead. A burglar. The window. Byron, the top room. Ada—
>
> **ADA:** I saw nothing, sir.
>
> **ELDGARD:** *(a long look at her)* I know.

**Exit state:** Before: the family had an heir. After: it has a secret upstairs and a funeral to stage, and every adult in the house has agreed to a lie.

**Craft:** The coma decision is made on screen, by choice, so the midseason reveal is earned rather than a cheat. Protection as control in its purest form: they save his life by burying him.

### 25. The banister

*Act Four · Harding House, the great stair. One in the morning.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Turn

Nathaniel comes home last, still in his evening coat, the gauntlet case under his arm. The Board of Trade men kept him an hour. He's happy. He hasn't been happy in years.

The hall is full of the wrong people at the wrong hour: servants, a constable, Byron on the stair with his hand out. Frost on the banister, in ferns. Nathaniel puts his palm on it and it doesn't melt.

> **BYRON:** Sir. Don't go up.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** Where's Jamie?
>
> *(Byron doesn't answer. Upstairs, a door closes.)*

**Exit state:** Before: Nathaniel had the best night of his life. After: he is a brother in mourning who doesn't yet know he's been lied to.

**Craft:** Give the lead the last human beat before the tag. His happiness is the price he paid at the service door.

### 26. My child is out

*Tag · The Harding Works, the control room. Three in the morning.* · Thread: The light / Morgana · Reveal, Setup

A long room of brass gauges and needle dials above the core, every needle shivering. Silas Pike sits alone with his ledger, writing the yield in a tidy hand. It fell eleven points at midnight and hasn't come back. He doesn't know why. The speaking-pipes that carry the core's hum to the gauge room, which have hummed one note for his whole working life, change.

A woman's voice comes through the brass. Not words in any language. Words in his.

> **MORGANA:** *(through the pipes, very calm)* My child is out.
>
> *(Pike's pencil rolls off the desk. Tick. Tick. Tick across the floor.)*
>
> **CUT TO BLACK.**

**Exit state:** Before: Pike knew the numbers were falling. After: he knows the light is alive, and it has just spoken to him.

**Craft:** The tag changes the board (Rule 8): a man who has already caught Lambrick's eye now has something to sell. Morgana's first spoken line goes to the wrong person, a Weeks-style secret leaking through the side door.
