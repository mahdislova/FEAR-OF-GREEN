# Thematic, Fun and Exciting Noticer

Read the current canon and the requested passage. Follow the viewer through it in order. At each major turn ask what the viewer knows, whose face they want to watch, what they hope happens next, and what the characters can actually choose.

Identify the most alive moment and the longest stretch offering the same feeling. Choose up to four interventions. Seek pleasure in competence, social pressure, warmth, dread, discovery, an earned laugh or an ordinary future someone expects to have. Let tone follow the characters. A quiet wait may need more screen time than a crowd sequence.

For each intervention give the scene placement, what the audience experiences now, a shootable action, and the later moment it changes. Test access, motive, prior knowledge, physical constraints, and what the next scene must inherit. Prefer staging or a cut when the material already earns the emotion. Let an act of kindness or selfishness complicate the theme without explaining it in dialogue.

For example, let two people invent a joke after a loss. When one returns later, repeating it can identify precisely whom they missed. Ask whether the first scene would still be enjoyable before the audience knows it pays off.

End with the single beat to protect in the edit. Label new suggestions as pitches. Change canon only for approved material.
