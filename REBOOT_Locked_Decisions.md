# The Green Gallows — Reboot: Locked Decisions

Working log of canon agreed in the reboot conversation, 17 November 2026. This supersedes the lore in `FULL NEW SEASON (5).md` wherever they conflict. The bible will be rewritten from this.

## The secret, in one breath
The light is a living thing in a freezer, and the Hardings got it by breaking a promise.

## Origin (c. 1780)
- **Alistair Harding**, a failed inventor, finds a crashed ship in the woods by the Harding estate.
- He captures the being aboard, **Morgana**, and tortures her. She has a built-in universal translator: she speaks to anyone in their own language.
- **The deal:** teach him the ship's power system, and he will let her and her offspring go. She teaches him.
- **The betrayal:** he hooks her into her own cryo-stasis pod and draws her life-source energy, keeping her frozen and harvested for months, then years. First gallowglass. He writes every secret down for the family.
- **1783:** Tyburn's gallows are taken down. The Hardings buy the gallows field and build the Works over it. The name explains itself.
- **The ship is never seen whole.** Only pieces of it inside the Works machinery. Morgana stays unknowable: no origin dump.

## The massacre: the Hargraves
- **Hargrave** is the village on the edge of the Harding woods, and its people hauled the wreck for Alistair.
- Alistair's first full draw kills the village: a cryo backlash that freezes it in one night.
- Survivors who talked were called rebels and hanged at Tyburn: the last mass hanging before the gallows came down. A cover-up.
- **One survivor, a child.** Alistair took the child in out of guilt. Every generation since, the Hardings have kept a Hargrave close: ward, servant, Guardsman. Guilt and surveillance, never separable. "My family looks after its own."
- City built on a massacre and a stolen creature. Two crimes, one secret.

## Nixon
- The current kept Hargrave. Eldgard raised him; a father who never was.
- His wound: he cannot tell whether Eldgard loved him or kept him. The answer is both.
- Learning it breaks him, and breaks the friendship: Nathaniel's whole life is lit by Nixon's people, and Nixon now holds the keys to the house that killed them.
- **Tobias Marsh** replaces the Orchard material: the Newgate prisoner who whispers the true Hargrave history. He is the previous kept Hargrave who asked questions and was locked away as a madman. Nixon's future if he keeps asking. Exact blood tie (e.g. uncle) to be decided.

## Nathaniel's buried memory (c. 1873)
- Two generations after Alistair, an experiment goes wrong and the **offspring** escapes the cryo underground. It is life-source energy; its body did not survive the crash.
- It wanders into five-year-old Nathaniel's room and imprints a memory deep in his subconscious: understanding of the ship's technology.
- Eldgard traps it in an **ornament** (a piece of the ship's containment) and mesmerises Nathaniel with a swinging lantern to make the memory a dream.
- Payoff: Nathaniel's engineering genius was never his own. He is also the only person who could design a way to shut the core down safely.

## The Lost One (1889)
- Jamie's sabotage cracks the ornament. The offspring escapes, reaches the river, and takes the nearest dying body: Miriam's.
- She knows Alice's face because Miriam's last thought was Alice. Her cold is the cryo energy she was soaked in.
- She carries **two griefs in one body**: Miriam's memories and guilt, and glimpses of what she actually is: her mother's voice, a sky she has never seen.
- **The break:** Alice pushes her away. "You're not her. You're not my sister." The Lost One: why do I have all her memories, why do I love you so much?
- **The choice:** she learns love and unsaid sisterhood, and chooses to stay with Alice. She stays because Alice lets her leave.

## Morgana and the finale
- Ninety years drained. She is dying, so London's light is dying: **this is the clock**, replacing the Tithe.
- Betrayed and tortured: she has every right to go mayhem, and she does. Freeing her is just and catastrophic.
- **Final image of the season:** a lone ship in the sky over dark London. The Lost One looks up at it, then back at Alice.

## Cut
The White, the wolves, the Tithe and first snow, the Orchard and Hargrave breeding, Edric, both Marians, all 1489 lore, the lockets as magic.
