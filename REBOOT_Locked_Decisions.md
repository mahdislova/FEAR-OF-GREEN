# The Green Gallows — Reboot: Locked Decisions

Working log of canon agreed in the reboot conversation, 17 November 2026. This supersedes the lore in `FULL NEW SEASON (5).md` wherever they conflict. The bible will be rewritten from this.

## The secret, in one breath
The light is a living thing in a freezer, and the Hardings got it by breaking a promise.

## Origin (c. 1780)
- **Alistair Harding**, a failed inventor, finds a crashed ship in the woods by the Harding estate.
- He captures the being aboard, **Morgana**, and tortures her. She has a built-in universal translator: she speaks to anyone in their own language.
- **The deal:** teach him the ship's power system, and he will let her and her offspring go. She teaches him.
- **The betrayal:** he hooks her into her own cryo-stasis pod and draws her life-source energy, keeping her frozen and harvested for months, then years. First gallowglass. He writes every secret down for the family.
- **1783:** Tyburn's gallows are taken down. The Hardings buy the gallows field and build the Works over it. The name explains itself.
- **The ship is never seen whole.** Only pieces of it inside the Works machinery. Morgana stays unknowable: no origin dump.

## The massacre: the Hargraves
- **Hargrave** is the village on the edge of the Harding woods, and its people hauled the wreck for Alistair.
- Alistair's first full draw kills the village: a cryo backlash that freezes it in one night.
- Survivors who talked were called rebels and hanged at Tyburn: the last mass hanging before the gallows came down. A cover-up.
- **One survivor, a child.** Alistair took the child in out of guilt. Every generation since, the Hardings have kept a Hargrave close: ward, servant, Guardsman. Guilt and surveillance, never separable. "My family looks after its own."
- City built on a massacre and a stolen creature. Two crimes, one secret.

## Nixon
- The current kept Hargrave. Eldgard raised him; a father who never was.
- His wound: he cannot tell whether Eldgard loved him or kept him. The answer is both.
- Learning it breaks him, and breaks the friendship: Nathaniel's whole life is lit by Nixon's people, and Nixon now holds the keys to the house that killed them.
- **Tobias Marsh** replaces the Orchard material: the Newgate prisoner who whispers the true Hargrave history. He is the previous kept Hargrave who asked questions and was locked away as a madman. Nixon's future if he keeps asking. He is Ada's brother and Nixon's uncle (see Ada Hargrave).

## Ada Hargrave
Supporting role, not a finale player.
- **Who:** about sixty, head housekeeper at Harding House. Has polished the same lamps for forty years. Keeps the cryo clean, so she knows Morgana exists.
- **Family:** Ada and Tobias Marsh are siblings. Nixon is their nephew, son of their younger sister, who died. Ada raised him below stairs; Marsh is the uncle he has never met. The kept Hargraves are one family, held close generation after generation.
- **The Lamp Saint faith:** how Hargrave's survivors made the massacre bearable. *Our dead became the light*: every green lamp is a Hargrave martyr shining. Morgana is **the Lady**, a saint fallen from heaven, given to the Hardings to light the world, with Hargrave's dead as her first offering. Ada tends a tortured prisoner like a shrine. The faith is a lie: the light is Morgana's blood and the village died as collateral. Ada never learns it; the audience carries it for her.
- **With Nixon:** first to tell him he was special. His loyalty comes from her love and Eldgard's approval. The Hardings pay for his schooling and rank. She is the only one in the house who calls him "love."
- **With Marsh:** when he ran, she reported him. She has never visited Newgate. She calls him "the apostate."
- **With the Hardings:** keeps the rite and the family's secrets. Eldgard depends on her; Josephine trusts her.
- **Ep 8, after the revelation, Nixon asks if she knew:** "Forgive them? Love, I forgave them before you were born. They took us in when they didn't have to. That's more than God did for Hargrave."
- **Her death:** she works out that Nixon is planning the break-out and goes to report him to Eldgard, as she reported her brother. On the servants' stair he begs her to wait one night. She touches his cheek: "I'm saving you, love. We're not them. We were never going to be them. We're Hargraves." He panics and holds his hand over her mouth too long. Her lamp smashes on the stairs. She dies still believing. He goes into the break-out with her blood on his cuffs and frees the brother she betrayed.
- **Mirror:** a sibling betrayed to protect the order (Ada), a man drowned to protect a sibling (Miriam), a hand over a mouth (Nixon). The show's central act in three keys.
- **Requirements:** seed her from Episode 1 (lamps, Nixon's collar, "love"). Her death must have aftermath: someone finds her on the stairs, and Nixon chooses whether to lie.

## Nathaniel's buried memory (c. 1873)
- Two generations after Alistair, an experiment goes wrong and the **offspring** escapes the cryo underground. It is life-source energy; its body did not survive the crash.
- It wanders into five-year-old Nathaniel's room and imprints a memory deep in his subconscious: understanding of the ship's technology.
- Eldgard traps it in an **ornament** (a piece of the ship's containment) and mesmerises Nathaniel with a swinging lantern to make the memory a dream.
- Payoff: Nathaniel's engineering genius was never his own. He is also the only person who could design a way to shut the core down safely.

## The Lost One (1889)
- Jamie's sabotage cracks the ornament. The offspring escapes, reaches the river, and takes the nearest dying body: Miriam's.
- She knows Alice's face because Miriam's last thought was Alice. Her cold is the cryo energy she was soaked in.
- She carries **two griefs in one body**: Miriam's memories and guilt, and glimpses of what she actually is: her mother's voice, a sky she has never seen.
- **The break:** Alice pushes her away. "You're not her. You're not my sister." The Lost One: why do I have all her memories, why do I love you so much?
- **The choice:** she learns love and unsaid sisterhood, and chooses to stay with Alice. She stays because Alice lets her leave.

## Jamie
- **How he finds out:** heir's initiation on his 25th birthday. Eldgard shows him the core. Morgana asks him one thing: "Where is my child?"
- **Why he breaks ranks:** in 1873, before reaching Nathaniel's room, the offspring visited Jamie's and imprinted resistance to the Harding aura (below). Nathaniel got the knowledge; Jamie got the will. The offspring planted two seeds in two brothers. The Lost One does not remember doing it.
- **His plan:** free the child, not the mother (he can't bring himself to darken London), and run with Miriam.
- **Episode 1 night:** the Exhibition at the Works (Nathaniel's gauntlet unveiled). Jamie slips home, breaks the ornament. The starving offspring tries to wear him, ravages him, tears out carrying his loudest thought (Miriam at the bridge) and leaves through the window.
- **Coma, not death.** Found frozen. The family declares him dead: public funeral, closed coffin. He lies in a locked room at the top of Harding House, alive, alien frost in his blood, tended in secret. Midseason reveal: Nathaniel finds him.
- **Rule:** Jamie never wakes to explain the plot. Leaning: he stays under all season, Season 2's fuse.

## Miriam's death
- Quitting the Vykra to run with a Harding heir. Grise won't let that leverage walk. A Vykra knife on the bridge; she goes into the river bleeding.
- The offspring reaches her as she dies. Her last words to it: "Go back for my sister." It takes her. The Lost One is keeping a dead woman's promise.

## Nathaniel and Alice: the season spine
- **Nathaniel's mission:** find who killed his brother. Jamie was found frozen, so London blames the Frost Killer. The killer he hunts is the Lost One, hiding in his own workshop.
- **Alice's mission:** find who killed Miriam. The answer is her own guild, the Vykra.
- Each investigation points at the other's people.
- **Alice's spy job:** Josephine, through Grise, hires Alice to get close to Nathaniel. The romance starts as a lie.
- **The hideout:** Nathaniel shelters the sisters in his workshop. His tech can manage the Lost One's cold. Being near her stirs his buried memory.
- Season-long will-they-won't-they while they investigate together.

## The Harding aura (proposed rules)
An Alistair recipe, built from Morgana's own technology. It keeps Hardings from betraying the secret.
1. Works on Harding blood only. Josephine and Nixon are outside it; their choices are entirely their own.
2. Persuasion, not compulsion. It amplifies loyalty that is already there and dulls doubt. It cannot make anyone do what they don't half-want. Eldgard's choices remain his.
3. Strength grows with proximity to the core and years of exposure. Nathaniel, shut out as the second son, has had the least. Being unwanted kept him free.
4. Jamie's imprint made him immune.

## Morgana and the finale
- Ninety years drained. She is dying, so London's light is dying: **this is the clock**, replacing the Tithe.
- Betrayed and tortured: she has every right to go mayhem, and she does. Freeing her is just and catastrophic.
- **Final image of the season:** a lone ship in the sky over dark London. The Lost One looks up at it, then back at Alice, and stays.
- **Morgana's end:** too weak to fight on, she boards the ship. Before it retreats, it does substantial damage to London, and she vows revenge: hell brought down on this hellhole. Season 2's dread and setup. Full details to come.
