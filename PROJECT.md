# The Green Gallows

A ten-episode gothic drama. London, November 1889. The whole city glows green, and the light is a living thing in a freezer, stolen by a family that broke its word.

## Where everything lives

| Folder / file | Owns | Never put here |

| `archive/` | **Superseded. Not the story.** The old season, early reboot logs, and Claude's superseded drafts. Where it conflicts with canon, canon wins | Anything new |

**One fact, one home.** If you need a fact from another file, point to it; don't copy it.
