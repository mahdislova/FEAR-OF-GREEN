# World and rules

## The secret, in one breath
The light is a living thing in a freezer, and the Hardings got it by breaking a promise.

## The engine
The light is dying. Morgana has been drained for over a century and is failing. The Works' engineers watch the yield fall; the lamps flicker; London doesn't know why. When she dies, the Hardings mean to put her child in the pod in her place.

**Clock card:** every episode opens on THE LIGHT HAS 49 DAYS (counting down).

## History

**c. 1780 — the crash.** Alistair Harding, a failed inventor, finds a crashed ship in the woods by the Harding estate. He captures the being aboard, **Morgana**, and tortures her. She has a built-in universal translator: she speaks to anyone in their own language.

**The deal.** Teach him the ship's power system, and he will let her and her offspring go. She teaches him.

**The betrayal.** He hooks her into her own cryo-stasis pod and draws her life-source energy, keeping her frozen and harvested for months, then years. The first gallowglass. He writes every secret down for the family in his own cipher: **the Harding book**.

**The Hargrave massacre.** Hargrave was the village on the edge of the Harding woods; its people hauled the wreck for Alistair. His first full draw went wrong: a cryo backlash froze the village in one night. Survivors who talked were called rebels and hanged at Tyburn, the last mass hanging before the gallows came down. One survivor, a child, was taken into the house out of guilt. Every generation since, the Hardings have kept a Hargrave close: ward, servant, Guardsman. Guilt and surveillance, never separable. "My family looks after its own."

**1783.** Tyburn's gallows are taken down; hangings move to Newgate. The Hardings buy the gallows field and build the Works over it. The name *Green Gallows* explains itself.

**Two generations later.** London and the countryside run on green light. The Hardings are the most powerful family in the city.

**1873 — the escape.** An experiment goes wrong and Morgana's offspring escapes the cryo underground. It is life-source energy; its own body did not survive the crash. On its mother's instruction, it goes to the two sons of the house:
- **Jamie** (nine): it imprints resistance to the Harding aura.
- **Nathaniel** (five): it imprints understanding of the ship's technology.

Eldgard traps it in an **ornament**, a piece of the ship's containment, and mesmerises Nathaniel with a swinging lantern so the memory becomes a dream.

**1889.** See `continuity-and-reveals.md` and `season-one.md`.

## The rules

**Gallowglass and the core**
1. Gallowglass is Morgana's life-source energy, drawn from her in the pod. There is only one source.
2. She is dying. The yield falls every year. When she dies, the light dies, unless something takes her place. Her offspring can.
3. Opening the pod frees her and ends the light.

**The Harding aura** (an Alistair recipe, built from Morgana's technology, centred on the Works)
1. It works on Harding blood only. Josephine, Nixon and every servant are outside it: their choices are entirely their own.
2. It persuades; it does not compel. It amplifies loyalty that is already there and dulls doubt. It cannot make anyone do what they don't half-want. **Eldgard chose.**
3. Its strength grows with proximity to the core and years of exposure. Nathaniel, shut out as the second son, has had the least. Being unwanted kept him free.
4. Jamie's 1873 imprint made him immune.

**The offspring and the Lost One**
1. The offspring has no body. It can try to wear a living person, but the living fight back. That is what ravaged Jamie: it tried, failed and tore back out.
2. It can take a body at the moment of death. That is how it became the Lost One, in Miriam.
3. It carries what the dying hold most. It left Jamie carrying his thought of Miriam at the bridge. It took Miriam carrying Alice, and her last words: "Go back for my sister."
4. Its cold is the cryo energy it was soaked in. It comes out when she feels too much or chooses to use it, and it kills by touch. The cold holds Miriam's body exactly as it was in the river.
5. Miriam's memories return to her one at a time, never in the order she'd choose, and the heaviest comes last. Glimpses of what she actually is come too: her mother's voice, a sky she has never seen.
6. Harding technology can contain her cold. That is why Nathaniel's workshop can hide her: a cold-sink of two gauntlet cores and a copper coil wound in the imprint's spiral, worn at the wrist (Ep 3).

**Morgana's voice and grief**
1. She can speak through the Ring: lamps and the Works' speaking-pipes carry her voice. She chooses who hears.
2. The light dips with her grief. When her child escapes (Ep 1), every lamp in London breathes in at once; the yield falls eleven points and stays down.
3. Her child can feel her through the lamps and is drawn toward the light. Touching a lamp or a crystal tree with bare hands makes it frost over and sing.

**Mesmerism.** Victorian mesmerism is real in this world but small: a swinging light, a quiet voice and a frightened child. It buries memories; it doesn't create them.

## What gallowglass does
- Burned in lamps and furnaces, it gives a green-white light and steady heat. **The Ring**, London's lamp grid, carries it under every street.
- The Works can cut the Ring district by district. In Episode 6 Eldgard cuts the East End to keep the West lit.
- Ground to dust it becomes **glim**, the undercity's drug. Users hear **the Weeping**: a woman crying under the hum. It is Morgana in the pod. *(Carried over from the old bible; re-review when scenes are written.)*

## Factions

**House Harding.** Keepers of the light and the secret. Harding House in Mayfair; the Works over Tyburn, with the core and the pod deep below.

**The Lambricks.** Coal. Hector Lambrick is losing the light war and wants London back on coal. When he learns what the light is, he wants to own it. Tamara, his daughter, is the only person who can break Alistair's cipher. Ajax is his enforcer.

**The Vykra.** A guild of undercity women, spies and knives for hire, run by Mother Grise from a Shadwell laundry. Their mark is a knot stitched inside the glove. They sell to every faction.

**The Royal Lantern Guard.** London's elite, armed by the Harding Works, hunting the Frost Killer. Oath: *Light the dark. Keep the flame.* Ranks: Wick (recruit), Flame (trooper), Lantern-Captain. *(Gear carried over from the old bible, minus anything tied to the White: lantern gauntlet with flash-burst, leap-boosters, tether-hook, quench-blade, lantern rifle. Re-review when scenes are written.)*

**The kept Hargraves.** Not a faction: a family. Ada, Marsh and Nixon are what's left. Ada's faith, the Lamp Saint faith, is their way of surviving: *our dead became the light*. See `characters/ada-hargrave.md`.

St Bride's. Westminster, Committee Room 14. The Bond Street tea shop. Blackwood Bridge and the Thames foreshore. The Harding Works over Tyburn, with the core and the pod below. ## The ship
Never seen whole until the finale. Pieces of it live inside the Works machinery. The ornament is one. In Episode 10 a ship comes for Morgana.
