# Season one

**Status:** the spine is provisional; Episode 1 is the episode the writer is happy with. Ideas are locked, pitched or open (see `handoff/01-READ-FIRST.md`). Open questions live in `PROJECT.md`.

## The promise

**We follow Nathaniel Harding as he hunts his brother's killer, choosing the people in front of him over the family that made him, until he must decide whether London's light is worth a child in a freezer.**

Under it: **Alice Bounds learns her sister is dead from the thing that loves her like one, until she must let it choose.**

The audience's pleasure: an investigation, a forbidden intimacy, and dread that keeps climbing.

**The season's question:** *is keeping someone the same as loving them?* The pod keeps Morgana to drain her; the ornament kept her child; Nathaniel's cold-sink keeps the Lost One's cold in. The Hardings keep the light, a Hargrave, the West. Alice holds on; Nixon was kept; Josephine killed to keep her son home. The ending answers it: the Lost One stays because she was free to leave.

**Theme:** protection as control. Every adult hurts someone to keep them safe. Underneath it: bought or given, who pays for the light, what gets written down, the spare and the heir, where you belong.

## The threads

**Nathaniel and Nixon.** Ep 1: Nixon is away in the east; his empty chair says it all ("That's Nixon's chair, Nat"). Ep 2: Nixon comes home to report village after village frozen in ice, and gets the keys and the father. Ep 3: they lie to each other in both directions. **Ep 5, the first break:** Nixon, hunting the Frost Killer for Eldgard, discovers Nathaniel has hidden the woman London suspects of killing Jamie. Nathaniel says the accusation is unproved. **Ep 7:** Nathaniel hears her confession beside Alice and tries to restrain her; Alice intervenes and sends her away. **Ep 8, the point of no return:** Nathaniel confirms the confession to Nixon. Nixon makes the arrest and takes the Lost One, then reads the Hargrave pages and understands what he has delivered to Eldgard. Ep 9: same side, not friends. Ep 10: changed, not restored.

**Jamie's death.** Ep 1: the offspring, starved and half-mad, ravages Jamie as it escapes; he dies of his wounds. London blames the Frost Killer (Ep 3), and Nathaniel hunts his brother's killer while sheltering her. Her memories of it return slowly. Ep 5 brings an incomplete fragment; in Ep 7 she tells Alice who she is and, when Nathaniel arrives, remembers Jamie's death clearly enough to tell them both. Nathaniel's protective act in Ep 10 must coexist with his grief, not erase it.

**The two imprints.** Ep 1: the gauntlet Nathaniel can't explain, and its misfire. Ep 2 cold open: Nathaniel as a boy, a shadow and eyes as limitless as the sky, a shriek; a nightmare. Eps 3–5: Nathaniel's memory flickers near the Lost One. **Ep 6, reveal #1:** Nathaniel remembers all of 1873; his genius was planted and his father erased him. **Ep 7:** the Lost One, regaining memories of what she is, tells him about the pod and reminds him the way in is buried in that memory. **Ep 9, reveal #2:** the same night from the offspring's side, Jamie's room first; Morgana tells the Lost One she sent her. Same night, two readings.

**The sisters.** Ep 1: the Narrows raid with Jennie, the birthday orange, "Not tonight." Ep 2: on Grise's lie that Miriam abandoned her, Alice joins the Vykra, then gets "Miriam" back. Eps 3–6: closeness. Ep 6: Grise tells Alice Miriam's entry price, their stepfather. **Ep 7, the break:** the Lost One tells Alice that Miriam died; Nathaniel arrives for her account of Jamie's death. Alice stops him restraining the Lost One, then sends her away without forgiving her. "You're not her." **Ep 8 morning:** Alice returns by choice. "Nine is the one where we get up." Ep 10: Alice offers her the ship; she stays.

**The killer (Josephine), one beat per episode.** Ep 1: Miriam falls; a figure in the shadows backs away; Jennie watches. Ep 2: Josephine burns her gloves; Jennie sees Miriam’s face in Alice’s room. Ep 3: Jennie withholds the bridge account. Ep 4: Grise's hand-sign test; she sells the location to Lambrick. Ep 5: Josephine summons Alice privately. Ep 6: Josephine and Grise; Josephine pays to remove the witness. The same night Alice tells Nathaniel herself that his mother hired her. Ep 7: the Lost One saves Jennie. Ep 8: after Alice returns, Jennie names Josephine. Josephine supplies a lead for the arrest. Ep 10: Alice confronts Josephine and spares her.

**The Duke.** Ep 2: he agrees to be wrong in public, "for Jamie. Not for the light." Ep 4: Hector breaks him at the hearing; "Ask me when the lamps in my sister's street go out." Ep 6: his ultimatum at the Works: if the light fails, the Royal Engineers go into the vaults. It's why Eldgard keeps the West lit. Ep 9: he serves his warrant in person at the Works gate (engineers at dusk) and asks Hector his own question in front of the clerks. Tragic, then terrifying.

**Jennie, the warm body.** Ep 1: the raid, then the bridge from the stair. Ep 2: the gloves at the door. Ep 3: the buns; "Don't tell Grise." Ep 4: "It's a terrible message"; she sees what Alice is in the fog and tells Grise her first lie. Ep 5: the truthful sighting. Ep 6: crumpets; "She liked that." Ep 7: Garrow; she takes the hand. Ep 8: she names Josephine. Ep 9: she signs. Ep 10: the orange.

**Alice's aura moments,** each one planted. Ep 4: the bounty men in the fog. Ep 6: the knife at Grise's throat. Ep 7: Miriam's throw, landed at last (planted Ep 1).

**Grise, to the end.** Sells to everyone (Eps 1–6). Ep 7: Garrow dies, Jennie walks, and her girls watch her do nothing. Ep 9: she burns the bridge-night page. Ep 10: her laundry empties in the dark; she takes Josephine out of London, the one client who can pay for ever. Survival was always her only loyalty.

**The plot twist (Hector and Tamara).** Ep 3: she writes Ajax's dead men's names; Hector never asked them. Ep 4: Hector wears her move against the Duke. Ep 7: she sees the Gin Lane audit in his drawer; Ajax takes the first cart east on her orders. Ep 9: the Duke humiliates Hector at the Works gate and hands her the Crown's coal. Hector orders Ajax to kill her. Ajax sells him to her. In the Gin Lane crater: "I would have lived in peace, Father. You brought war to my doorstep." Ep 10: she opens the depots over his body, and the East takes the blame and the coal.

**Tamara.** Ep 1: the only one who doesn't laugh. Ep 2: Eldgard sees her. Ep 3: the relined coat. Ep 4: her move, her father's applause. Ep 5: the toast; the conservatory ("I'm going to be very good at it, Nix"). Ep 6: right about the branch, too late; Eldgard gives her Alistair's book. Ep 7: writes her own marriage and takes the East's coal; "The rest is me." Ep 8: the pages; the vow of honesty, broken by her silence at breakfast; the ask Nixon refuses. Ep 9: covers Ada's death. Ep 10: the coup; across the street.

## The ending
London is dark, and the dark kills: Eldgard was partly right. Morgana keeps Eldgard as he kept her: alive in gallowglass beside the empty pod, where the Royal Engineers find him under the Duke's warrant. The Crown ends the Hardings in law; Lambrick coal wins the dark, in Tamara's hands, over her father's body. Grise takes Josephine downriver. Opening the pod released the beacon Alistair silenced, and that is what calls the ship. Morgana, freed and too weak to fight on, boards the ship after it damages the city. Morgana waits aboard the ship. When her child chooses Alice, she lets her stay and departs in silence. Lambrick's coal is king: Nathaniel's righteous act has handed London to his nemesis. Tamara opens the depots to the East for nothing. The Lost One looks up at the ship, then back at Alice, and stays. At dawn, in Jennie's room over the dyehouse, warmed by Lambrick coal, Alice halves an orange with her: the half orange from Ep 1, paid off.

**Season 2 setup:** a dark London run on Lambrick coal; the Crown in the Works, with a man in crystal who can still see; the ship somewhere above; Tamara holding Nixon's worst act (Ada) while he knows nothing of hers (Hector), with Ajax beside her; Grise and Josephine loose with the Harding pearls and every secret.
