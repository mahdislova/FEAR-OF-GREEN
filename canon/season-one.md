# Season one

**Status:** the spine is provisional; Episode 1 is the episode the writer is happy with. Ideas are locked, pitched or open (see `handoff/01-READ-FIRST.md`). Open questions live in `PROJECT.md`.

## The promise

**We follow Nathaniel Harding as he hunts his brother's killer, choosing the people in front of him over the family that made him, until he must decide whether London's light is worth a child in a freezer.**

Under it: **Alice Bounds learns her sister is dead from the thing that loves her like one, until she must let it choose.**

The audience's pleasure: an investigation, a forbidden intimacy, and dread that keeps climbing.

**Theme:** protection as control. Every adult hurts someone to keep them safe. Underneath it: bought or given, who pays for the light, what gets written down, the spare and the heir, where you belong.

## The threads

**Nathaniel and Nixon.** Ep 1: Nixon is away in the east; his empty chair says it all ("That's Nixon's chair, Nat"). Ep 2: Nixon comes home to report village after village frozen in ice, and gets the keys and the father. Ep 3: they lie to each other in both directions. **Ep 5, the first break:** Nixon, hunting the Frost Killer for Eldgard, learns Nathaniel has been hiding his brother's killer. He can't understand it, and for a while Nathaniel can't explain it either. **Ep 8, the point of no return:** Nixon leads the raid and takes the Lost One, then learns what was done to Hargrave and understands what he just did. Ep 9: same side, not friends. Ep 10: changed, not restored.

**Jamie's death.** Ep 1: the offspring, starved and half-mad, ravages Jamie as it escapes; he dies of his wounds. London blames the Frost Killer (Ep 3), and Nathaniel hunts his brother's killer while sheltering her. Her memories of it return slowly. When she finally understands what she did, she goes to Nathaniel in tears, holds him tight: "I'm so sorry. There's something I have to tell you…" (Ep 5, the midseason).

**The two imprints.** Ep 1: the gauntlet Nathaniel can't explain, and its misfire. Ep 3 cold open: a fragment of 1873. Eps 3–5: Nathaniel's memory flickers near the Lost One. **Ep 6, reveal #1:** Nathaniel remembers all of 1873; his genius was planted and his father erased him. **Ep 9, reveal #2:** the same night from the offspring's side, Jamie's room first; Morgana tells the Lost One she sent her. Same night, two readings.

**The sisters.** Ep 1: the Narrows raid with Jennie, the birthday orange, "Not tonight." Ep 2: Alice gets "Miriam" back. Eps 3–6: closeness. Ep 6: Grise tells Alice Miriam's entry price, their stepfather. **Ep 7, the break:** the Lost One confirms and confesses on Miriam's behalf, which is how Alice knows. "You're not her." Ep 8 morning: "Nine is the one where we get up." Ep 10: Alice offers her the ship; she stays.

**The killer (Josephine), one beat per episode.** Ep 1: Miriam falls; a figure in the shadows backs away; Jennie watches. Ep 2: Josephine burns her gloves (the audience knows). Ep 3: Jennie sees "Miriam" alive. Ep 4: Grise's hand-sign test; she sells the location to Lambrick. Ep 5: Josephine sees the Lost One in the mirror. Ep 6: Josephine and Grise; Josephine pays to remove the witness. Ep 7: the Lost One saves Jennie; Jennie tells Alice. Ep 8: Alice tells Nathaniel she was hired; Josephine gives Eldgard the workshop. Ep 10: Alice confronts Josephine and spares her.

## The ending
London is dark. Morgana, freed and too weak to fight on, boards the ship; it does substantial damage to the city, then retreats, and she vows revenge. Lambrick's coal is king: Nathaniel's righteous act has handed London to his nemesis. Tamara opens the depots to the East for nothing. The Lost One looks up at the ship, then back at Alice, and stays.

**Season 2 setup:** a dark London run on Lambrick coal, a ship and a vow somewhere above.
