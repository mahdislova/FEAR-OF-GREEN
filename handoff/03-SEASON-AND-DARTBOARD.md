# The Green Gallows: Season Shape and Dartboard

Nothing here is locked except where marked. The season shape was approved as a working spine, but the writer has only been happy with Episode 1 so far. Treat the rest as material for steps 1–4 of the process.

## Provisional spine (ten episodes)
| Ep | Title | The change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Exhibition | The secret gets out: the child escapes, Jamie falls, Miriam dies |
| 2 | The Wake | Two grieving siblings get missions; Alice takes the contract |
| 3 | The Frost Killer | Nathaniel takes a side without knowing it; he shelters the sisters |
| 4 | The Hearing | The light's failure goes public (Lambrick in Parliament) |
| 5 | Open (title) | Nixon discovers Nathaniel hid the suspected Frost Killer; their first break |
| 6 | The Remembering | A quiet aftermath. Nathaniel recovers the 1873 memory; the East End is cut off |
| 7 | The Girl Who Isn't | Alice learns Miriam died; she and Nathaniel hear the Jamie confession together; Alice stops him, sends the Lost One away |
| 8 | The Wedding | Alice returns by choice; Nathaniel confirms the confession to Nixon; the wedding and raid take the Lost One for the pod |
| 9 | The Break-out | Ada's death; Nixon frees Marsh; storming the Works; imprint reveal #2 |
| 10 | Lights Out | Morgana freed, mayhem, the ship, the Lost One stays, Lambrick wins |

Cold opens tell the history in pieces: the crash (Ep 1), Nathaniel's nightmare of 1873 (Ep 2), 1873 from the offspring's side (Ep 9), the deal (Ep 4), the sisters' childhood (Ep 7), the Hargrave freeze (Ep 8), the pod closing (Ep 10).

## Threads and their spacing
- **Nathaniel and Nixon:** lies in both directions (Ep 3); the first break when Nixon discovers Nathaniel hid the suspected Frost Killer (Ep 5); Nathaniel hears her account beside Alice (Ep 7) and confirms it to Nixon before Nixon leads the raid (Ep 8); the same side but not friends (Ep 9).
- **The imprints:** the nightmare cold open (Ep 2), Nathaniel's full memory (Ep 6), and the same night from the offspring's side, where Morgana tells the Lost One she sent her (Ep 9).
- **The sisters:** closeness (Eps 2–6); the Lost One tells Alice who she is, Alice stops Nathaniel restraining her but sends her away (Ep 7); Alice returns by choice, "Nine is the one where we get up" (Ep 8); the choice to stay (Ep 10).
- **The killer thread, one beat per episode.** This spacing is agreed:
  - Ep 1: Miriam's fall, a figure in the shadows, Jennie watching.
  - Ep 2: Josephine burns her gloves.
  - Ep 2 tag: Jennie sees Miriam alive at Alice’s door. Ep 3: she withholds the bridge account.
  - Ep 4: Grise tests "Miriam" with a Vykra hand-sign, sees it fail, and sells her location to Lambrick.
  - Ep 5: Alice reports privately to Josephine.
  - Ep 6: Josephine and Grise ("You were the one holding the knife"). Josephine pays to remove the witness. Grise tells Alice that Miriam drowned their stepfather.
  - Ep 7: Grise's man comes for Jennie, the Lost One saves her. Jennie names Josephine in Ep 8 after Alice returns.
  - Ep 6: Alice tells Nathaniel herself that his mother hired her.
  - Ep 8: Josephine supplies a lead for the arrest.

## Dartboard: pitched ideas, not committed
- Every episode opens on a clock card: **THE LIGHT HAS 49 DAYS**, counting down.
- At the Ep 1 luncheon the engagement is dropped over lunch, and the table stays calm when Jamie asks what would happen if the light went out. That calm is the aura at work. A telegram from Nixon in the east sits face down by Eldgard's plate.
- The gauntlet misfires because Nathaniel can't fix the part of the design he doesn't understand: the first crack in the imprint.
- The photograph in Ep 1 has a gap at Eldgard's right hand, left for Nixon.
- The aura is felt as "the quiet." Jamie feels nothing and lies that he does.
- Alistair's portrait has a painted-out child, the Hargrave survivor.
- Morgana's voice travels through the lamps; the light dims with her grief.
- Nathaniel's workshop is a converted bell-foundry in Clerkenwell. A cold-sink at the Lost One's wrist contains her frost.
- A Christmas crystal market set piece: the tree sings when the Lost One touches it.
- The Lost One's first kill is one of Ajax's men attacking Alice.
- Tamara cracks the Harding book in Ep 8 and gives Nixon the Hargrave pages.
- Marsh: "Hargrave chin," and "Ask the woman with the keys."
- Minor new characters: Peg the mudlark (a witness), Moss (Ajax's man).
