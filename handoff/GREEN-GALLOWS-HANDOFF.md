# The Green Gallows: Complete Handoff

The whole show as it stands on 19 November 2026: what's agreed, where we are, and how to work with the writer. If you're a new assistant picking this up, read all of it before you suggest anything.

---

## 1. How to work with this writer

- **Only what the writer writes, edits or explicitly finalizes is canon.** Your drafts and suggestions are never canon until the writer approves them.
- **Every idea has a status.** **Locked** means the writer agreed. **Pitched** means suggested, not committed. **Open** means undecided. Never treat a pitch as locked. The season is meant to keep growing, so new ideas are welcome, offered as pitches.
- **Don't interpret, ask.** The writer has been misread before and hates it. When something is unclear, ask one sharp question.
- **Don't skip steps.** Follow the process in section 2. Don't lock scene-level ideas until the level above them holds.
- **Ripple-check every change.** When anything changes, walk every main character: who knows, when, and what it costs them. Update the timeline and the who-knows-what table in the same pass.
- **Be a creative partner, not a yes-man.** Give honest opinions with a recommendation, and push back on clutter. Be confident and direct.
- **When writing prose:** detailed and sensory. Introduce characters head to toe. Spell out sounds (*krrk*, *whmmm*). Never end a scene on a question; end on action. The writer likes Brent Weeks's style: close third person, one point of view per section, dark humour, short hard lines in violence.
- **Space the reveals.** The writer's recurring note is that too much piles into one episode. Give each big hit an aftermath before the next: plot point, emotional reaction, emotional decision (the writer calls this PP-ER-ED).

## 2. Where we are

**Status:** the lore is locked. The ten-episode spine is provisional. **Episode 1 is the episode the writer is happy with,** and its scenes are on the writer's scene board. Several holes in the later episodes are open (section 14).

**The agreed process, in order:**
1. **The season in one sentence, then one paragraph:** setup, three disasters (around Eps 1, 5 and 8) and the ending. The writer writes or approves these in their own words. *This is the next step.*
2. **One page per main character:** want, obstacle, the lie they believe, what they realise. Do a whydunit pass on every antagonist: why do they do it, and how can they live with it?
3. **The character grid:** characters across ten episodes, one line per box. A first version exists (the thread grid, section 9).
4. **Break the season:** tentpoles first (the end of Ep 1, the Ep 5 midseason, Ep 8, Ep 10), then one change per episode.
5. **Break each episode:** every scene is goal, conflict, disaster; every disaster gets a reaction, a dilemma and a decision before the next hit.
6. **Scenes:** the writer writes them on the scene board, marks them Final, and the assistant files them word for word.

## 3. The show

**London, 1889. The whole city glows green. The light is a living being kept in a freezer, stolen by a family that broke its word, and it's dying.**

**The promise:** we follow Nathaniel Harding as he hunts his brother's killer, choosing the people in front of him over the family that made him, until he must decide whether London's light is worth a child in a freezer.

Under it: Alice Bounds learns her sister is dead from the thing that loves her like one, until she must let it choose.

**The audience's pleasure:** an investigation, a forbidden intimacy, and dread that keeps climbing.

**Theme:** protection as control. Every adult hurts someone to keep them safe. Underneath it: bought or given, who pays for the light, what gets written down, the spare and the heir, where you belong.

## 4. The secret and the history (locked)

**The secret:** the light is a living thing in a freezer, and the Hardings got it by breaking a promise.

- **c. 1780, the crash.** Alistair Harding, a failed inventor, finds a crashed ship in the woods by the Harding estate. He captures the being aboard, **Morgana**, and tortures her. She has a built-in universal translator: she speaks to anyone in their own language.
- **The deal.** Teach him the ship's power system and he'll let her and her offspring go. She teaches him.
- **The betrayal.** He hooks her into her own cryo-stasis pod and draws her life-source energy for months, then years. That's the first gallowglass, the green light. He writes every secret down in his own cipher: **the Harding book**.
- **The Hargrave massacre.** Hargrave is the village at the edge of the Harding woods, and its people hauled the wreck. Alistair's first full draw goes wrong and freezes the village in one night. Survivors who talked are called rebels and hanged at Tyburn, the last mass hanging before the gallows came down. One child survives, and Alistair takes them into the house out of guilt. Every generation since, the Hardings have kept a Hargrave close: guilt and surveillance, never separable. "My family looks after its own."
- **1783.** The Hardings buy the old Tyburn gallows field and build the Works over it. Hence *the Green Gallows*.
- **Two generations later,** London and the countryside run on green light, and the Hardings are the most powerful family in the city.
- **1873.** An experiment goes wrong and Morgana's offspring, which is life-source energy with no body, escapes. On its mother's instruction it visits both sons of the house. **Jamie** (nine) is imprinted with resistance to the Harding aura. **Nathaniel** (five) is imprinted with understanding of the ship's technology. Eldgard traps it in an **ornament** (a piece of the ship's containment) and mesmerises Nathaniel with a swinging lantern so the memory becomes a nightmare.

## 5. The rules (locked unless marked)

**The light**
1. Gallowglass is Morgana's life-source energy, drawn from her in the pod. There is only one source.
2. **She is dying.** The yield falls every year. That's the season's clock. When she dies the light dies, unless something takes her place. **Her offspring can,** and Eldgard means to put it in the pod.
3. Opening the pod frees her and ends the light.
4. In the east it's already happening: Nixon reports village after village frozen in ice, their heat and power gone. (*Open:* is this only the cold of a failing light, or something stranger?)
5. **Clock card:** every episode opens on THE LIGHT HAS 49 DAYS, counting down.

**The Harding aura** (an Alistair recipe built from Morgana's technology)
1. It works on Harding blood only. Josephine, Nixon and the servants are outside it.
2. It persuades rather than compels. It amplifies loyalty and dulls doubt, so **Eldgard's choices are still his own.**
3. Jamie's 1873 imprint made him immune.
4. (*Proposed, to confirm:* it's stronger the closer you are to the core and the longer you've been exposed. Nathaniel, shut out as the second son, has had the least, so being unwanted kept him free.)

**The offspring and the Lost One**
1. It has no body. Starved and half-mad after sixteen years in the ornament, **it ravaged Jamie when he freed it, and he died of his wounds.**
2. It can take a body at the moment of death. That's how it became the Lost One, in Miriam.
3. It carries what the dying hold most. It left Jamie carrying his thought of Miriam at the bridge. Miriam's last thought was Alice, which is why the Lost One goes to her.
4. Its cold kills by touch, and comes out when she feels too much or chooses to use it. It holds Miriam's body exactly as it was in the river.
5. Miriam's memories return one at a time, heaviest last, along with glimpses of what she really is: her mother's voice, a sky she's never seen.
6. Harding technology can contain her cold, which is why Nathaniel's workshop can hide her.

**Mesmerism** is real but small. It buries memories; it doesn't create them.

**Glim and the Weeping** (*carried over from the old version, re-review*): ground gallowglass is the undercity's drug, and users hear a woman crying under the hum.

## 6. Factions and places

- **House Harding:** keepers of the light and the secret. Harding House in Mayfair; the Works over Tyburn, with the core and the pod below.
- **The Lambricks, the arch-nemesis.** Coal. Hector wants London back on coal, then, once he learns what the light is, wants to own it. Tamara, his daughter, is the only one who can break Alistair's cipher. Ajax is the enforcer.
- **The Vykra:** a guild of undercity women, spies and knives for hire, run by Mother Grise from a Shadwell laundry. Their mark is a knot stitched inside the glove. They sell to every faction.
- **The Royal Lantern Guard:** London's elite, armed by the Works, hunting the Frost Killer. Oath: *Light the dark. Keep the flame.* (*Gear carried over from the old version, re-review.*)
- **The kept Hargraves:** not a faction, a family. Ada, Marsh and Nixon are what's left.
- **Places:** Harding House (study, servants' stair, long gallery), the Works (gauntlet shop, great hall, core), Nathaniel's workshop, Gin Lane and the East End, Blackwood Bridge, the Shadwell laundry, Lambrick House, Newgate, Parliament.
- **The ship:** never seen whole until the finale. Pieces of it live in the Works machinery; the ornament is one.

## 7. Characters

**Leads**

- **Nathaniel Harding** (21): second son, gauntlet engineer at the Works. Wants his father's respect; believes being useful is the same as being loved. His genius was planted in 1873 and he doesn't know it. **His mission is to find who killed his brother. The killer is the Lost One, and he's sheltering her.** Arc: hides the Frost Killer while hunting his brother's killer, then loses Nixon, Alice's trust and finally London's light. He opens the pod and puts out the light.
- **Alice Bounds** (20): glim-runner and Vykra novice from Gin Lane. Believes love means holding on, because nobody ever stood in front of her. Takes Josephine's money, through Grise, to get close to Nathaniel. Arc: learns what Miriam did (Ep 6); loses the sister illusion and learns who killed Miriam (Ep 7); confesses the contract to Nathaniel herself (Ep 8); spares Josephine (Ep 10); offers the Lost One the ship and lets her choose. The wound: the stepfather and his belt, the counting game ("eight posts and he's done"), "behind the eightfold fence."
- **The Lost One:** Morgana's offspring wearing Miriam's body. Wants to free her mother; believes love is a weakness of the body she wears. **She killed Jamie and doesn't remember it.** The memories return slowly, and at the midseason (Ep 5) she goes to Nathaniel in tears and holds him tight: "I'm so sorry. There's something I have to tell you…" In Ep 9 she learns her mother sent her to the boys in 1873, and wonders whether her love for Alice was a plan too. She stays. Lines: "I know you." "Then why do I have all her memories? Why do I love you so much?" "Eight posts, Alice. She counted them with you. She never once moved. I would have moved."
- **Nixon Hargrave** (23): youngest Lantern-Captain, the current kept Hargrave, raised by Ada below stairs on Harding money. **Absent in Ep 1** ("That's Nixon's chair, Nat"); **first appears in Ep 2,** back from the east with his report. Eldgard is the father who never was. He can't tell whether Eldgard loved him or kept him; the answer is both. Arc: hunts the Frost Killer for Eldgard; the first break with Nathaniel when he learns Nathaniel hid Jamie's killer (Ep 5); leads the raid that takes the Lost One, then reads the Hargrave pages (Ep 8); kills Ada in panic and breaks his uncle out of Newgate (Ep 9); holds the Guard for London, not the Hardings (Ep 10). In love with Tamara.

**Antagonists**

- **Eldgard Harding** (58): patriarch and keeper of the core. Believes the many outweigh his sons. The aura's most comfortable servant. Mesmerised Nathaniel in 1873, plans to put the child in the pod, and cuts the East End to keep the West lit. Confesses (Ep 9): "Did you love me, or did you keep me?" "Yes." Dies at Morgana's hand (Ep 10).
- **Josephine Harding** (50): matriarch, ex-Vykra, outside the aura, so everything she does she does fully awake. **She killed Miriam** on Blackwood Bridge, in person. She bought the time and place from Grise, went to stop the girl her son meant to run away with, and her old training took over: "I didn't go there to kill her. My hands did." Thirty years ago she escaped the Vykra by marrying a Harding, which was exactly Miriam's plan. She hired Alice to watch Nathaniel. The audience learns she's the killer in Ep 2 (she burns her gloves); Alice learns it at the end of Ep 7. Spared by Alice in Ep 10. Her fate after the finale is open.
- **Lord Hector Lambrick** (55): the coal king. Wants the light dead, then wants to own it. **Wins:** a dark London runs on his coal.

**Supporting**

- **Ada Hargrave** (about 60): head housekeeper. Keeps the cryo clean. Last believer in the Lamp Saint faith: the Hargrave dead became the light, and Morgana is "the Lady." Marsh's sister and Nixon's aunt; she raised him and calls him "love." She reported her brother. Ep 8: "Forgive them? Love, I forgave them before you were born. They took us in when they didn't have to. That's more than God did for Hargrave." **Death (Ep 9):** she goes to report Nixon's break-out plan. On the servants' stair he holds his hand over her mouth too long. "I'm saving you, love. We're not them. We were never going to be them. We're Hargraves." She dies still believing. Her death needs aftermath.
- **Tobias Marsh:** a Newgate prisoner held as a madman. The previous kept Hargrave who asked questions. Ada's brother and Nixon's uncle. Ep 4: "Hargrave chin." Freed by Nixon in Ep 9.
- **Tamara Lambrick** (19): codebreaker, engaged to Nathaniel in a merger on both sides. Wants to be her father's heir. Cracks the Harding book and gives Nixon the Hargrave pages (Ep 8). Opens the coal depots to the East for nothing (Ep 10).
- **Mother Grise** (70): runs the Vykra. Sells to everyone, loves no one. Took Miriam's entry price (their stepfather), sold Josephine the rendezvous, and gave Alice the contract. Tests "Miriam" with a hand-sign (Ep 4). Takes Josephine's money to remove the witness and sends a man after her own best girl.
- **Jennie** (19): Grise's best knife. On the Narrows raid with Miriam in Ep 1, where Alice joins uninvited and a bond and rivalry with Alice begins. **The only witness on the bridge.** The one person who knows the face is wrong (Ep 3). The Lost One saves her life (Ep 7), and the guilt breaks her: she tells Alice, "Miriam was the best of us."
- **Miriam Bounds** (24): Alice's older sister, a Vykra runner, Jamie's lover. At sixteen she drowned their stepfather to join the Vykra. She was quitting to run with Jamie and come back for Alice. Killed by Josephine; her last thought is Alice.
- **Jamie Harding** (25): heir. Immune to the aura. Initiated and shown the core; Morgana asks him, "Where is my child?" He frees the child, not the mother. **Dies in Ep 1.**
- **Byron** (64): butler. Tells Nathaniel what the light is in Ep 6 ("You were always enough"), and dies helping the freezing East End. His secret and his Ep 5 role are open.
- **Ajax Fink** (35): Lambrick's enforcer. Alice cuts his face in Ep 1. Hunts the Frost Killer.
- **The Works' chief engineer:** unnamed. Nathaniel's mentor. Sells the yield figures and the secret to Lambrick.
- **Morgana:** the being in the pod. A victim with every right to her rage. Sent her child to imprint the boys. Freed in Ep 10, she kills Eldgard, boards the ship and vows revenge.
- **Alistair Harding:** the founder, seen only in cold opens.
- **The Duke:** brought back by the writer for Ep 2's funeral. *Who he is is open.*

## 8. The season

**The five changes**
1. **Ep 1. The secret gets out:** the child escapes, Jamie dies, Miriam dies.
2. **Ep 3. Nathaniel takes a side without knowing it:** he shelters the Frost Killer.
3. **Ep 5, the midseason. The killer is in the house:** the Lost One confesses she killed Jamie, and Nixon learns Nathaniel hid her.
4. **Eps 6–7. The light kills again:** the East End is cut off and freezes, and every love Alice has turns out to have been bought.
5. **Ep 8. The child is taken:** everyone picks a side. Eps 9–10 pay it off, with no new mythology after Ep 8.
