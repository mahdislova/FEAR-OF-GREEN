# The Green Gallows: Story Bible (locked)

Everything here was agreed by the writer. Anything not here is pitched or open (see `03`).

## The secret
The light is a living thing in a freezer, and the Hardings got it by breaking a promise.

## History
- **c. 1780, the crash.** Alistair Harding, a failed inventor, finds a crashed ship in the woods by the Harding estate. He captures the being aboard, **Morgana**, and tortures her. She has a built-in universal translator: she speaks to anyone in their own language.
- **The deal.** Teach him the ship's power system and he'll let her and her offspring go. She teaches him.
- **The betrayal.** He hooks her into her own cryo-stasis pod and draws her life-source energy for months, then years. That's the first gallowglass, the green light. He writes every secret down for the family.
- **The Hargrave massacre.** Hargrave is the village at the edge of the Harding woods, and its people hauled the wreck. Alistair's first full draw goes wrong and freezes the village in one night. Survivors who talked were called rebels and hanged at Tyburn, the last mass hanging before the gallows came down. A cover-up. One child survived, and Alistair took them in out of guilt. Every generation since, the Hardings have kept a Hargrave close. Guilt and surveillance, never separable.
- **1783.** The Hardings buy the old Tyburn gallows field and build the Works over it. Hence *the Green Gallows*.
- **Two generations later.** London and the countryside run on green light. The Hardings are the most powerful family in the city.
- **1873.** An experiment goes wrong and Morgana's offspring escapes the cryo underground. It's life-source energy with no body. It visits both Harding sons:
  - **Jamie** (nine): imprinted with resistance to the Harding aura.
  - **Nathaniel** (five): imprinted with understanding of the ship's technology.

  Eldgard traps it in an **ornament** and mesmerises Nathaniel with a swinging lantern so the memory becomes a dream.

## Rules
- **The light is dying.** Morgana has been drained for about a century and is failing. That's the season's clock, and it replaces the old Tithe.
- **The Lost One is the replacement.** Eldgard means to put the offspring in the pod when the mother dies.
- **The Harding aura.** An Alistair recipe that keeps Hardings from betraying the secret. It persuades rather than compels: it amplifies loyalty and dulls doubt, so Eldgard's choices are still his own. It works on Harding blood only, so Josephine and Nixon are outside it. Jamie's imprint made him immune. The finer rules (proximity, exposure) were proposed but should be confirmed.
- **The offspring has no body.** It can take a body at the moment of death.

## The night of Episode 1
- **Jamie** is shown the core at his heir's initiation. Morgana asks him, "Where is my child?" He breaks the ornament to free the child, not the mother. The starving offspring ravages him and leaves through the window carrying his strongest thought: Miriam waiting at the bridge.
- **Jamie dies.** The offspring, starved and half-mad, ravages him as it escapes, and he dies of his wounds. Episode 1 closes on Miriam drowning, intercut with Nathaniel coming into the study where Jamie is bleeding out.
- **The Lost One killed Jamie and doesn't remember it.** Her memories return slowly. When she understands, she goes to Nathaniel in tears and holds him tight: "I'm so sorry. There's something I have to tell you…" This is the midseason (Ep 5). The same episode, Nixon learns Nathaniel has been hiding his brother's killer: their first break.
- **Miriam Bounds** dies at Blackwood Bridge. On screen we see only her fall and a figure in the shadows backing away. The offspring reaches her as she dies and takes her body; her last thought is Alice. That's **the Lost One**.
- **Nathaniel's day:** Eldgard moves him out of Nixon's empty chair at lunch ("That's Nixon's chair, Nat"). At the Exhibition he demonstrates his gauntlet to impress a visiting professor and it misfires, humiliating him in Eldgard's eyes. Jamie finds him on the roof for a quiet brothers' moment ("Never lose the light within you"), then goes to free the child.
- **Nixon isn't in Episode 1.** He first appears in Ep 2, back from the east to report village after village frozen in ice, their heat and power source faded.
- **Jennie** (Vykra) rides the Narrows warehouse raid with Miriam that day, and Alice joins uninvited. That starts the bond and the rivalry between Jennie and Alice. Jennie witnesses the bridge.

## Who killed Miriam
**Josephine Harding**, ex-Vykra, in person. She went to stop the girl her son meant to run away with, and her old training took over. "I didn't go there to kill her. My hands did." Thirty years ago she escaped the Vykra by marrying a Harding, which was Miriam's exact plan.
- The audience learns it in **Episode 2**, when she burns her gloves and the Vykra knot is stitched inside. Played as dread, not a twist. (The writer asked the previous assistant to choose this timing.)
- Alice learns it at the **end of Episode 7.** The Lost One saves Jennie's life, and the guilt breaks Jennie: "Miriam was the best of us."
- Josephine hired Alice, through Grise, to get close to Nathaniel.

## Main characters
- **Nathaniel Harding** (21), second son and gauntlet engineer. His mission is to find who killed his brother. The killer is the Lost One, whom he's sheltering. He shelters the sisters in his workshop. A season-long will-they-won't-they with Alice. Learns the light is Morgana in Ep 6.
- **Alice Bounds** (20), glim-runner. Spies on Nathaniel for the Vykra, paid by Josephine. Hunting her sister's killer.
- **The Lost One:** the offspring in Miriam's body, carrying Miriam's memories, guilt and love, plus glimpses of what she really is. Alice pushes her away: "You're not her. You're not my sister." The Lost One: "Then why do I have all her memories? Why do I love you so much?" She learns humanity and chooses to stay with Alice.
- **Nixon Hargrave** (23), Lantern-Captain and the current kept Hargrave. Eldgard raised him: the father who never was. Learning the Hargrave truth breaks him and strains his friendship with Nathaniel. Learns the light is Morgana in Ep 8, from the Harding book.
- **Eldgard Harding** (58): keeper of the core.
- **Josephine Harding** (50): ex-Vykra, Miriam's killer.
- **Ada Hargrave** (about 60), head housekeeper and supporting role. Keeps the cryo clean. Last believer in the Lamp Saint faith: the Hargrave dead became the light, and Morgana is "the Lady." Marsh's sister and Nixon's aunt; she raised him. She reported her brother. Line: "Forgive them? Love, I forgave them before you were born. They took us in when they didn't have to. That's more than God did for Hargrave." **Death:** she goes to report Nixon's break-out plan. On the servants' stair Nixon holds his hand over her mouth too long. "I'm saving you, love. We're not them. We were never going to be them. We're Hargraves." She dies still believing. Seed her from Ep 1; her death needs aftermath.
- **Tobias Marsh:** a Newgate prisoner held as a madman. The previous kept Hargrave who asked questions. Whispers the true Hargrave history. Ada's brother, Nixon's uncle.
- **The Lambricks, the arch-nemesis.** Hector is the coal king. Tamara, his daughter and a codebreaker, is engaged to Nathaniel and is the only one who can read Alistair's cipher. Ajax is the enforcer.
- **The Vykra:** a spy and knife guild run by Mother Grise. Jennie is her best girl (the old twins merged into one).
- **The Works' chief engineer:** a new character. Not Harding blood, Nathaniel's mentor, sells the secret to Lambrick. Name not set.
- **Byron:** the Harding butler.

## The ending
- Nathaniel frees Morgana and the light ends.
- Morgana is too weak to fight on. She boards a ship that comes for her, it does substantial damage to London and retreats, and she vows revenge. That's Season 2's dread.
- **Lambrick wins:** London is dark and runs on his coal.
- **Final image:** the lone ship in the sky. The Lost One looks up at it, then back at Alice, and stays.
