The secret

The light is a living thing in a freezer.

The light is dying. Morgana has been drained for over a century and is failing. The Works' engineers watch the yield fall; the lamps flicker; London doesn't know why. When she dies, the Hardings mean to put her child in the pod in her place.

In the east it's already happening. Nixon reports (Ep 2) village after village frozen in ice, their heat and power source faded. Open: is this only the cold of a failing light, or something stranger?

Clock card: every episode opens on THE LIGHT HAS 49 DAYS (counting down).

Episode 1

The Exhibition

Change: the secret gets out

POV: Nathaniel and Jamie

Cold open: c. 1780. The crash in the Harding woods. Alistair's lantern finds a hand in the wreck.

  • Jamie's initiation, days earlier: the stair, the vault, Morgana in the pod. "Where is my child?"
  • The luncheon chair. Nathaniel sits in the empty chair at his father's right; Eldgard moves him: "That's Nixon's chair, Nat." Nixon isn't in the episode. Eldgard has sent him east to see the failures for himself.
  • The gauntlet failure. At the Exhibition at the Works, Nathaniel demonstrates his gauntlet prototype to impress a visiting professor. It misfires in front of the crowd and humiliates him in Eldgard's eyes.
  • The roof. Jamie finds him on the roof: a quiet brother-to-brother moment. "Never lose the light within you." Then Jamie goes to free the child.
  • Ada polishing lamps (seed her from Ep 1).
  • Cross-cut: Jamie at Harding House breaks the ornament; the offspring, starved and half-mad, ravages him and goes out of the window. Miriam waits at the bridge. We see only her fall, and a figure in the shadows backing away (Josephine, unrevealed). Jennie watches from the stair. The river.
  • Closing image: Miriam drowning, intercut with Nathaniel coming into the study where Jamie is bleeding out.

Episode 4

The Hearing

Change: the light's failure goes public

POV: Lambrick, Tamara, Nathaniel

Cold open: The deal. Morgana teaches; Alistair: "You have my word."

  • Lambrick attacks the Harding Charter in Parliament with yield figures smuggled out by the Works' chief engineer. The lamps flicker in the chamber on cue.
  • Tamara properly introduced: the codebreaker, the engagement as a merger on both sides.
  • Nixon visits Marsh in Newgate. The madman pinches his chin and whispers Hargrave. Nixon walks away not understanding.
  • Nathaniel and Alice trace the shard to the Works. The romance warms; Alice reports to Josephine and hates it.
  • Grise tests "Miriam". Jennie fetches her to the laundry. Grise gives a Vykra hand-sign the real Miriam would answer without thinking. The Lost One doesn't. Grise smiles: it isn't Miriam, and it's worth more than anything she's ever sold. She sells the Frost Killer's whereabouts to Lambrick.
  • Tag: Eldgard to Josephine: when the mother dies, they use the child. The audience knows the stakes before the characters do.

Episode 7

The Girl Who Isn't

Change: every love Alice has turns out to have been bought

POV: Alice

Cold open: The Bounds girls under the bed, counting. A belt buckle.

  • Act One. The frozen East End: bodies in doorways, Byron, who went to help, among them. The Hardings blame the Frost Killer; mobs form. The massacre repeats, smaller. In the riot, Grise's man comes for Jennie. The Lost One saves her. Jennie can't look at her.
  • The middle. Alice brings home what Grise told her. The Lost One confirms it from Miriam's memory and confesses on Miriam's behalf, which is how Alice knows. "You're not her." "Why do I love you so much?" Alice walks out.
  • B story. Lambrick's coal is the only heat left. Eldgard's terms: Nathaniel marries Tamara, the East gets coal. Nathaniel: "Tell my father yes."
  • End of episode. Jennie finds Alice alone. The thing wearing the face of the woman she watched die has saved her, and Grise sold her: guilt and betrayal at once. She tells Alice: Josephine killed Miriam. "Miriam was the best of us."
