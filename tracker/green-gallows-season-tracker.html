Season Tracker

We follow Nathaniel Harding as he hunts his brother's killer, choosing the people in front of him over the family that made him, until he must decide whether London's light is worth a child in a freezer.

Under it: Alice Bounds learns her sister is dead from the thing that loves her like one, until she must let it choose.

The audience's pleasure: an investigation, a forbidden intimacy, and dread that keeps climbing.

Theme: protection as control. Every adult hurts someone to keep them safe. Underneath it: bought or given, who pays for the light, what gets written down, the spare and the heir, where you belong.

The secret

The light is a living thing in a freezer.

The light is dying. Morgana has been drained for over a century and is failing. The Works' engineers watch the yield fall; the lamps flicker; London doesn't know why. When she dies, the Hardings mean to put her child in the pod in her place.

In the east it's already happening. Nixon reports (Ep 2) village after village frozen in ice, their heat and power source faded. Open: is this only the cold of a failing light, or something stranger?

Clock card: every episode opens on THE LIGHT HAS 49 DAYS (counting down).

The Exhibition

Change: the secret gets out

POV: Nathaniel and Jamie

Cold open: c. 1780. The crash in the Harding woods. Alistair's lantern finds a hand in the wreck.

  • Jamie's initiation, days earlier: the stair, the vault, Morgana in the pod. "Where is my child?"
  • The luncheon chair. Nathaniel sits in the empty chair at his father's right; Eldgard moves him: "That's Nixon's chair, Nat." Nixon isn't in the episode. Eldgard has sent him east to see the failures for himself.
  • The gauntlet failure. At the Exhibition at the Works, Nathaniel demonstrates his gauntlet prototype to impress a visiting professor. It misfires in front of the crowd and humiliates him in Eldgard's eyes.
  • The roof. Jamie finds him on the roof: a quiet brother-to-brother moment. "Never lose the light within you." Then Jamie goes to free the child.
  • Ada polishing lamps (seed her from Ep 1).
  • On the Narrows raid Miriam steals a book from the Lambrick consignment and hands it to Grise. There is more to the cargo than the Lambricks want to admit.
  • Cross-cut: Jamie at Harding House breaks the ornament; the offspring, starved and half-mad, ravages him and goes out of the window. Miriam waits at the bridge. We see only her fall, and a figure in the shadows backing away (Josephine, unrevealed). Jennie watches from the stair. The river.
  • Closing image: Miriam drowning, intercut with Nathaniel coming into the study where Jamie is bleeding out.
Episode 2

The Wakescenes in progress

Change: With Miriam missing, Alice takes the oath her sister always opposed. Hours later she finds her and has to earn her trust. Nathaniel starts an investigation by lying to the friend in charge of it

POV: Alice

Cold open: Five-year-old Nathaniel gets out of bed. Something slips into his room. He sees eyes as limitless as the sky. A shriek wakes him.

  • On the day of Jamie’s funeral the Lost One comes out of the river mute. Alice has spent the intervening days looking for Miriam.
  • Grise, who knows Miriam is dead, tells Alice her sister ran off and left her, and offers her the knot. Jennie watches Alice swear at the laundry. Grise tells Alice to collect her gloves in the morning.
  • Letters from the darkened east interrupt the convoy. The Duke honours Jamie; the Lanterns lower their crystal lights. Nixon returns to Nathaniel under the mended umbrella. At the wake the Duke privately asks Hector how much coal his sister’s house east of the line will need. Hector sends him to another merchant and learns from the hour of the question that the Duke expects the light to fail. Tamara writes the time on the back of the Narrows tally. Hector sends Ajax to find out what was taken.
  • Two children and a frightened gang give Alice a trail. Miriam’s fighting reflex survives in a body the Lost One is still learning to use.
  • Alice brings her home. The girl’s skin stays cold long after she is dry; Alice blames the river. Ren offers a provisional explanation for the silence and memory loss. The girl chooses Alice for company.
  • Ada feeds Nixon at the wake: “love.” Nathaniel keeps the shard. He watches Eldgard give Nixon the keys, then lies to his friend, knowing the account goes to his father. Tamara and Nixon steal a minute together at the wake.
  • Josephine burns her Vykra gloves while Grise stitches the knot into Alice’s. Josephine hires Grise to put someone close to Nathaniel; Grise chooses Alice and sends Jennie with the gloves. Jennie sees Miriam’s face.
  • Tag: Alice reaches for her gloves. Jennie doesn’t let go.

Episode 4

The Hearing

Change: the light's failure goes public

POV: Lambrick, Tamara, Nathaniel

Cold open: The deal. Morgana teaches; Alistair: "You have my word."

  • Lambrick attacks the Harding Charter in Parliament with yield figures smuggled out by the Works' chief engineer. The lamps flicker in the chamber on cue.
  • Tamara at the hearing: the codebreaker, the engagement as a merger on both sides.
  • The prison train climbs to Newgate, its cell galleries cut into a mountain face above the freight line. From the glazed transfer tower Nixon sees relief guards glide down to the valley on folding patrol wings. The moving stair sticks on the way to Marsh’s landing. Every prisoner lifts a foot at the same instant. Nixon visits Marsh in Newgate. The madman pinches his chin and whispers Hargrave. Nixon walks away not understanding. When he mentions the encounter to Ada, she asks if Marsh has his spectacles. Nixon tries to learn how she knows him. She wraps the glasses and explains that one arm is loose. Nixon must ask for the cell to be opened and give them to Marsh himself. He agrees. Ada asks whether he is coming on Sunday. He asks if she still keeps the crisp end of the joint for him. She tells him he can cut it himself this time. He promises to come. Ada has found a way to care for her brother in the very prison she helped put him in.
  • Nathaniel and Alice match the shard to a Works drawing. Jamie could have opened the ornament himself. The Guard discounted that possibility on the strength of Nathaniel's statement. His silence now protects his own lie. At the workshop Nathaniel shows Miriam the exterior drawing and asks whether Jamie showed her the ornament. She asks for time. He leaves the drawing with her. The romance warms; Alice reports to Josephine and hates it. As they talk Alice fastens the pearl buttons at Josephine's wrist. Both treat it as an ordinary kindness.
  • Grise tests "Miriam". Jennie fetches her to the laundry. Grise gives a Vykra hand-sign the real Miriam would answer without thinking. The Lost One doesn't. Grise smiles: it isn't Miriam, and it's worth more than anything she's ever sold. She sells the Frost Killer's whereabouts to Lambrick.
  • Tag: Eldgard to Josephine: when the mother dies, they use the child. The audience knows the stakes before the characters do.

Episode 7

The Girl Who Isn't

Change: Alice learns Miriam died, and she and Nathaniel learn together what the Lost One did to Jamie. Alice stops Nathaniel from taking her, then cannot yet stay with her

POV: Alice

Cold open: The Bounds girls under the bed, counting. A belt buckle.

  • Act One. The East End is freezing. At Gin Lane bodies lie in the torn-open houses, across the road and on the washhouse racks. The shell around Byron flakes away. He recognizes Nell by her bare feet and finds the old man’s tin against his knee. A relief clerk asks him for the names of the dead. The Harding butler knows just one. He spends the morning finding people who can give him the others. When the Hardings blame the Frost Killer, he gives the survivors his own name as a witness: the green came up through the floor before the girl arrived. The Hardings blame the Frost Killer; mobs form. The massacre repeats, smaller. In the riot, Grise's man comes for Jennie. The crowd carries the Lost One clear of the danger. She looks back and sees Jennie pinned where the crowd has left her. Stay with Jennie long enough for her to see the girl reach safety, stop, and come back for her. Jennie watched Miriam go over the rail. Now she has to take that same hand through a fold of coat and let herself be pulled free. Jennie can't look at her.
  • The middle: the shared reveal. Alice brings home what Grise told her about their stepfather. The Lost One confirms it from Miriam's memory, then tells Alice the truth: Miriam died at the bridge; the person before her entered Miriam's body and carries her memories. "You're not her." "Why do I love you so much?" Nathaniel arrives before Alice can absorb it. The Lost One's final memory of the study resolves, and she tells them both that she ravaged Jamie when he freed her from the ornament. She owns the act without claiming that she intended his death. Nathaniel, shocked and furious, moves to restrain her with his technology. His grief gives him a defensible reason to take Jamie's killer; Alice sees the danger of putting her into another box. Alice stops him and makes a way for the Lost One to escape. She does not forgive her or follow her: she sends her away and stays with the loss. The Lost One does not use her cold against either of them. Nathaniel and Alice are left divided by the choice she made in front of him.
  • B story. Lambrick's coal is the only heat left. Eldgard's terms: Nathaniel marries Tamara, the East gets coal. Nathaniel: "Tell my father yes." His anger at the Lost One and his decision to buy heat for the East both remain live going into the wedding.
  • End on the separation. Alice has given the Lost One a way out. Alice directs her to Jennie, whose life she saved. Jennie shelters her for the night outside Grise’s control. Alice stays behind.
