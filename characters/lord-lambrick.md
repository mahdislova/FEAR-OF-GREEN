# Lord Augustus Lambrick

**Age:** 74. **Role:** supporting. Founder of the Lambrick coal empire, father of Hector and Tamara. Dying.
**Look:** to be written. A big man gone to bone in a wheeled chair by the fire, blanket over his knees, a rattle in his chest he calls "the seams": coal dust, forty years of it. His hands are still black in the creases.

- **Wants:** the empire to outlive him, in the hands that will keep it.
- **The lie he believes:** tradition is safety. The firstborn son is the heir because men follow sons, and an empire is only the men who follow it.
- **The question he keeps postponing:** whom to name heir. Hector is the eldest, the son, the one the trade expects. Tamara is the late child of his second marriage and the better mind, and he knows it. He never says it to her face. That silence is what she has spent her life trying to break.
- **How he judges:** by asking innocent questions in front of both of them. Every scene with him is a confrontation disguised as a conversation.

**Track**
- **Ep 2:** offscreen. Eldgard at the tea urn: "Your father has a clever daughter. Does he know?" "He knows my price." "That isn't the same thing."
- **Ep 3, the names:** Hector reports the Gin Lane widows. Lord Lambrick asks Tamara to read him the names, and she does, from memory. "You paid them. She remembered them. Which will they remember?"
- **Ep 4, breakfast:** watches Tamara break the cipher in three lines, then hands the figures to Hector. "Your sister found them. You'll say them."
- **Ep 6, two books:** "Your sister has a chair at the Works." "I put her in it." "Did you."
- **Ep 7, the sickbed:** makes Hector hand Tamara the delivery orders himself. Then, alone with her: "Hector will have it. He's the one they'll follow. You'll be the one who keeps him alive."
- **Ep 8:** too ill to walk her in. Hector gives his sister away; Lord Lambrick hears her answer from his chair at the front.
- **Ep 9:** hears what the Duke did at the Works gate, and sends for his lawyer, and for Tamara, alone, tonight. The message is what makes Hector order the hit.
- **Ep 10, the coronation:** at dawn, with his son dead in Gin Lane, he names Tamara heir. He asks one question about Gin Lane and accepts her answer. He chooses the child who survived. "Don't make me regret the one who lived."

**Season 2:** alive, barely, and watching the daughter he crowned. He suspects. He'll never ask.
