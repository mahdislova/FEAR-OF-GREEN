# Ajax Fink

**Age:** 35. **Role:** supporting. Lambrick's enforcer, ex-Guardsman drummed out of the corps.
**Look:** to be written.

- Funny, patient and terrifying.
- Hunts the Frost Killer for Lambrick, who has worked out the creature is worth more than any coal seam.
- Old-bible Black Gauntlet (Nathaniel's stolen prototype): carried over, not re-reviewed.
- **Loyal to his men, not to Hector.** Ep 3: two of the widows Hector pays are his men's; Tamara writes their names and asks how to spell them, which Hector never did. Ep 7: he takes the first coal cart east on her orders.
- **Ep 9, the price:** Hector orders him to kill Tamara. He takes the order, tells her, and brings Hector to the Gin Lane crater for "proof". "Everyone has a price, sir. Sorry."
- **Ep 10:** answers for her when the Lambrick clerks ask whose orders they follow. "Hers." Season 2: Tamara's knife.
