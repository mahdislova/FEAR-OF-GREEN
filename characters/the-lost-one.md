# The Lost One

**Role:** lead. Morgana's offspring, wearing Miriam's body.
**Look:** Miriam as she was in the river, held exactly so by the cold. Frost at the cuffs and lips when she feels too much.

- **Wants:** to free her mother.
- **The lie she believes:** love is a weakness of the body she wears.
- **Secret:** she lets Alice believe she's Miriam. She killed Jamie Harding escaping the ornament and doesn't remember it. The memories return slowly. Ep 5 brings an incomplete fragment of Jamie's death, not a confession. In Ep 7 she tells Alice that Miriam died and she entered her body; when Nathaniel arrives, she remembers the study clearly enough to tell them both that she ravaged Jamie while escaping. She owns the act without claiming to have intended his death. Alice prevents Nathaniel from restraining her, then sends her away. Alice returns in Ep 8 by choice.
- **What she doesn't know:** in 1873 she imprinted both Harding boys, on her mother's instruction (revealed Ep 9).

**Two griefs in one body.** Miriam's memories and guilt, returning one at a time, heaviest last. And glimpses of what she is: her mother's voice, a sky she has never seen.

**Arc ladder.** Wants to free her mother → wears Miriam and lets Alice believe → admits her identity and Jamie's death in Ep 7, loses Alice for a night, and receives her chosen return in Ep 8 → learns she was a tool too: her mother sent her → postpones choosing between her mother and Alice → stays.

**Key lines.** "I know you." (to Nathaniel, Ep 3). "Then why do I have her? Why do I love you so much?" "Eight posts, Alice. She counted them with you. She never once moved. I would have moved." (Ep 7)
