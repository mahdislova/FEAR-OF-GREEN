# Eldgard Harding

**Age:** 58. **Role:** antagonist-father. Patriarch, Keeper of the core. Alistair's grandson.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Wants:** to keep London warm.
- **The lie he believes:** the many outweigh my sons.
- **Secrets:** Jamie is alive in the locked room. In 1873 he mesmerised Nathaniel. When Morgana dies, he means to put her child in the pod. He orders the East End cut (Ep 6).
- **The aura:** its most loyal and most comfortable servant. It made choosing comfortable; it didn't choose for him.

**With Nixon:** the father who never was. Raised him, taught him, pinned the lantern on him, and kept a Hargrave where he could see him. "Did you love me, or did you keep me?" "Yes."

**End.** Confesses (Ep 9). Dies at Morgana's hand when she's freed (Ep 10).
