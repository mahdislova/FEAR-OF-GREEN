# The Works' chief engineer

**Name:** Silas Pike (provisional). **Role:** supporting. New character.
**Look:** spare and grey, pince-nez on a ribbon, a leather apron over a good suit worn shiny at the cuffs, the burn-scarred hands of a man who came up from the furnace floor. 50s.

- Not Harding blood: outside the aura.
- Nathaniel's mentor; the man who kept him out of "the work," for reasons Nathaniel always took personally.
- Sees the yield numbers every day. Knows the light is dying, and has decided the Hardings will go down with it.
- Sells the figures, then the secret, to Lambrick: the Lambricks' door into the core.
- Right about the light, wrong about everything else.
- **Ep 1 tag:** alone in the control room, he hears the light speak: "My child is out."
- **Ep 2:** starts a second ledger. **Ep 3:** Ajax buys him. **Ep 4:** Eldgard asks him to find the traitor, meaning himself.
