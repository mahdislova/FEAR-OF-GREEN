# Tamara Lambrick

**Age:** 19. **Role:** major supporting. Hector Lambrick's daughter, codebreaker, engaged to Nathaniel.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Wants:** to be her father's heir, not his bargaining chip.
- **The lie she believes:** if she proves her worth, her father will finally see her.
- **Secret:** already in a secret relationship with Nixon. In Ep 3 she asks for time to speak to Hector. In Ep 5 Nixon gives her another day. She uses her private talk with Hector to bargain for a seat at the Works meeting and announces the engagement herself. Nixon sees Hector draw up her chair. At the meeting she learns what cutting the East would buy and asks when its light would come back. Nobody answers.
- **Ep 3, the ledger:** holds her father's ledger while he pays the widows and learns what grief buys.
- **Why she matters:** the only person who can break Alistair's cipher. Whoever has Tamara and the Harding book has the secret. The engagement is a merger on both sides: each father thinks he's buying the other's secrets.

**The engine.** She wants a seat nobody can take back: not her father's approval, his position. The lie: if she proves her worth, they'll make room. The truth she learns: nobody makes room; you take it, and it costs you everyone who loved you before you had it. Something personal: no man with power has ever *seen* her, only priced her. Warm under the shell, with Nixon only. A Cersei who is often right: heat for the East, warning the washhouse, telling Nathaniel where the prisoner is.

**Her four relationships**
- **Nixon:** lovers across a divide. Every time she chooses power she gives him something he needs in the same breath: the relined coat (Ep 3), the warning about the journal (Ep 5), the coat handed back (Ep 7), the Hargrave pages (Ep 8), covering Ada's death (Ep 9), the coat across the street (Ep 10).
- **Eldgard:** the first man who sees her (Ep 2), raises his glass to her alone (Ep 5), and gives her Alistair's book to decode the instructions for putting the child in the pod (Ep 6). She helps build the cage.
- **Nathaniel:** rivals for one father's regard (Ep 5); they promise honesty before the service. At breakfast, when the Works transfer comes out, she stays the bride and says nothing about the pod or the beacon (Ep 8).
- **Hector:** the father she overthrows, and then kills. Her Ep 7 deal puts the East's coal on her orders, and she glimpses the Gin Lane audit in his drawer. In Ep 9 the Duke hands her the Crown's coal; Hector orders Ajax to kill her; Ajax sells him to her, and she kills him in the Gin Lane crater: "I would have lived in peace, Father. You brought war to my doorstep." In Ep 10 she opens every depot under her own name, and London blames the East.

**Season 2:** she holds the Harding name, the East's coal and Alistair's book; she knew about the beacon; she holds Nixon's worst act, and he doesn't know hers; Ajax is her knife. The lie (prove your worth and he'll see you) pays off in the worst way: he saw her, and ordered her dead. Full scenes live in each episode's "Tamara's track".
