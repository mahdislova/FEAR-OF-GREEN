# Tamara Lambrick

**Age:** 19. **Role:** major supporting. Hector Lambrick's daughter, codebreaker, engaged to Nathaniel.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Wants:** to be her father's heir, not his bargaining chip.
- **The lie she believes:** if she proves her worth, her father will finally see her.
- **Secret:** already in a secret relationship with Nixon. In Ep 3 she asks for time to speak to Hector. In Ep 5 Nixon gives her another day. She uses her private talk with Hector to bargain for a seat at the Works meeting and announces the engagement herself. Nixon sees Hector draw up her chair. At the meeting she learns what cutting the East would buy and asks when its light would come back. Nobody answers.
- **Ep 3, the ledger:** holds her father's ledger while he pays the widows and learns the method. In Ep 10 she turns it against him: the depots opened for nothing, the same gesture without the price.
- **Why she matters:** the only person who can break Alistair's cipher. Whoever has Tamara and the Harding book has the secret. The engagement is a merger on both sides: each father thinks he's buying the other's secrets.

**The engine.** She wants a seat nobody can take back: not her father's approval, his position. The lie: if she proves her worth, they'll make room. The truth she learns: nobody makes room; you take it, and it costs you everyone who loved you before you had it. Something personal: no man with power has ever *seen* her, only priced her. Warm under the shell, with Nixon only. A Cersei who is often right: heat for the East, warning the washhouse, freeing the prisoner in the pod.

**Her four relationships**
- **Nixon:** lovers across a divide. Every time she chooses power, she gives him something he needs in the same breath: the gift is the confession. (*Come home* in Ep 1; the relined coat in Ep 3; the warning about the journal in Ep 5; carrying the dead in Ep 6; the coat handed back in Ep 7; the Hargrave pages in Ep 8; covering Ada's death in Ep 9; the coat across the street in Ep 10.)
- **Eldgard:** the first man who sees her, and grooms her as the heir his sons weren't. The most dangerous relationship she has, because it feels like love. In Ep 9 he gives her the beacon page.
- **Nathaniel:** rivals for one father's regard (Ep 5), allies by an honest pact (Ep 7), betrayed when she withholds the beacon (Ep 9).
- **Hector:** the father she overthrows with his own methods (Ep 10).

**Her moves:** reads the misfire (Ep 1); seen by Eldgard (Ep 2); the widows' list (Ep 3); sells her father's leaker to Eldgard for the Harding library (Ep 4); the conservatory confession (Ep 5); right about the branch, too late (Ep 6); writes her own marriage (Ep 7); the pages, the word at the altar, the ask Nixon refuses (Ep 8); covers Ada's death, withholds the beacon (Ep 9); the coup by the list (Ep 10). Full scenes live in each episode's "Tamara's track".

**Season 2:** she holds both houses and Alistair's book; she knew about the beacon; she holds Nixon's worst act; Hector is her first enemy.
