# Mother Grise

**Age:** 70. **Role:** supporting. Runs the Vykra from the Shadwell laundry.
**Look:** to be written.

- Sells to everyone and loves no one.
- Took Miriam's entry price (the stepfather) at sixteen. Took Josephine's money to have Miriam "stopped." Gave Alice the Nathaniel contract, paid by Josephine.
- **Ep 4:** tests "Miriam" with a Vykra hand-sign; the Lost One fails it. Grise now knows it isn't Miriam, tells no one, and sells the Frost Killer's whereabouts to Lambrick.
- **Ep 6:** the two-hander with Josephine ("You were the one holding the knife"). Takes Josephine's money to remove the witness. Tells Alice what Miriam paid to join, as cruelty.
- **Ep 7:** sends a man after Jennie, her own best girl.
