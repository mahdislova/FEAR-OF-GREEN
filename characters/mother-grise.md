# Mother Grise

**Age:** 70. **Role:** supporting. Runs the Vykra from the Shadwell laundry.
**Look:** tiny and immaculate in dove-grey, a lace collar pinned with a mourning brooch, white hair in a coronet, little round spectacles, hands softened by sixty years of other people's laundry. Looks like everyone's grandmother.

- Sells to everyone and loves no one.
- Took Miriam's entry price (the stepfather) at sixteen. Took Josephine's money to have Miriam "stopped." Gave Alice the Nathaniel contract, paid by Josephine.
- **Ep 7:** tells Alice what Miriam paid to join, as cruelty.
