# Ada Hargrave

**Age:** about 60. **Role:** supporting, not a finale player. Head housekeeper at Harding House.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Who she is:** has polished the same lamps for forty years. Keeps the cryo clean, so she knows Morgana exists. Ada and Tobias Marsh are siblings; Nixon is their nephew. She raised him below stairs.
- **The Lamp Saint faith:** how Hargrave's survivors made the massacre bearable. *Our dead became the light*: every green lamp is a Hargrave martyr shining. Morgana is **the Lady**, a saint fallen from heaven, given to the Hardings to light the world, with Hargrave's dead as her first offering. Ada tends a tortured prisoner like a shrine.
- **The lie:** the light is Morgana's blood; the village died as collateral. Ada never learns it. The audience carries it for her.
- **With Nixon:** the first to tell him he was special. The only one in the house who calls him "love."
- **With Marsh:** when he ran, she reported him. Has never visited Newgate. Calls him "the apostate."
- **With the Hardings:** keeps the rite and the secrets. Eldgard depends on her; Josephine trusts her.

**Ep 8.** Nixon: "Did you know?" Ada: "Forgive them? Love, I forgave them before you were born. They took us in when they didn't have to. That's more than God did for Hargrave."

**Death (Ep 9).** She works out the break-out plan and goes to report Nixon to Eldgard, as she reported her brother. On the servants' stair he begs her to wait one night. She touches his cheek: "I'm saving you, love. We're not them. We were never going to be them. We're Hargraves." He panics and holds his hand over her mouth too long. Her lamp smashes on the stairs. She dies still believing.

**Ep 6:** sees Tamara reading Alistair's book in the library, and is afraid. **Ep 9 aftermath:** Tamara finds her body and tells the household she fell.

**Requirements.** Seed her from Ep 1 (the lamps). Her "love" with Nixon starts when he arrives in Ep 2. Her death needs aftermath: someone finds her, and Nixon chooses whether to lie.

**Mirror.** A sibling betrayed to protect the order (Ada), a man drowned to protect a sibling (Miriam), a hand over a mouth (Nixon).
- **Ep 6, two keepers:** past midnight, mending Nixon's shirts, Josephine across the table: "Do you ever hate this house, Ada?" "A house doesn't ask to be built, ma'am. It only asks to be swept. When the master took my brother, he gave my nephew a captain's coat. God never offered me half as good a trade." That's her own story of her life; she believes it.
