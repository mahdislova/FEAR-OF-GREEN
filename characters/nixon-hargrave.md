# Nixon Hargrave

**Age:** 23. **Role:** lead. Youngest Lantern-Captain; Nathaniel's best friend.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Who he is:** the current kept Hargrave. Son of Ada and Marsh's younger sister, who died. Raised by Ada below stairs; schooled and ranked on Harding money.
- **First appears in Ep 2,** back from the east to report the failures: village after village frozen in ice, their heat and power source faded. In Ep 1 he's absent; his empty chair is enough ("That's Nixon's chair, Nat").
- **Wants:** to belong to the family that raised him.
- **What he learns:** being kept is not being loved. "Did you love me, or did you keep me?" "Yes." (Ep 9)
- **The lie he believes:** obedience is the price of belonging.
- **Secret (from Ep 2):** Eldgard's right hand. Holds the keys to the house. Eldgard does not show him the pod: Nixon believes the Frost Killer is only a killer until the Harding book (Ep 8).
- **Ep 3:** signs Eldgard's Frost Killer statement, his first signature under a lie. That night Nathaniel sends his men to the wrong street.
- **Ep 5, the first break with Nathaniel:** discovers Nathaniel has hidden the Frost Killer, whom London suspects of killing Jamie. Nathaniel says it is unproved. In Ep 8 Nathaniel confirms what she admitted in Ep 7, and Nixon takes her in the raid without yet knowing Eldgard's plan for the pod.
- **Wound:** he cannot tell whether Eldgard loved him or kept him. The answer is both.

**Arc ladder.** Wants to belong → hunts the Frost Killer and keeps Eldgard's secrets → loses Nathaniel → postpones choosing between obeying and knowing → breaks his uncle out of Newgate with Ada's blood on his cuffs, then holds the Guard for London, not for the Hardings.

- **Eps 6–7, "Find her":** follows Nathaniel to the workshop and leads the dawn raid. From the roof he sees the Frost Killer freeze his best friend's hand. It's why, in Ep 8, he believes she belongs in custody.

**Key moments.** Ep 5: catches Nathaniel hiding the suspect, then hands Eldgard his journal and watches it become a hunt ("Find her."); Ep 8: hears confirmation of Jamie's death before the raid. Ep 8: arrests the Lost One at Jennie’s, signs her into Guard custody for Newgate, then reads the Hargrave pages. "Did you know?" (to Ada). Ep 9: Ada's death; "Did you love me, or did you keep me?" Eldgard: "Yes."

- **Ep 9:** Tamara finds him over Ada's body and covers it up ("Mrs Hargrave fell"). He owes her his freedom and she holds his worst act.

**With Tamara:** in love with her. She relines his coat (Ep 3), warns him not to give Eldgard the journal (Ep 5), writes her own marriage (Ep 7), gives him the Hargrave pages and asks him to stay inside it; he refuses (Ep 8). Across the frozen street in her coat (Ep 10).
