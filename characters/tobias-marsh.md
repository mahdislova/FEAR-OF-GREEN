# Tobias Marsh

**Role:** supporting. Newgate prisoner, held as a madman.
**Look:** a long frame folded on a plank bed, prison grey, a white beard grown into a nest, a sharp nose, fingers drumming on the stone, and eyes clear as a bell. Chalks a lamp with a tear-shaped flame on every wall.

- **Who he is:** the previous kept Hargrave who asked questions. Ada's brother; Nixon's uncle. Ran; Ada reported him; Eldgard had him declared mad and locked away. Whispers the true Hargrave history to anyone who'll listen.
- **What he is to Nixon:** Nixon's future if he keeps asking.
- **Ep 4:** has raved about "the Lady's child" since the night of the Exhibition; Nixon comes to question him about the Frost Killer. Pinches his chin ("Hargrave chin"), sings the Hargrave rhyme, and shouts "Ask the woman with the keys!" Nixon doesn't understand.
- **Ep 9:** broken out by Nixon, with Ada's blood on his cuffs. Nixon learns he's his uncle.

Unset: arrest date.
