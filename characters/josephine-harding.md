# Josephine Harding

**Age:** 50. **Role:** antagonist-mother. Matriarch, ex-Vykra.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Wants:** to keep her sons alive and the family whole, at any cost.
- **The lie she believes:** control is protection.
- **Outside the aura:** not Harding blood. Every terrible thing she does, she does fully awake.
- **Secrets:** **killed Miriam herself** on Blackwood Bridge (Ep 1). She bought the rendezvous from Grise and went in person, out of retirement for one night, because she wouldn't trust the guild with her son. She didn't go there to kill: Miriam fought, Vykra against Vykra, and thirty-year-old training took over. "I didn't go there to kill her. My hands did." She'll never be sure that's true. Then she hired Miriam's little sister to watch her other son.
- **Mirror:** thirty years ago Josephine was a Vykra girl who escaped the guild by marrying a Harding: Miriam's exact plan. She kills the next girl trying the same way out.

- **Ep 2, the hire:** Grise names Alice in front of her: "Bounds." Josephine knowingly hires the sister of the girl she killed. Grise now holds her.
- **The penance direction.** She tells herself it's control: safer close than out there looking. Underneath, it's penance: she is the only person in the show who chooses to keep her guilt within arm's reach. Ep 4: offers her wrist for the buttons on purpose. Ep 5: "Have you family, Miss Bounds?", and sits through her crime told back as an abandonment. Ep 6: Grise: "And you hired her sister." Ep 8: uses Alice's reports to give up the Lost One, then uses Nathaniel’s laughter with Tamara against Alice at the window. Jennie interrupts with news of the arrest. Ep 10: Alice learns Josephine knew from the first day. "I chose you so I'd have to look at you."
- **What must stay true:** she never confesses on her own, never asks forgiveness, and never stops using Alice. The penance is real and so is the use.

**The audience knows from Ep 2:** alone, she burns her gloves in the grate; the Vykra knot is stitched inside them. No music, no twist. From then on every scene between her and Alice runs on dread.

**The face comes back.** Ep 5: summons Alice during the dinner. Ep 6: confronts Grise, works out that the creature wears the face of her crime, and pays to remove the witness. Ep 8: provides the lead for the arrest, so the evidence goes in the pod.

**End.** Alice learns the truth in Ep 8, after Alice returns. Confronted by Alice and spared (Ep 10). An hour later Grise comes for her, hands her a new pair of gloves with the knot inside, and they go downriver with the Harding pearls before Jennie's paper reaches either of them. Season 2: the matriarch and the old knife, loose together.
