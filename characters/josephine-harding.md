# Josephine Harding

**Age:** 50. **Role:** antagonist-mother. Matriarch, ex-Vykra.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Wants:** to keep her sons alive and the family whole, at any cost.
- **The lie she believes:** control is protection.
- **Outside the aura:** not Harding blood. Every terrible thing she does, she does fully awake.
- **Secrets:** **killed Miriam herself** on Blackwood Bridge (Ep 1). She bought the rendezvous from Grise and went in person, out of retirement for one night, because she wouldn't trust the guild with her son. She didn't go there to kill: Miriam fought, Vykra against Vykra, and thirty-year-old training took over. "I didn't go there to kill her. My hands did." She'll never be sure that's true. Then she hired Miriam's little sister to watch her other son. Knows Jamie is alive.
- **Mirror:** thirty years ago Josephine was a Vykra girl who escaped the guild by marrying a Harding: Miriam's exact plan. She kills the next girl trying the same way out.

**End.** Alice learns the truth at the end of Ep 7. Confronted by Alice and spared (Ep 10). Fate after the finale: open.
