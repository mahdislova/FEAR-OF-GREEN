# Josephine Harding

**Age:** 50. **Role:** antagonist-mother. Matriarch, ex-Vykra.
**Look:** black silk the colour of a closed piano, grey-streaked hair coiled tight as rope, a jet brooch she has never explained, kid gloves indoors, always.

- **Wants:** to keep her sons alive and the family whole, at any cost.
- **The lie she believes:** control is protection.
- **Outside the aura:** not Harding blood. Every terrible thing she does, she does fully awake.
- **Secrets:** paid Grise to have Miriam "stopped" before Jamie ran with her; Jennie's knife did the rest. Then hired Miriam's little sister to watch her other son. Knows Jamie is alive.

**End.** Named by Jennie (Ep 9). Confronted by Alice and spared (Ep 10). Fate after the finale: open.
