# Josephine Harding

**Age:** 50. **Role:** antagonist-mother. Matriarch, ex-Vykra.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Wants:** to keep her sons alive and the family whole, at any cost.
- **The lie she believes:** control is protection.
- **Outside the aura:** not Harding blood. Every terrible thing she does, she does fully awake.
- **Secrets:** **killed Miriam herself** on Blackwood Bridge (Ep 1). She bought the rendezvous from Grise and went in person, out of retirement for one night, because she wouldn't trust the guild with her son. She didn't go there to kill: Miriam fought, Vykra against Vykra, and thirty-year-old training took over. "I didn't go there to kill her. My hands did." She'll never be sure that's true. Then she hired Miriam's little sister to watch her other son.
- **Mirror:** thirty years ago Josephine was a Vykra girl who escaped the guild by marrying a Harding: Miriam's exact plan. She kills the next girl trying the same way out.

**The audience knows from Ep 2:** alone, she burns her gloves in the grate; the Vykra knot is stitched inside them. No music, no twist. From then on every scene between her and Alice runs on dread.

**The face comes back.** Ep 5: summons Alice during the dinner. Ep 6: confronts Grise, works out that the creature wears the face of her crime, and pays to remove the witness. Ep 8: provides the lead for the arrest, so the evidence goes in the pod.

**End.** Alice learns the truth in Ep 8, after Alice returns. Confronted by Alice and spared (Ep 10). Fate after the finale: open.
