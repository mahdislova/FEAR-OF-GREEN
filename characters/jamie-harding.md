# Jamie Harding

**Age:** 25. **Role:** catalyst. Eldest son and heir.
**Look:** tall and immaculate in evening black, cravat crooked from pulling at it, grey eyes that haven't slept.

- **Imprinted in 1873** with resistance to the Harding aura: the only Harding immune to it.
- **Initiated on his 25th birthday:** shown the core. Morgana asks him: "Where is my child?"
- **His plan:** free the child, not the mother (he can't bring himself to darken London), and run with Miriam. Half heroism, half cowardice.
- **Ep 1:** breaks the ornament. The offspring tries to wear him, ravages him, and tears out of the window.
- **After:** found frozen. Declared dead; public funeral, closed coffin. Alive in a locked room at the top of Harding House, alien frost in his blood, tended by Byron.

**Rule:** Jamie never wakes to explain the plot. He stays under all season: Season 2's fuse.
