# Morgana

**Stays in the background. Until she is freed, she is helpless.**

**Role:** the being at the core.
**Look:** to be written. Never fully seen until late in the season.

- Captured c. 1780 by Alistair Harding, tortured, betrayed after keeping her side of the deal. Frozen in her own cryo pod and drained ever since.
- A built-in universal translator: she speaks to anyone in their own language. Every word is intimate and terrifying.
- **Dying.** Her failure is the season's clock.
- **The mother's plan:** in 1873 she sent her child to imprint the two Harding boys. Even the victim used her child.
- **Not a villain:** a victim with every right to her rage. Freeing her is just and catastrophic.

**Key lines.** "Where is my child?" (to Jamie, Ep 1). "My child is out." (Ep 1 tag). "My child." (Ep 8 tag).

**End (Ep 10).** Freed. Kills Eldgard. The ship damages London. Morgana waits aboard the ship. When her child chooses Alice, she lets her stay and departs in silence.
