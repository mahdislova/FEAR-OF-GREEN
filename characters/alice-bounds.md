# Alice Bounds

**Age:** 20. **Role:** lead. Undercity glim-runner; initiated into the Vykra in Ep 2. Gin Lane.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Wants:** first, to stand where her sister stands and be seen. Then, Miriam's killer, and to keep "Miriam."
- **The lie she believes:** love means holding on by any means, because nobody ever stood in front of her.
- **Secret:** her first Vykra contract, paid by Josephine through Grise, is to get close to Nathaniel.
- **How she joins (Ep 2):** Grise, who knows Miriam is dead, tells Alice her sister ran off and left her, and offers the knot Miriam always kept from her. "She isn't here to object, love." Alice swears in at the laundry hours before she finds "Miriam".

**The wound.** See `miriam-bounds.md`: the stepfather, the belt, the counting game ("eight posts and he's done"), **behind the eightfold fence**. Alice believes he drowned drunk. She once saw Miriam crying at his grave and thought she was grieving him.

**Arc ladder.** Wants to stand where Miriam stood → joins the Vykra on Grise's lie that Miriam abandoned her, then gets "Miriam" back (Ep 2) → her first envelope, paid by Josephine, is Nathaniel → learns what Miriam did (Ep 6), learns the Lost One's identity and hears her Jamie confession beside Nathaniel (Ep 7), stops him taking her but sends her away → returns to the Lost One by choice, hears Jennie name Josephine and confesses the contract to Nathaniel herself (Ep 8) → postpones choosing between holding on and letting go → offers the Lost One the ship and lets her choose.

**Key moments.** "You're not her" (Ep 7). Stops Nathaniel restraining the Lost One but does not yet forgive her. "Nine is the one where we get up" (Ep 8). Spares Josephine (Ep 10). "Us" at the lock (Ep 10).
