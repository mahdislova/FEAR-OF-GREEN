# Alice Bounds

**Age:** 20. **Role:** lead. Undercity glim-runner; initiated into the Vykra in Ep 2. Gin Lane.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Wants:** first, to stand where her sister stands and be seen. Then, Miriam's killer, and to keep "Miriam."
- **The lie she believes:** love means holding on by any means, because nobody ever stood in front of her.
- **Secret:** her first Vykra contract, paid by Josephine through Grise, is to get close to Nathaniel.
- **How she joins (Ep 2):** Grise, who knows Miriam is dead, tells Alice her sister ran off and left her, and offers the knot Miriam always kept from her. "She isn't here to object, love." Alice swears in at the laundry hours before she finds "Miriam".

**The wound.** See `miriam-bounds.md`: the stepfather, the belt, the counting game ("eight posts and he's done"), **behind the eightfold fence**. Alice believes he drowned drunk. She once saw Miriam crying at his grave and thought she was grieving him.

**Arc ladder.** Wants to stand where Miriam stood → joins the Vykra on Grise's lie that Miriam abandoned her, then gets "Miriam" back (Ep 2) → her first envelope, paid by Josephine, is Nathaniel → learns what Miriam did and confesses the contract to Nathaniel herself (Ep 6), learns the Lost One's identity and hears her Jamie confession beside Nathaniel (Ep 7), stops him taking her but sends her away → returns to the Lost One by choice, hears Jennie name Josephine (Ep 8) → postpones choosing between holding on and letting go → offers the Lost One the ship and lets her choose.

**Ep 4, the first bad choice:** Grise threatens to give Ajax her sister's address unless Alice brings her in. Alice walks Miriam into Grise's room on her own terms to buy time; Grise sells the sighting anyway.

**Ep 7 to Ep 8, the overnight step:** Jennie sends word the girl is bleeding from the nose after saving her. Alice can't hold the grudge against that.

**What she learns:** keeping someone isn't loving them. In Ep 9 she still says it the old way ("I don't care whose plan it was. You're mine"); in Ep 10 she offers the ship and means it.

**Aura moments.** Each one is planted. The bounty men in the fog (Ep 4); the knife at Grise's throat (Ep 6); Miriam's throw, landed at last (Ep 7, planted Ep 1). See `canon/season-one.md`.

**Key moments.** "You're not her" (Ep 7). Stops Nathaniel restraining the Lost One but does not yet forgive her. "Nine is the one where we get up" (Ep 8). Spares Josephine (Ep 10). "Us" at the lock (Ep 10).

**With Nathaniel: enemies to lovers.** A Mayfair second son who's better with brass than people, and a Vykra knife sure every aristocrat is a soft parasite, paid to watch him. They fall for each other while trying hard to hate it.
- **Ep 3, the waistcoat:** "You buttoned your waistcoat three holes crooked, your lordship." "I'm not a lord." He re-does the buttons when he thinks she isn't looking. She is.
- **Ep 4, the burn and the lens:** cold water over his blistering arms. "I'm eleven stone." "Then you're underfed. Eat a turnip." Then the near-kiss over the lens, broken by the girl crying out in her sleep. "Blow out the lamp, Nat." It's the first time she calls him that.
- **Ep 5:** upstairs after the toast, she works out that he knew about Tamara since the Ep 1 lunch and kept it from her.
- **Ep 6:** he offers her a train north and "a room where you aren't holding a knife", and that's what makes her confess the contract. "Did she pay you to let me kiss you?" "Nobody's ever paid me enough for that." That night they stop circling.
- **Ep 7:** she opens the door for the Lost One, and he can't forgive her yet. "I'd rather you went."
- **Ep 10:** "Us. Together, Nat. Look at me. On three." Then, on a cellar step, she tells him what his mother did; burned hand in ruined hand.
