# Jennie

**Age:** 19. **Role:** supporting. Grise's best knife. (The old twins, merged.)
**Look:** lean and still, mousy hair pinned flat, forearms corded from the mangle wheel. Scar through the left eyebrow (carried over).

- Silent, patient, never the first to blink. Trained beside Alice.
- Killed Miriam on the bridge (Ep 1), after watching her buy the tickets and buying the driver.
- **Ep 3:** sees Miriam's face alive at the crystal market. Grise: "Then you didn't do it properly."
- **Seed her doubt** in Eps 3 and 7.
- **Ep 9:** turns, and tells Alice who paid: Josephine.
