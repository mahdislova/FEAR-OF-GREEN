# Jennie

**Age:** 19. **Role:** supporting. Grise's best knife. (The old twins, merged.)
**Look:** to be written. Scar through the left eyebrow (carried over).

- Silent, patient, never the first to blink.
- **Ep 1:** on the Narrows raid with Miriam; Alice joins uninvited. Bond and rivalry with Alice start there.
- **Ep 1:** Grise sends her to follow Miriam. She watches from the stair as a figure in the shadows puts Miriam over the rail. She is the only witness.
- Holds a secret worth killing for: a Harding wife knifed a Vykra girl.
- **She knows the Lost One can't be Miriam.** She watched Miriam die. When she sees her alive (Ep 3, the crystal market), she's the one person in London who knows the face is wrong, and she's terrified of it.
- **Seed her doubt** in Eps 3 and 7.
- **Ep 4:** fetches "Miriam" to the laundry for Grise's test.
- **Ep 7, Act One:** Grise's man comes for her in the riot; the Lost One saves her life.
- **Ep 8, after Alice returns:** The thing wearing the face of the woman she watched die has just saved the witness who did nothing. The guilt breaks her, and she tells Alice: Josephine killed Miriam. "Miriam was the best of us."
