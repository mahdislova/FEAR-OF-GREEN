# Jennie

**Age:** 19. **Role:** supporting. Grise's best knife. (The old twins, merged.)
**Look:** to be written. Scar through the left eyebrow (carried over).

- Silent, patient, never the first to blink, with Grise.
- **The warm body.** Funny without jokes. Raised by the Vykra, she can't say a feeling out loud, so she does something useful instead: stolen buns (Ep 3), a caraway loaf and a terrible message (Ep 4), crumpets toasted on the cold-sink (Ep 6), the only chair (Ep 7). The audience should love her by Ep 6.
- **Ep 6:** alone with the Lost One she asks if Miriam is in there. "You sang at the mangles. She liked that." She hears the past tense.
- **Ep 1:** on the Narrows raid with Miriam; Alice joins uninvited. Bond and rivalry with Alice start there.
- **Ep 1:** Grise sends her to follow Miriam. She watches from the stair as a figure in the shadows puts Miriam over the rail. She is the only witness.
- Holds a secret worth killing for: a Harding wife knifed a Vykra girl.
- **Ep 2:** watches Alice swear in on Grise's lie that Miriam ran away. Grise sends her with Alice's gloves; at the door she sees Miriam's face.
- **She knows the Lost One can't be Miriam.** She watched Miriam die. When she sees her alive (Ep 2, the Bounds room), she's the one person in London who knows the face is wrong, and she's terrified of it.
- **Seed her doubt** in Eps 3 and 7.
- **Ep 4:** carries Grise's ultimatum to Alice and walks the sisters out after the test. Ordered to follow them home, she says she lost them in the fog: her first lie to Grise.
- **Ep 7, Act One:** Grise's man comes for her in the riot; the Lost One saves her life.
- **Ep 8, after Alice returns:** The thing wearing the face of the woman she watched die has just saved the witness who did nothing. The guilt breaks her, and she tells Alice: Josephine killed Miriam. "Miriam was the best of us."

**Ep 9:** Jennie returns to the Guard desk and signs her account of the bridge. She stands by both that testimony and her earlier sighting.
