# Alistair Harding

**Role:** the founder (cold opens only). Eldgard's grandfather.
**Look:** to be written.

- A failed inventor until the crash, c. 1780.
- Tortures Morgana, makes the deal, breaks his word, hooks her into her pod.
- His first full draw freezes Hargrave. He hangs the witnesses and takes one child into his house.
- Writes everything down in cipher: the Harding book. Builds the aura.

**Cold opens:** the crash (Ep 1), the deal (Ep 4), Hargrave (Ep 8), closing the pod (Ep 10).
