# Byron

**Age:** 64. **Role:** supporting. Harding butler.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Wants:** to see Nathaniel safe.
- **The lie he believes:** loyalty means silence.
- **Secret:** open (was tending Jamie in a locked room, now cut).
- **Ep 1:** covers for Josephine's absence from the Exhibition ("the mistress went home with a headache"). He suspects, and says nothing.
- **The ring:** issued to him as a guard of the core, a tool of the post, not a gift.
- **Arc:** tells Nathaniel what the light is: "You were always enough." (Ep 6). Goes to help the freezing East End and survives the Gin Lane explosion inside the shield from Eldgard’s gift. He puts his name to what he saw against the household’s account (Eps 5–7).

Old-bible history (ex-Lantern sergeant, loved Josephine thirty years ago): carried over, not re-reviewed.
