# Byron

**Age:** 64. **Role:** supporting. Harding butler.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Wants:** to see Nathaniel safe.
- **The lie he believes:** loyalty means silence.
- **Secret:** tends Jamie in the locked room. Stops sleeping.
- **Arc:** his slip points Nathaniel upstairs (Ep 5). Confesses to Nathaniel: "You were always enough." (Ep 6). Goes to help the freezing East End and dies there, in the cold the house made (Ep 6–7).

Old-bible history (ex-Lantern sergeant, loved Josephine thirty years ago): carried over, not re-reviewed.
