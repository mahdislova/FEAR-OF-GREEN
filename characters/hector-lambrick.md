# Hector Lambrick

**Age:** 34. **Role:** arch-nemesis. Heir apparent to the Lambrick coal empire; runs it in all but name for his dying father, Lord Lambrick (`lord-lambrick.md`). Tamara's older half-brother.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Wants:** the Harding light dead, London back on coal, and his father's chair, which he has been told since childhood is his.
- **The lie he believes:** the throne is his by right; anything that threatens it is theft.
- **Arc:** the greed curve. He wants the light gone until he learns what it is; then he wants to own her, and he'd bleed her faster. Underneath, a man who has never once had to earn the thing he's sure of, watching his little sister earn it in front of their father.
- **With Tamara:** real affection, and real use. They work together against the Hardings, and they're good at it. He takes her moves and wears them, and she lets him, because that's how the house has always worked. He splits his breakfast with her on her wedding morning (Ep 8). It's all true, which is why the order in Ep 9 is a tragedy, not just a twist.
- **Ep 2:** at the wake the Duke asks him privately how much coal his sister’s house east of the line will need. Hector sends the sale elsewhere and reads the hour of the question: the Duke expects the light to fail. He sends Ajax to find out what was taken at the Narrows.
- **Ep 3, the widows:** he pays every family the ice touched on Gin Lane. "Grief, my dear. It's the cheapest thing I'll ever buy." At home, their father asks Tamara to read him the names, and she does it from memory.
- **Ep 4:** attacks the Harding Charter in Parliament with yield figures from the Works' chief engineer, the figures his sister decoded and his father handed to him. The lamps flicker on cue. He breaks the Duke with the question on Tamara's card.
- **Ep 6:** "Your sister has a chair at the Works." "I put her in it." "Did you." He locks the Gin Lane audit in his drawer and says nothing when the East is cut.
- **Ep 7:** his coal is the only heat left, and the price is his sister's marriage. In the yard: "You'll have Mayfair, and I'll have the pits... we take the room we're given." He thinks her answer is a joke. At their father's order he hands Tamara the delivery orders himself, from the drawer where the audit lies.
- **Ep 8:** gives his sister away, because their father can't walk.
- **Ep 9, humiliated and killed:** at the Works gate the Duke gives the Crown's coal to Tamara and asks Hector, in front of the clerks, whose name is on the carts. Then word comes that their father has sent for his lawyer, and for Tamara, alone. Hector feels the chair go. He orders Ajax to take her out. Ajax brings word it's done and takes him to the Gin Lane crater for proof. Tamara puts a blade in him. "Ajax— why?" "Everyone has a price, sir. Sorry." He dies where he let a street burn.
- **End:** Lambrick wins; Hector doesn't. A dark London runs on Lambrick coal, in his sister's hands.
