# Lord Hector Lambrick

**Age:** 55. **Role:** arch-nemesis. The coal baron.
**Look:** to be written.

- **Wants:** the Harding light dead and London back on coal, and owned by him.
- **Arc:** the greed curve. Wants the light gone until he learns what it is; then wants to own her. He'd bleed her faster.
- **Ep 2:** at the wake the Duke asks him privately how much coal his sister’s house east of the line will need. Hector sends the sale elsewhere and reads the hour of the question: the Duke expects the light to fail. Sends Ajax to find out what was taken at the Narrows.
- **Ep 3, the widows:** pays every family the ice touched on Gin Lane. "Grief, my dear. It's the cheapest thing I'll ever buy." When the East freezes (Ep 7), the street remembers who came.
- **Ep 4:** attacks the Harding Charter in Parliament with yield figures from the Works' chief engineer. The lamps flicker on cue. Breaks the Duke with one question about his sister's coal.
- **Ep 7:** his coal is the only heat left; the price is his daughter's marriage.
- **Ep 7:** hands Tamara the delivery orders from the drawer where the Gin Lane audit lies.
- **Ep 9, humiliated and killed:** at the Works gate the Duke gives the Crown's coal to Tamara and asks Hector, in front of the clerks, whose name is on the carts. Hector orders Ajax to take her out. Ajax brings word it's done and takes him to the Gin Lane crater for proof. Tamara puts a blade in him. "Ajax— why?" "Everyone has a price, sir. Sorry." He dies where he let a street burn.
- **End:** Lambrick wins; Hector doesn't. A dark London runs on Lambrick coal, in his daughter's hands.
