# Episode 4 — The Hearing

**Clock:** THE LIGHT HAS 38 DAYS · 13 November 1889

**Cold open:** c. 1781. The deal: Morgana teaches; Alistair gives his word.

**Change:** The light's failure goes public.  
**Governing question:** What happens to a family secret once it's a matter of public record?  
**POV:** Lambrick, Tamara and Nathaniel  
**Set piece:** The Charter hearing; Ajax's ambush at the Works wall  
**Heaviest moment:** Nathaniel watches his father lie under oath, and knows it's a lie.  
**Tag:** Eldgard to Josephine: when the mother dies, they use the child.

## Scenes

### 1. You have my word

*Cold open · A tithe barn on the Harding estate. Winter, c. 1781.* · Thread: The light / Morgana · Reveal, Setup

Snow sifting through the roof slats. Morgana, chained to the barn's king-post with ship's cable, sits on the straw. We see more of her than before: nine feet folded small, skin like the blue in the heart of a glacier, a long skull crowned with something between hair and frost, eyes with no whites at all, only depth. Scars on her wrists where the cable has been. She is exhausted and very dignified.

In a corner, in a cage of brass and glass that will one day sit on a desk in Mayfair, a small brightness pulses: her child.

Alistair sits across from her with a notebook on his knee. She draws in frost on the barn floor with one long finger: lines, spirals, a system. He copies everything in a cipher of his own invention. The first page of the Harding book.

> **ALISTAIR:** Teach me the rest, and I'll let you go. You and the little one. You have my word.
>
> **MORGANA:** *(her voice in his head, his own tongue)* Your word is a small thing.
>
> **ALISTAIR:** It's all I have.
>
> *(She looks at the cage. Then she bends and keeps drawing.)*

**Exit state:** Before: we knew there was a broken promise. After: we've seen the promise made, and the mother choosing to trust it for her child's sake.

**Craft:** Lore that alters the next decision (atlas lesson 9): this cold open is why Morgana, tonight, will ask for 'the boy who knows': she taught one Harding once, and she means to be taught back.

### 2. Breakfast at Lambrick House

*Act One · Lambrick House, the breakfast room.* · Thread: Lambrick / coal · Reveal, Turn

Coal-gas light, kippers, a fire built so high the room sweats. A coal sack sits on the sideboard among the chafing dishes. Inside: Pike's second ledger, copied fair, every figure in a substitution cipher.

Lambrick's secretary has been at it an hour. Tamara, buttering toast, takes it from him, looks at three lines, and writes the key on her napkin. Two minutes. Lambrick reads the decoded column: 61. 58. 55. 52. He laughs out loud.

> **LAMBRICK:** *(to his secretary)* Well done, Hoskins.
>
> **TAMARA:** You're welcome.
>
> **LAMBRICK:** You'll be a Harding by Christmas, my girl. Read everything in that house.
>
> **TAMARA:** And if there's nothing to read?
>
> **LAMBRICK:** There's always something to read in a house that old. *(tapping the figures)* Harding's light is dying, and he's paying a hundred men a week to say it isn't.

**Exit state:** Before: Lambrick suspected. After: he has the numbers, a source inside the Works, and a daughter who can read anything.

**Craft:** The audience learns Pike sold out (dread) while Eldgard doesn't (Weeks's 'reader knows more'). Tamara is established as the one person who can break Alistair's cipher, before anyone mentions the cipher.

### 3. The Committee Room

*Act One · Westminster, Committee Room 14. Morning.* · Thread: Lambrick / coal · Turn, Reveal

A long panelled room, MPs at a horseshoe table, a public gallery packed with press; the room lit by six great gallowglass gasoliers. Lambrick's petition: revoke the Harding Charter's monopoly over the Ring. Eldgard sits in the witness chair as if he owned the building. Nathaniel and Josephine in the front row. Tamara two seats down.

Lambrick's counsel reads the yield into the record, year by year, then month by month. 61. 58. 55. 52. The press gallery goes into uproar. Eldgard's face doesn't move, but his eyes go along the row of Works men behind him, one by one, and stop on nobody.

Then, mid-answer, the six gasoliers dip at once, *whmmmm*, and hold at half-light for three long seconds. Every head in the room looks up.

> **COUNSEL:** Lord Harding, is the light failing?
>
> **ELDGARD:** There is a fault in the eastern lines. It is being corrected.
>
> **COUNSEL:** And the yield at the source itself?
>
> **ELDGARD:** *(under oath)* Is unchanged.
>
> *(The gasoliers dip.)*
>
> **LAMBRICK:** *(from the floor, delighted)* Is *that* a fault, my lord?

**Exit state:** Before: the failing light was rumour. After: it's on the parliamentary record, an inquiry is granted, and Eldgard knows someone inside the Works has betrayed him.

**Craft:** An institution doing dramatic work (atlas lesson 6): the hearing constrains what Eldgard can do next, because now the Crown is watching the Works. The light itself testifies, a rule (it dips with her grief) used as a plot weapon.

### 4. Which are you?

*Act One · Westminster, a corridor. After.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Turn

Nathaniel walks out fast, his father's 'unchanged' still in his ears. He saw the numbers. He's been in the gauntlet shop under that chalkboard every day for three years. His father lied under oath, and did it beautifully.

Tamara falls into step beside him, fan closed, quick as a clerk.

> **TAMARA:** Your father's lying and mine's buying. Which are you?
>
> **NATHANIEL:** I make gauntlets.
>
> **TAMARA:** That's not an answer, it's a trade.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** What do you want, Miss Lambrick?
>
> **TAMARA:** To see your family's library. Before the wedding. I like to know what I'm marrying into.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** *(a beat; then, because his father lied)* Thursday.

**Exit state:** Before: Tamara was his father's arrangement. After: she's an ally with her own agenda, and he has given her the door to the house.

**Craft:** Heaviest moment of the episode, played as a walk, not a speech. Nathaniel's first act after seeing the lie is to open a door to the enemy's daughter: a choice with a cost he can't see yet.

### 5. Forty ledgers

*Act Two · The Harding Works, the control room.* · Thread: The light / Morgana · Turn

Eldgard comes straight from Westminster and stands behind Pike's chair the way he stood behind it the night after the Exhibition. Pike writes the yield without looking up. His hand is steady. It costs him everything.

> **ELDGARD:** Someone gave Lambrick my numbers.
>
> **PIKE:** Your numbers are in forty ledgers, sir. Every shift-book in the Works.
>
> **ELDGARD:** Then find me the forty men.
>
> **PIKE:** And when I find him?
>
> **ELDGARD:** *(already going)* Bring him to me. Not to the Met.

**Exit state:** Before: Pike was selling quietly. After: he has been asked to hunt himself.

**Craft:** Pure dread (atlas lesson 5): the audience knows the traitor is the man being asked to find the traitor. The phrase 'not to the Met' echoes Nixon's order in Episode 3: the Hardings run their own law.

### 6. The apostate

*Act Two · Newgate, a cell on the madmen's corridor.* · Thread: Nixon & the kept Hargraves · Setup, Reveal

Nixon comes with a turnkey and a lantern because a madman in Newgate has been raving about *the Lady's child* since the night of the Exhibition, and Nixon is hunting a frost child.

**TOBIAS MARSH**, about sixty-five: a long frame folded on a plank bed, prison grey, a white beard grown into a nest, a sharp nose, fingers drumming a rhythm on the stone, and eyes clear as a bell, which is the most frightening thing about him. Chalked all over his walls: the same small shape again and again, a lamp with a flame like a tear.

He looks up at Nixon. And something happens in his face.

> **MARSH:** *(standing, coming to the bars)* Oh. Oh, there you are.
>
> **NIXON:** You've been talking about a child. The Lady's child.
>
> **MARSH:** She's out, isn't she? I felt it. Whole prison went cold. *(he reaches through the bars and pinches Nixon's chin, gently, like an uncle)* You've her chin. Hargrave chin.
>
> **NIXON:** *(pulling back)* I'm Hargrave. It's my name.
>
> **MARSH:** *(laughing, then weeping)* Course it is. They keep one. *(singing, thin and old)* Hargrave in the frost, Hargrave on the rope, Hargrave in the lamplight, burning up for hope…
>
> **MARSH:** *(as Nixon goes)* Ask the woman with the keys! Ask her what she polishes!

**Exit state:** Before: Nixon's name was just his name. After: a madman has told him it means something, and he's walking away pretending it doesn't.

**Craft:** A long-fuse secret delivered by the least credible witness in London (Weeks). The rhyme carries the massacre without a flashback. 'The woman with the keys' points the audience at Ada four episodes early: dread, not surprise.

### 7. The door plates

*Act Two · Nathaniel's workshop. Evening.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Reveal, Turn

Drawings across every bench: Works archive plans Nathaniel signed out under Pike's name. One shows the core's sealed door, and on it six plates of 'decorative glass', each etched with a line of script. The same script as the shard. He lays the shard on the drawing and the lamp above the bench dims.

The shard came from under the Works.

Later, chestnuts popping on the stove, *pop-pop*, and the two of them teaching each other: Alice shows him how to open a padlock with a hairpin in eleven seconds; he straps the gauntlet on her arm and shows her how to fire it without breaking her own wrist. She fires it. It knocks her flat on her back. They both laugh until they can't breathe. The Lost One watches from the window with Miriam's smile on her face, and doesn't know it's Miriam's.

> **NATHANIEL:** It's from the core. My brother died holding a piece of the core.
>
> **ALICE:** How does a piece of the core end up on your father's carpet?
>
> **NATHANIEL:** Because my father brought it home.
>
> **ALICE:** *(flat on the floor, laughing)* That's not a gauntlet, that's a mule.

**Exit state:** Before: the investigation was about a killer. After: it points into Nathaniel's own family. And the romance has stopped being a job for Alice.

**Craft:** The clue and the intimacy in one scene; the laughter is earned because the stakes just went up. The Lost One's borrowed smile is the sisters' story going on silently in the background.

### 8. The veiled client

*Act Three · A tea shop off Bond Street. Afternoon.* · Thread: The Vykra / the contract · Turn, Setup

Lace curtains, cream cakes, ladies of good family. Alice in her best stolen gloves, the knot inside. Across the table a woman in deep mourning, face invisible behind a black veil, a jet brooch at her throat, kid gloves on her hands. She never gives a name. Grise said: *the client.*

Alice reports, carefully: Nathaniel Harding is investigating his brother's death; he spends his nights in a workshop in Clerkenwell; he found a piece of glass. She leaves out everything that matters. She leaves out a sister.

> **CLIENT:** What kind of glass?
>
> **ALICE:** Green-blue. Like ice on a pond. It hums.
>
> *(The client's cup stops halfway to her veil.)*
>
> **CLIENT:** Bring it to me.
>
> **ALICE:** That's not the job.
>
> **CLIENT:** The job is whatever I pay for, Miss Bounds.

**Exit state:** Before: Alice's contract was watching. After: it's stealing from him, and she has realised she'll lie to her own employer to protect him.

**Craft:** The audience knows the veil, the brooch, the gloves: it's Josephine (dread). Alice doesn't. It's the episode's slow-burning fuse to Episode 7, when Nathaniel learns who hired her.

### 9. Come home

*Act Three · The Harding Works, the outer wall at Tyburn. Night.* · Thread: The light / Morgana · Reveal, Turn

The Lost One slips out while the others sleep, the copper coil cold on her wrist. She follows the hum through the streets the way a dog follows a whistle, to Tyburn: the great curtain wall of the Works, black brick fifty feet high, steam leaking from its seams, and under the ground a sound only she can hear.

She puts both palms on the brick. Frost ferns up the wall twenty feet. And through every lamp along the wall her mother speaks to her, for the first time since the box.

> **MORGANA:** *(through the lamps, aching)* Child. Come home.
>
> **THE LOST ONE:** I don't know the way.
>
> **MORGANA:** Then bring me the boy who knows.

**Exit state:** Before: Morgana only wanted her child back. After: she wants Nathaniel, and the Lost One now has a mission that runs straight through the man sheltering her.

**Craft:** The mother's plan surfaces as one line, its full meaning held until Episode 9. Every scene of Nathaniel and the Lost One from here plays double: is she his friend, or her mother's errand?

### 10. Ajax at the wall

*Act Three · The Works wall, Tyburn.* · Thread: Lambrick / coal · Turn

Ajax has followed the frost trail from Seven Dials to Clerkenwell and on to here. He steps out of the steam with his Black Gauntlet charged, *hssssss*, and four men behind him.

Nathaniel and Alice arrive at a run, having woken to an empty floor. The fight, in phases:
**One:** Alice vs Ajax, the rematch: knife against iron, *KLANG*, his stitched face grinning.
**Two:** Nathaniel's leap-gauntlet against the Black Gauntlet, his own stolen prototype; he knows its weak valve because he designed it, and he hits it.
**Three:** Ajax gets an iron hand on Alice's throat, again. The Lost One takes hold of the Black Gauntlet with both bare hands. Frost goes into the iron, *krrrrrk*, the pistons seize, the casing splits like a dropped bell. Ajax looks into her face from six inches away and understands exactly what he's looking at.

> **AJAX:** *(to Alice)* I remember you.
>
> **ALICE:** Good.
>
> **AJAX:** *(backing off, arm dead, laughing in disbelief)* Winter. You're made of *winter*. *(to Nathaniel)* And you're Harding's boy. Oh, my governor's going to love you.

**Exit state:** Before: Lambrick suspected the Hardings owned the killer. After: his enforcer has seen her, seen her power, and seen Nathaniel Harding protecting her.

**Craft:** The episode's action unit is also its biggest information turn. The outcome fails for the heroes: they win the fight and lose the secret (atlas: 'the objective can fail without the episode failing').

### 11. Leverage

*Act Four · Lambrick House, the study. Midnight.* · Thread: Lambrick / coal · Turn

Ajax's dead iron arm on Lambrick's desk like a trophy, split down the casing, still white with frost. Lambrick in a dressing-gown, turning the frost-cold plate in his hands like a jeweller. On the stair outside the half-open door, Tamara in her nightgown, listening.

> **AJAX:** It's a woman made of winter. And Harding's second boy is hiding her.
>
> **LAMBRICK:** *(softly, ecstatic)* The heir's dead, the light's failing, and the spare's hiding the thing that killed his brother. *(a beat)* I don't want to ruin the Hardings, Ajax. I want to *own* them.

**Exit state:** Before: Lambrick wanted London back on coal. After: he wants the creature and the leverage, and his daughter has heard every word.

**Craft:** The greed curve, step two: he stops wanting the light dead and starts wanting it. Tamara on the stair is a choice point for her, deferred to Thursday's visit to the Harding library.

### 12. Frost on the hem

*Act Four · Harding House, the back hall. Dawn.* · Thread: Nixon & the kept Hargraves · Turn

Nathaniel comes in by the garden door at dawn, filthy, his knuckles skinned. Nixon is sitting on the bottom stair in his shirtsleeves, waiting. He looks at the hem of Nathaniel's coat. Frost. In a warm house. Not melting.

> **NIXON:** Where've you been, Nat?
>
> **NATHANIEL:** Out.
>
> *(Nixon reaches out and touches the frost on the hem. It burns his fingers. He looks up. Nathaniel looks back. Neither speaks.)*
>
> **NIXON:** *(later, in the study, to Eldgard)* Could someone in this house be hiding it?
>
> **ELDGARD:** *(not looking up)* Find out.

**Exit state:** Before: Nixon suspected. After: he knows, and he's been given the order to prove it.

**Craft:** The friendship's fuse shortens without a word spoken between them. Nixon's loyalty test is now a ticking clock running into the midseason.

### 13. When the mother dies

*Tag · The top room, Harding House. Night.* · Thread: Jamie / the locked room · Reveal, Turn

Jamie in his bed of frost, Byron dozing upright in his chair. Eldgard and Josephine stand at the foot of the bed in their night-clothes like two people visiting a grave. The tallow candle gutters.

> **ELDGARD:** She's dying, Jo. A year. Less, now Lambrick has the numbers.
>
> **JOSEPHINE:** And then?
>
> **ELDGARD:** When the mother dies, we use the child.
>
> **JOSEPHINE:** *(very quiet)* And Nathaniel?
>
> **ELDGARD:** Nathaniel builds lamps. He doesn't need to know what's in them.
>
> *(Josephine looks at her frozen son. Byron's eyes are open. He heard.)*

**Exit state:** Before: Eldgard wanted the child back. After: we know what for: the next ninety years in the pod. And Byron, the one man in the house who loves Nathaniel without terms, has heard it.

**Craft:** The tag hands the audience the stakes before the characters (spine design). Byron's open eyes are the hinge into Episode 5: his slip is caused by this, not by accident.
