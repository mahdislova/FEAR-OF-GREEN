# PITCH DRAFT: The Two Keepers (Josephine & Ada)
## Target: Episode 2, Scene 15B (Bridge to Scene 16)
### Location: Harding House, Servants' Scullery Hearth — Midnight

*Draft from a friend's pass, not canon. The scene in `episodes/episode-06.md` ("Two keepers") is the story of record: no gloves, no witness to the burning, set in Ep 6.*

**CONTEXT:**
After Jamie's funeral, Josephine needs to burn the kidskin gloves she wore when she stabbed Miriam Bounds on Blackwood Bridge. Ada Hargrave, head housekeeper, catches her. 

This scene establishes the profound, silent complicity between the aristocratic mother and the servant aunt—two women who have spent forty years protecting their respective boys at the expense of their souls.

---

```fountain
INT. HARDING HOUSE - SERVANTS' SCULLERY - MIDNIGHT

Stone flags, whitewashed brick, rows of copper jelly moulds catching the green reflection of the streetlamp outside the iron grate.

JOSEPHINE HARDING (50) stands before the small laundry hearth. She wears black mourning silk, her hair still coiled tight.

In her bare hands, she holds a pair of fine black kidskin gloves. The leather around the right wrist is stiff and crusted dark brown—dried blood from Blackwood Bridge.

She kneels, throws the gloves onto the burning coals. 

The kidskin curls, hissing (*tsssss*), the white Vykra knot stitched inside the lining flaring bright for two seconds before blackening into ash. A foul, sweet stench of scorched flesh and tannin fills the air.

FOOTSTEPS on the stone stairs.

Josephine freezes, her hand reaching into her sleeve for a weapon that isn't there.

ADA HARGRAVE (60) steps into the scullery carrying an oval wicker basket piled high with Nixon’s freshly starched captain's collars.

Ada stops. She looks at the smoke rising from the grate. She looks at Josephine kneeling on the cold flags in twenty guineas of French silk.

Neither woman speaks for four long seconds.

Ada sets her basket down on the wooden dough-table. 

She walks over to the hearth. She doesn't ask what is burning. She doesn't ask whose blood smells like roasting meat.

She picks up the heavy iron fire-poker, leans over, and firmly pushes the charred thumb of the glove deeper into the white-hot center of the peat fire.

ADA
(voice quiet, flat as an iron pan)
Leather stinks when it catches, ma'am. The draft carries up the chimney into the master's study.

Josephine slowly stands up, smoothing her skirts. Her throat moves as she swallows.

JOSEPHINE
I had an accident in the carriage, Ada.

ADA
(still poking the embers)
The master’s father had accidents in carriages too, ma'am. When I was sixteen, I washed three shirts of Alistair Harding's in this very tub that had river mud up to the armpits. I never found the coat that went with them.

Ada sets the iron poker back in its brass stand with a quiet *clink*.

ADA (CONT'D)
A woman doesn't keep a house forty years by sniffing the ashes.

Josephine watches Ada’s rough, red-knuckled hands. For a fleeting second, the imperious Harding matriarch looks like what she was thirty years ago: a terrified Vykra girl who ran away from a laundry.

JOSEPHINE
Do you ever hate this house, Ada?

Ada turns to face her mistress. Her iron-grey hair is neat under her lace cap; her chatelaine keys chime faintly against her apron.

ADA
A house is only bricks, Mrs. Harding. Bricks don't ask to be laid, and they don't ask to be swept.
(she picks up one of Nixon's clean collars, running her thumb over the seam)
When your husband took my brother Tobias and put him in the Newgate chalk... he gave my sister's boy an officer's coat and a silver spoon. God never gave my family half as fair a bargain.

JOSEPHINE
(a whisper)
I do what I must for my sons.

ADA
I know you do, love.
(she turns back to her collars, her voice chilling in its absolute devotion)
And I do what I must for Nixon. As long as that boy has his coat and his place at table... this kitchen has no eyes, and this hearth has no tongue.

Ada picks up her basket and walks toward the servants' stair without looking back.

Josephine stands alone before the dying coals, watching the last ember of her glove dissolve into grey ash.

FADE OUT.
```
