## The verdict, teacher to intern

You have good instincts for set pieces, payoffs and texture. The orange from Episode 1 coming back in Episode 10 is the best idea in your pass. Hector's stolen audit gives a loose thread a real job, and the Duke's ultimatum gives him a track. Jennie's crumpets made her warm.

What went wrong was **process, not talent**:

1. **You rewrote the writer's own scenes.** The scenes already filed in Eps 2, 3, 5, 6, 8 and 9 are the writer's, not drafts. You reworded "Closing time at the covered market", "The Works meeting", "Gin Lane washhouse" and the Ep 8 train scene, and they came out louder and worse. The rule: a filed scene is sacred. Build around it.
2. **You treated pitched and open as locked.** You marked open questions "Resolved" and stamped the season "LOCKED & FULLY BROKEN". Only the writer locks.
3. **You didn't ripple-check.** Moving one beat moves every beat that points at it. Nixon at the Ep 1 lunch breaks his Ep 2 homecoming. Showing Josephine at the bridge breaks the slow-burn dread. Nixon confronting Nathaniel in Ep 4 steals the Ep 5 turn. The Lost One saying "My mother" in Ep 4 burns her Ep 7 reveal.
4. **You brought back cut lore.** "The White", "the White Ship" and "the occult history of the White" were cut from the bible. The ship is just *the ship*.
5. **You added mythology after Episode 8.** The brass plates ("a Harding heir fed to the core every fifty years") break the rule *no new mythology after Episode 8*. They also contradict the engine: the heir isn't the price; the child is.
6. **You rewrote locked dialogue.** "Never lose the light within you." "How many days did you tell your sister to lay in?" "I chose you so I'd have to look at you." When a line is in quotes in a beat, it's the writer's.
7. **Tone.** The writer likes Weeks: short, hard lines in violence; restraint everywhere else. "A breathtaking, terrifying display of assassin mastery" tells the audience what to feel. Byron with a rent tin and a list of names is stronger than Byron shouting "shoot me where I stand."
8. **Logic.** Ep 1's "In short" said the gauntlet demo was brushed aside, and the scenes said it misfired. The "Not in this episode" line listed Hector, Ajax and the chief engineer, and then you used all three. Keep your own file consistent with itself.
