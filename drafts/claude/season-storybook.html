The Exhibition

On the board

Beats locked. Your scenes are on the scene board and haven't been filed into the episode file yet, so this follows the beat order.

ForegroundNathaniel's worst day: the stolen chair, the gauntlet misfire, the roof with Jamie. Then Jamie frees the child and is torn apart by it.
BackgroundMiriam, Alice and Jennie on the Narrows raid, heading for the bridge; Byron covering for Josephine; Ada's lamps; Tamara the only one not laughing.

Not in this episodeHector, Ajax, Marsh, the chief engineer. Nixon is away in the east.

Cold open

Harding woods, c. 1780 A ship lies broken in the trees. Alistair's lantern finds a hand in the wreck. Somewhere in the hull a signal pulses, steady as a heartbeat. Alistair traces it and silences it. That silenced beacon is the season's last domino.

  1. 1

    Jamie's initiation (days earlier)

    The Works vault Eldgard takes Jamie down the stair to the vault, the heir's initiation. In the pod lies Morgana. She speaks to him in his own language: "Where is my child?" Jamie now carries the family secret and the question that will get him killed.

  2. 2

    The luncheon chair

    Harding House Nathaniel sits in the empty chair at his father's right. Eldgard moves him: "That's Nixon's chair, Nat." Nixon isn't in the episode; Eldgard has sent him east to see the failures for himself. In one line we learn who the father's real son is.

  3. 3

    The Narrows raid

    The Narrows Miriam and Jennie ride a Vykra raid on a Lambrick consignment, and Alice comes along uninvited. That starts the bond and the rivalry between Jennie and Alice. Miriam drops a man with her one-handed throw, the move Alice has watched her whole life, and it pays off in Ep 2. Miriam steals a book from the cargo and hands it to Grise: there's more on that wagon than the Lambricks admit. The sisters' thread also carries the birthday orange and Miriam's "Not tonight" (season-one threads). [DROPPED THREAD] The stolen Lambrick book never pays off. In Ep 2 Hector sends Ajax to find out what was taken, and nobody ever says what it was. Tamara's book is Alistair's and comes from Eldgard, so this one is a separate, orphaned object.

  4. 4

    The gauntlet failure

    The Works, the Exhibition Nathaniel demonstrates his gauntlet prototype to impress a visiting professor. It misfires in front of two hundred people. Eldgard watches his second son humiliated. The crowd laughs. Tamara, a polite stranger to Nathaniel for years, doesn't. A gauntlet he can't explain, failing: the first sign of the imprint.

  5. 5

    Byron covers for Josephine

    The Exhibition Josephine is the hostess and has to leave before midnight. Byron tells anyone who asks that the mistress went home with a headache. He suspects, and says nothing: for him, loyalty means silence.

  6. 6

    The roof

    The Exhibition roof Jamie finds Nathaniel on the roof. A quiet moment between brothers: "Never lose the light within you." Then Jamie goes home to free the child. It's the last time they speak.

  7. 7

    Ada's lamps

    Harding House Ada polishes the lamps. Placed only to seed her for her death in Ep 9. [UNPLOTTED / GAP] This beat has no position or scene yet. It's a seed, not a moment.

  8. 8

    The ornament and the bridge (cross-cut)

    Harding House, the study Jamie breaks the ornament to free the child, not the mother. The offspring, starved and half-mad, ravages him and goes out through the window carrying his strongest thought: Miriam waiting at the bridge. Blackwood Bridge Miriam waits for Jamie, the boy she meant to run away with. We see only her fall, and a figure backing away in the shadows (Josephine, not yet revealed). Jennie watches from the stair. The river.

  9. 9

    Closing image

    The river Miriam drowning, intercut with the study Nathaniel coming in to find Jamie bleeding out. The secret is out, and two families have lost a child.

Next episode bridgeInto Episode 2 · Strong

Ep 1 closes on Jamie bleeding out and Miriam in the river. Ep 2 opens inside Nathaniel's buried nightmare of 1873, then on the funeral convoy and the Lost One hauling herself out of that same river. Clean, causal, emotional.

The Wake

Scenes broken

Fully broken: 17 scenes in order, plus three beats (the vestry, the tea urn and a duplicated Duke beat) that sit outside the scene list.

ForegroundAlice: Grise's lie, the oath her sister fought, and finding the mute girl from the river.
BackgroundJamie's funeral and wake; Nathaniel's first lie to Nixon; the Duke's word to Eldgard; Josephine hiring Grise and being sold Alice's name.

Not in this episodeMarsh, Byron, Morgana, the chief engineer.

Cold open

Harding nursery, 1873 Five-year-old Nathaniel climbs out of bed. Something slips into his room. Eyes as limitless as the sky. A shriek, and he wakes. A memory pretending to be a dream.

  1. 1

    Funeral convoy / river steps

    Funeral route Nathaniel wakes in the carriage. Families line the road holding up letters from the dark east. A woman taps the glass: her brother's village lamps went out on Tuesday. Before Nathaniel can open the window, the Guard clears the wheels. The quay Across town the Lost One hauls herself out below the quay, a beat behind every spoken word, and flinches from a ferryman's help. The light's failure and its cause arrive in the same shot.

  2. 2

    The Bounds room

    Bounds room Alice comes in from another night searching and finds Grise sitting on Miriam's bed. Grise lies, as a kindness: girls who leave the guild for a man don't send word home. Then the offer: the knot Alice has wanted since she was twelve. "She isn't here to object, love." Alice crosses another stretch of river off her list.

  3. 3

    The knot

    Shadwell laundry Noon, steam and lye. Alice swears the oath with her hand flat on Grise's ledger. "Come for your gloves in the morning." She finds Jennie among the women. Jennie knows where Miriam went and says nothing. Alice goes back to the river.

  4. 4

    The Duke's word (vestry)

    The church vestry Before the service Eldgard asks the Duke, his old friend, to tell the Lords the eastern failures are a fault in the lines. "You're asking me to be wrong in public, so you don't have to be." "Will you?" "For Jamie. Not for the light." [UNPLOTTED / GAP] This is a locked beat with no scene in the 17-scene list. It needs a slot, probably just before scene 4.

  5. 5

    The Duke at the funeral doors

    Church doors the Duke protects the family from a reporter's question about the east. From the lectern he tells how young Jamie ate the food off his plate after his wife died, "three Thursdays" before he caught him. Josephine laughs. The Duke asks London to keep coming to one another's tables.

  6. 6

    Buttons

    Market awning Two children button a coat wrong and giggle. the Lost One tries her own top button; the little one points to the right hole. For a moment she lets a stranger see her: the first time she's human with anyone.

  7. 7

    The lowering / Nixon's umbrella

    Graveside The Lanterns turn their crystal collars; bands of colour cross the descending coffin; every shutter clicks shut at once. Nathaniel stays while the chairs are folded. The rain stops: Nixon is home, holding the umbrella Nathaniel once mended with white thread. A hug that soaks them both.

  8. 8

    The covered passage

    Covered passage Local boys corner a soaked woman in a coat that isn't hers. One grabs her sleeve and is on the flags before she's found a word. the Lost One stands exactly as Miriam stood after a throw. No frost. Everyone gets up. She runs.

  9. 9

    Hector and the Duke

    The wake, Harding House Ajax brings Hector the broken Narrows tally and wants men for tonight. Tamara takes the tally from his hand as the Duke crosses the room. The Duke asks how many days of coal his sister's house east of the line will need. Hector answers, sends him to another merchant, and reads the hour of the question: the Duke expects the light to fail. Tamara writes the time on the back of the tally. Hector: find out what was taken. [INFO-DUMP CLUTTER] The beat list states the Duke's coal question twice: once in the vestry bullet ("two hours later...") and again in the convoy bullet. One idea, written down twice.

  10. 10

    The tea urn (Tamara's track)

    The wake Eldgard saw Tamara write the hour. "Your father has a clever daughter. Does he know?" "He knows my price." "That isn't the same thing." It's the first time a man with power has seen her instead of pricing her. [UNPLOTTED / GAP] A Tamara-track beat with no slot in the scene list yet.

  11. 11

    Alice finds her

    The passage The children remember the buttons; the winded boy swears she threw him one-handed. Alice knows that throw. She finds the girl behind a shutter and scolds her for disappearing. The familiar face recoils. Alice sits on the wet flags until the Lost One takes her sleeve, and walks her home that way.

  12. 12

    Tea after the burial

    The wake Nathaniel and Nixon share a plate and Jamie's old receiving-line signal: squeeze the shoulder for "get me out", cover the hand for "stay". For a few seconds Nathaniel gets to be a little brother again. Ada piles Nixon's plate: "You're thin, love." Nixon asks Nathaniel to walk him through the night Jamie died. Eldgard pulls Nixon aside, and the east is confirmed: village after village frozen. The letters outside were true.

  13. 13

    The Bounds room, afternoon

    Bounds room Dry clothes and too many questions. Alice reads the silence as Miriam being stubborn, until the girl keeps watching her mouth after she's finished speaking. The skin stays cold an hour after she's dry. Alice blames the river and takes her to Ren.

  14. 14

    Nixon and Tamara, service yard

    Service yard Tamara turns the tally face down when Nixon arrives. His hands have shaken since the east; she closes her fingers over his around a fresh cup until they're steady. A time before his shift tomorrow. At her father's voice: "Your tea, Captain." The secret love, established.

  15. 15

    Ren's rooms

    Ren's rooms Ren, an underground healer, watches the girl reach for a towel and lets her take it. A knock or a fright can steal speech and memory, and familiar company may bring it back. When Alice stands to pay, the Lost One shifts along the bench to stay beside her. "She wants you here."

  16. 16

    The parents / the study

    Harding House Josephine unpins the mourning ribbon from Jamie's door and can't rub out the pinhole. Grief between husband and wife; a laugh downstairs that she thinks for half a second is Jamie's. Eldgard questions Nathaniel: when did Jamie change, where did he go after the roof? "He went to see if you were all right. I should have gone myself." Then he asks again. Nathaniel walks out, retraces Jamie's last steps, and finds a shard by the skirting that the sweep missed. He pockets it. Nixon is waiting in the hall.

  17. 17

    Eldgard's keys / Nixon's journal

    The hall Eldgard gives Nixon the keys of the house and the family's inquiry. Nathaniel watches. Nixon puts the keys in the pocket with his journal. Was anything disturbed? Nathaniel touches the shard and says no. Nixon writes it down. The first lie, now on the record.

  18. 18

    Josephine commissions Grise

    Josephine's grate / Shadwell laundry Cross-cut: Josephine burns the gloves she wore on the bridge, the Vykra knot showing as the lining catches, while Grise stitches the same knot into new gloves for Alice. Josephine hires Grise to put someone close to Nathaniel. Grise names her: "Bounds." Josephine knowingly buys the sister of the girl she killed. "I'm selling you the only person in London who'd look for you." Grise sends Jennie with the gloves.

  19. 19

    Jennie at the door (tag)

    Bounds room Dusk. Jennie holds out the gloves and sees, behind Alice, the face she last saw going over the rail. the Lost One edges toward Alice. Alice reaches for the gloves. Jennie doesn't let go.

Next episode bridgeInto Episode 3 · Strong

Jennie at the door leads straight into Ep 3's morning, where Jennie tells Alice to keep Miriam's return secret. Grise's lie, now exposed, becomes Alice's double game. The shard and the logged lie carry into Nathaniel's hunt.

The Frost Killer

Fragments

The richest beat list in the season, but only two scenes written (the covered market and one finger). The rest is ordered here from the beats.

ForegroundThe Frost Killer: the market outburst, the citywide hunt, and Nathaniel hiding the sisters.
BackgroundAlice playing Grise; Nixon and Tamara's secret; Eldgard naming the monster; Hector paying the widows.

Not in this episodeJosephine, Ada, Marsh, Byron, Morgana.

Cold open

[UNPLOTTED / GAP] The cold open is open. The 1873 fragment moved to Ep 2 and nothing has replaced it.

  1. 1

    Morning in the Bounds room

    Bounds room Still in disbelief, Jennie tells Alice to keep it secret that Miriam is back. Alice asks why; Jennie can't say, and leaves. Alice does the maths: Grise swore Miriam ran off, and Miriam is in Alice's bed. Grise lied.

  2. 2

    The first envelope

    Shadwell laundry Alice leaves the girl with Ren and plays the grieving initiate. Her first envelope: Nathaniel Harding. "Any word of my sister?" "Still gone, love." Now Alice knows Grise lies to her face, and Grise doesn't know she knows. She takes the job to stay inside long enough to learn what Grise had to do with the bridge.

  3. 3

    The rented room

    The rented room Before his shift Nixon meets Tamara. She's in his arms before the door has closed. He wants to walk her home openly and asks whether his pay and his place make him someone she can only see here. The engagement letter is in her glove, and her fingers go to it. Instead of telling him, she gives him his greatcoat, relined in grey wool, because he froze for nine nights in the east. "I can sew. Nobody's ever asked me to." "Walk with me on Sunday." "Give me a little time." She lets him leave believing the timing is still theirs to choose.

  4. 4

    The girl slips her sitter

    The market the Lost One slips away from Ren the way a child slips a sitter, pulled toward Alice. She finds Alice stalking Nathaniel at the market. "Hey, what are you doing here?" The girl holds up a hand: "Food." For the first time she follows a simple instruction from Alice. [LOGIC BREAK] The beat says her "first clear words come in the workshop tag", but she says "Food" here. Decide whether "Food" counts as her first word, or the tag line has to be clearly the first full sentence.

  5. 5

    The outburst

    The market Lambrick's men attack Alice. the Lost One understands she has to protect her, and the cold breaks out: ice kills several of Lambrick's men and some bystanders, bodies impaled nine feet in the air. London names a Frost Killer and blames Jamie's death on it. [UNPLOTTED / GAP] Why Lambrick's men go for Alice isn't stated on the page. The natural reason is Ajax hunting whoever took the Narrows cargo, but that link and the stolen book are never made explicit.

  6. 6

    Closing time at the covered market

    Covered market Earlier, Alice ducked a paper star and met the lamp boy who lowers the canopy for a penny. After the killing, Nixon finds two hundred people crushing against shut gates and opens both; his Lanterns leap to the iron galleries and light the aisles from above. Ajax, tracking the sisters for Lambrick (who has heard the killer leaves frost like gallowglass), looks up and finds Nixon staring back across the glass roof. Alice pays the boy, and the stars come down. Under the paper, two women walk slowly enough to keep it still. At the workshop door, it's Nathaniel's turn to decide.

  7. 7

    The workshop / one finger

    Nathaniel's workshop Nathaniel hides the sisters. The Lost One can't uncurl her hand. He offers his prototype; Alice asks her sister before touching her. One finger first. The crystal takes the cold. Nathaniel has now taken a side without knowing what she is. (In Ep 7 both women will recognise the device when he reaches for it.)

  8. 8

    Eldgard names the monster

    Harding House, the study Nixon reports the market killings. Eldgard drafts the Guard's statement himself, on family paper: "The Frost Killer." "We don't know it's the same—" "Londoners need one name for what they're afraid of, Captain, or they'll find their own. Sign it as the officer in charge." Nixon signs: the first time his name goes under something he doesn't know is true.

  9. 9

    The wrong street

    The Guard post That night Nixon gratefully shows Nathaniel the search map. Nathaniel reads it like an engineer and sends the frost down the old drains toward Gin Lane. "That's where I'd look." Twenty men go to Gin Lane; Clerkenwell is left open. "I don't know what I'd do without you tonight." "Neither do I." The first active lie to Nixon, and the one Nixon will remember in Ep 5.

  10. 10

    Grise learns

    Shadwell laundry Word reaches Grise: Miriam Bounds is alive, and was at the market killing men with ice. Alice sat across from her that morning and said nothing. Grise realises Alice is playing her.

  11. 11

    The chair

    Nathaniel's workshop The only chair that doesn't squeak is missing a leg. Alice drops into her sister's lap: it takes two to sit in this house. the Lost One laughs before Alice does. Their first joke since the river, and a memory that pays off in Ep 8.

  12. 12

    Hector pays the widows (dawn)

    Gin Lane First light, snow. Hector, Ajax and a strongbox. Hector pays every family the ice touched, not only his own men's. The Hardings send no one. Tamara holds the ledger. "Grief, my dear. It's the cheapest thing I'll ever buy." Tamara learns what grief buys. [LOGIC BREAK] This happens at dawn the next day, after the night of the tag. Either it opens Ep 4, or the tag scene happens before dawn and this cuts in after it.

  13. 13

    Tag: "I know you"

    Nathaniel's workshop the Lost One studies Nathaniel's face. "I know you." (She was in his room in 1873.)

Next episode bridgeInto Episode 4 · Partly connected

Nathaniel shows her the ornament drawing and asks whether Jamie showed it to her, but nobody follows up on what she meant. Ep 4 opens on the Morgana cold open and the hearing instead.

The Hearing

Beats only

Beats only, no scenes yet. You're polishing this episode now.

ForegroundThe hearing: Lambrick takes the light's failure to Parliament, the Duke is broken in public, and Tamara makes the winning move.
BackgroundNathaniel and Alice match the shard; Alice reports to Josephine; Grise's test of "Miriam"; Nixon, Marsh and Ada.

Not in this episodeByron.

Cold open

The wreck, c. 1780 The deal. Morgana teaches the ship's power system; Alistair: "You have my word."

  1. 1

    Lambrick House, breakfast

    Lambrick House Tamara breaks the chief engineer's cipher in three lines. Hector takes the figures to Parliament. [DROPPED THREAD] The engineer leaked the yield figures, and the bible says he sells the secret to Lambrick, but no one ever finds out and it costs him nothing. He reappears in Ep 5 and 6 as a neutral technician.

  2. 2

    The prison train and Marsh

    Newgate, the chalk hill The prison train climbs to Newgate on its chalk hill at London's edge. From the glazed transfer tower Nixon sees relief guards glide down on folding wings; the moving stair sticks, and every prisoner lifts a foot at once. (This sets up Ep 9.) Marsh, the madman, pinches Nixon's chin and whispers "Hargrave." Nixon walks away not understanding. [UNPLOTTED / GAP] Why Nixon goes to Newgate is never stated. He needs an errand (a prisoner transfer, a Frost Killer lead) or the visit looks like it exists only to set things up.

  3. 3

    Ada and the spectacles

    Harding House Nixon mentions the madman. Ada asks whether Marsh has his spectacles, won't say how she knows him, and wraps them for Nixon to deliver by hand. Sunday, the crisp end of the joint: "You can cut it yourself this time." He promises to come. Ada cares for her brother in the prison she helped put him in.

  4. 4

    Westminster: the hearing

    Westminster Hector attacks the Harding Charter with the smuggled figures; the chamber lamps flicker on cue. the Duke keeps his word to Eldgard and tells the Lords it's a fault in the lines. In the gallery Tamara slips her father a card: Ask the Duke how many days. Hector: "Your Grace, how many days did you tell your sister to lay in?" The Hardings' most respected witness is broken in public, and a friendship with him. The chamber applauds Hector; Tamara sits with her hands in her lap. It's the last time she lets him take credit for her move. [INFO-DUMP CLUTTER] The bullet "Tamara at the hearing: the codebreaker, the engagement as a merger" says nothing her track doesn't already cover. Cut it.

  5. 5

    The shard matches

    Nathaniel's workshop Nathaniel and Alice match the shard to a Works drawing: Jamie could have opened the ornament himself. The Guard dismissed that possibility because of Nathaniel's own statement, so his silence now protects his lie. He shows the Lost One the drawing of the outside and asks whether Jamie showed her the ornament. She asks for time. He leaves the drawing with her. The romance warms.

  6. 6

    The buttons at the wrist

    Harding House Alice reports on Nathaniel to Josephine, and hates it. She fastens the pearl buttons at Josephine's wrist; one has left a blue mark on the linen. Cold water will take it out, Alice says: Jennie keeps a room over the old dyehouse, and whenever the stair leaks they wash that same blue out of their hems. Josephine offers the wrist on purpose and holds still, letting her victim's sister touch her. A confession nobody hears, and an address planted for Ep 8.

  7. 7

    Grise's ultimatum

    East End Through Jennie, Grise sends Alice terms: bring your sister in to report, or Grise gives Ajax the address. Alice believes her, because Grise sells to everyone. Bringing her in on Alice's own terms buys time.

  8. 8

    The hand-sign test

    Shadwell laundry Daylight, a floor full of women, and Alice never leaves her sister's side. Grise asks for a report and gets fragments. Then a Vykra hand-sign the real Miriam would have answered without thinking. the Lost One doesn't. Alice is too new to know what it meant. Grise smiles: it isn't Miriam, and it's worth more than anything she's ever sold.

  9. 9

    Jennie's first lie / the sale

    The fog Grise orders Jennie to follow the sisters home. Jennie comes back saying she lost them in the fog: her first lie to Grise. Grise sells to Lambrick anyway: a sighting, and the girl who walked them out.

  10. 10

    Tag: use the child

    Harding House Eldgard to Josephine: when the mother dies, they use the child. The audience knows the stakes before the characters do.

Open (midseason)

ForegroundNathaniel and Nixon break: the family dinner, the admission, the second statement, the journal.
BackgroundJennie outplays Ajax; Josephine questions Alice; the Lost One draws the inside of the ornament; Tamara buys her seat with the toast; the Works counts the hours.

Cold open

None. Straight in. [UNPLOTTED / GAP] The episode still has no title. It's "Open (midseason)" everywhere.

  1. 1

    The men on the night shift

    The Works At shift change the chief engineer hands the foreman two sheets: the figure from the hearing and a lower one for actual use. He orders the presses idle and signs for the lost shift himself. An apprentice reaches too soon; the foreman shows him the split crystal housing: on a press, it costs a finger. Under Gin Lane a whole block shares one housing. This sets up the explosion.

  2. 2

    What Grise sold

    Shadwell laundry Ajax comes to collect on Hector's purchase. Grise tells him exactly what she has: the Frost Killer and her sister came here yesterday, and Jennie walked them out. In front of the women, Grise gives Jennie "the rest of the day off". Outside, Ajax's choice: take him to the woman, or sign an account at the Guard.

  3. 3

    A prisoner reads the paper

    Newgate Nixon has the cell opened and delivers Ada's spectacles to Marsh. Marsh spots Works grit in the newspaper fold; as a boy he carried those returns up from the draw room. He keeps the page with the district figures. The gaoler records the gift under Nixon's name, so their acquaintance is now on record. Harding House Byron chips his ring carrying a tray; Eldgard turns the green stone back toward Byron's palm, the way he gave it to him. This sets up the shield in Ep 6.

  4. 4

    The workshop, before dinner

    Nathaniel's workshop Alice has been sent for. the Lost One asks for an evening alone. Once the door closes she moves the chair back where she likes it and spreads Nathaniel's drawing on the bench.

  5. 5

    Jennie takes the second offer

    The Guard office Jennie dictates a true statement: her name, the laundry, the day, "I saw Miriam Bounds." Did she see her kill anyone? No. A public room where Ajax has to accept his own terms. Her signature keeps him away from the sisters tonight, and leaves a record Josephine would pay to bury. A copy goes to the Lantern-Captain.

  6. 6

    Upstairs at Harding House

    Harding House Josephine receives Alice: wait upstairs, report where Nathaniel goes after dinner. "Have you family, Miss Bounds?" Alice gives Grise's lie: a sister who ran off. Josephine has to hear her crime retold as an abandonment, and says something kind. Downstairs Tamara catches Nixon: she came to ask Hector to put off the announcement. "Tomorrow will do." In the little room, Hector rehearses a question about Nathaniel's account. Tamara reframes it (ask after the toast, as the father-in-law) and takes the job herself in exchange for a chair at tomorrow's Works meeting. Through the door she can see Nixon. She could still go to him. [LOGIC BREAK] Alice already reported to Josephine and fastened her buttons in Ep 4, so "Have you family?" reads like a first meeting. Either it's their first proper interview, or the line needs to acknowledge that they've met.

  7. 7

    The toast

    The dining room Tamara stands before the steward can call for quiet and announces the engagement, looking straight at Nixon. Nixon squeezes Nathaniel's shoulder as if the news made him happy; Nathaniel covers his hand, Jamie's signal for "stay". Eldgard raises his glass to her alone, and Nathaniel sees his father look at her the way he has never looked at him. "Did you speak to him?" "Yes." Hector draws up a chair for her. She takes it.

  8. 8

    Two drawings

    Nathaniel's workshop the Lost One draws the curve she remembers facing her. Side by side, the two sheets show opposite sides of the same wall: she knows the inside of the ornament. She folds hers small. She still can't account for Jamie's death.

  9. 9

    A bill for the lights

    Harding House The engineer brings the idle-shift sheet. Eldgard signs, and asks for two written estimates for tomorrow: the hours they buy by stopping the Works, and the hours they buy by cutting a residential district. The engineer delivers the wage sheet to his men first.

  10. 10

    The assurance

    The dining room Hector: did Nathaniel say anything in the study was disturbed? Nixon repeats what he was told: "He said he saw nothing disturbed." Nathaniel could correct him in front of everyone, and lets his friend's answer stand.

  11. 11

    Upstairs after dinner

    Harding House, upstairs Nathaniel complains to Alice about the announcement. Asked when he first heard, he tells the truth: lunch, before the Exhibition. That lunch has come up in his Jamie stories for days. She puts his coat back on its peg.

  12. 12

    The conservatory (Tamara's track)

    The conservatory Among the orange trees. "Tomorrow they're letting me read the real figures." "I'm going to be very good at it, Nix." That's her confession. Then her gift: "Your journal. Don't give it to him. Keep that one thing yours." "He's the family." "That's what I'm afraid of."

  13. 13

    A witness for sale

    Shadwell laundry Jennie repeats her statement in front of the women who watched Grise hand her over. Grise can't punish her without showing them all how little her protection is worth. The women make room for Jennie at the tubs. Ajax leaves with a name and no address.

  14. 14

    The second statement

    Nixon's desk Nathaniel hands over the shard and admits helping the woman at the market. Nixon remembers the search map: his friend sent his men down the wrong street. He unfolds Jennie's statement. Nathaniel owns it and refuses to say where she is. A clean page: "Was Jamie alive when you reached him?" Last time Nixon's pen was down. This time he writes.

  15. 15

    The empty chair

    Nathaniel's workshop Nathaniel will sleep at Harding House so he doesn't lead anyone to her. Alice returns alone. the Lost One had cleared a place on the bench for him. She puts the folded sheet back in her pocket.

  16. 16
