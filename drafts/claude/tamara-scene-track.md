# Tamara's scene track: Season One (draft)

**What this is:** every scene Tamara is in, episode by episode, in order. Each scene says where it happens, what she wants walking in, what she does, and the state it leaves her in, which causes the next scene. Scenes marked *(canon)* already exist; the rest are new. **Superseded:** this draft was filed and then cut back in a logic pass. The episode files' "Tamara's track" is the current version; this file is kept only for reference. It builds on the anchors in `tamara-arc-pitch.md`.

**How to read the Nixon thread:** she never says "I'm choosing power over you." Every time she chooses it, she gives him something he needs in the same breath. The gift is the confession.

---

## Episode 1: The Exhibition

**1. Lambrick House, the study. Afternoon.**
Hector tells her the engagement is settled. She asks to see the contract. He says she'll see the ring. The contract is face-up on his desk, and she reads it upside down while he talks, the way she reads everything. She finds her price: the Pimlico depots, as dowry. She says nothing about it.
*Exit:* she knows exactly what she's worth to him, and that nobody asked her.

**2. The Exhibition, the great hall.**
"Very polite for forty years" with Nathaniel. Then the misfire. Two hundred people laugh; she doesn't. Afterwards, at his elbow, quietly: "The fourth turn. Your spiral tightens too fast at the throat." He stares at her. She's right, and nobody else in the room even understood the machine. Then, lightly: "When is Captain Hargrave back from the east?" Nathaniel doesn't know. "Tuesday," she says, and moves on before he can wonder how she knows.
*Exit:* Nathaniel has met an equal, and we've glimpsed a secret.

**3. Her room, Lambrick House. Night.**
She writes to Nixon in the cipher she taught him. The first draft says *they've sold me*. She reads it, burns it in the candle, and writes the second: *The lamps are lovely tonight. T.* In their cipher, "lamps" means *come home*.
*Exit:* she's asked for him without telling him why. It's the pattern of the season.

## Episode 2: The Wake

**4. The wake, the Harding reception room** *(canon: Hector and the Duke)*.
She sees the Duke crossing toward her father and quietly takes the Narrows tally from Ajax's hand. After the Duke has gone, it's her pen that writes the hour on the back.
**New: across the room, Eldgard sees her do it.**

**5. The wake, the tea urn.**
Eldgard passes her with a cup.
> **ELDGARD:** Your father has a clever daughter. Does he know?
> **TAMARA:** He knows my price.
> **ELDGARD:** *(going)* That isn't the same thing.

*Exit:* for the first time in her life, a man with power has *seen* her, not priced her. She can't stop thinking about it.

**6. The service yard** *(canon)*.
She steadies Nixon's shaking hands around the cup. The edge of her letter shows in his breast pocket, *the lamps are lovely tonight*, carried all the way from the east. Neither of them mentions it. "Your tea, Captain."
*Exit:* he came home because she asked. She hasn't told him why.

**7. The Lambrick carriage, home.**
Hector says Ajax will find what was stolen at the Narrows. "Ledgers," he says. She keeps his ledgers. None are missing. She looks out of the window.
*Exit:* her father is hiding something in his own cargo from her. If he won't let her in, she'll build her own way in.

## Episode 3: The Frost Killer

**8. A rented room near the Guard tower. Before his shift** *(canon, sharpened)*.
She embraces him the moment the door closes. He wants to walk her home openly. The engagement letter is in her glove; her fingers go to it, and stay there *(canon)*. Instead of telling him, she gives him something: a parcel. His Guard greatcoat, which he left at the wake, relined in grey wool. He was freezing for nine nights in the east, and she noticed.
> **NIXON:** You did this yourself?
> **TAMARA:** I can sew. Nobody's ever asked me to.
> **NIXON:** Walk with me on Sunday. In the park.
> **TAMARA:** *(buttoning the coat on him)* Give me a little time.

*Exit:* she warms him and keeps the truth. The audience knows the coat is an apology.

**9. Gin Lane, dawn** *(canon: the widows)*.
She holds the ledger while Hector pays every family the ice touched. She writes each name as the coins go out.

**10. Lambrick House, her room. Night.**
By one candle she copies every name and address from her father's ledger into a small book of her own. Nobody asked her to.
*Exit:* she has something of her own. We don't know yet what it's for.

## Episode 4: The Hearing

**11. Lambrick House, breakfast.**
The engineer's figures came in cipher. Hector's secretary has been at them an hour. She takes the page, reads three lines and writes the key in the margin. From the phrasing, Works shift-terms and a habit of numbering, **she knows who leaked them.** She doesn't say so. Hector takes the decoded sheet to Parliament.
*Exit:* she holds a name her father doesn't know she has.

**12. Westminster, the gallery** *(canon: the hearing)*.
Before the session she slips her father a card in her hand: *Ask the Duke how many days.* He does, and breaks the Hardings' best witness. The chamber applauds Hector. She sits in the gallery with her hands in her lap.
*Exit:* the last time she lets him wear her move.

**13. Westminster, an empty committee room. After.**
Eldgard is gathering his papers. She closes the door.
> **TAMARA:** Your chief engineer gave my father those figures.
> **ELDGARD:** *(not looking up)* Why tell me?
> **TAMARA:** My father would use it once. You'll use it for years.
> **ELDGARD:** And the price?
> **TAMARA:** Your library. I'm told there's a book no one can read.
> **ELDGARD:** *(smiling, for the first time)* A bride's curiosity.
> **TAMARA:** If you like.

He agrees. Three generations of Hardings have failed at Alistair's cipher; he thinks he's given her nothing.
*Exit:* she has betrayed her father to his enemy for a key to the one door that matters. Eldgard keeps his engineer on, knowing.

## Episode 5: The Toast

**14. Harding House, the hall** *(canon)*.
Nixon: "Tomorrow will do." He gives her another day.

**15. The little room off the hall** *(canon: the bargain with Hector)*.
The toast in exchange for a chair at the Works meeting.

**16. The dining room** *(canon: the toast)*.
She announces the engagement herself, looking at Nixon. **New: as the toast ends, Eldgard raises his glass to her alone.** Nathaniel sees his father look at her the way he has never looked at him. Nixon sees it too, and understands: she isn't being sold, she's being *chosen*.

**17. The Harding conservatory. After dinner. (The scene: she chooses, and says it without saying it.)**
The orange trees under glass, the green light outside the panes, the party muffled through the door. Nixon finds her there. He doesn't accuse her.

> **NIXON:** You did that well.
> **TAMARA:** It needed doing well.
> **NIXON:** He raised his glass to you.
> **TAMARA:** *(a beat)* He let me read the figures this morning. The real ones. Do you know nobody's ever let me read anything I wasn't told to?
> **NIXON:** I know.
> **TAMARA:** Tomorrow they're letting me sit at the table. *(she picks a dead leaf off the orange tree; she doesn't look at him)* I'm going to be very good at it, Nix.
> **NIXON:** I know that too.

That's her confession: not "I choose this over you," but *I'm going to be very good at it*, and he hears exactly what it means. Then she gives him what he needs.

> **TAMARA:** Your journal. The one you write in for him.
> **NIXON:** What about it?
> **TAMARA:** Don't give it to him. *(now she looks at him)* Whatever he asks for. Keep that one thing yours.
> **NIXON:** He's the family.
> **TAMARA:** *(a long look; then she straightens his collar, the way Ada does)* That's what I'm afraid of.

She leaves him among the orange trees.

*Exit:* she has chosen, and she's tried to protect him from the man who chose her. **That night he gives Eldgard the journal anyway** (the canon Ep 5 tag). Her warning makes his obedience a choice, and makes the tag hurt twice.

## Episode 6: The Remembering

**18. The Works, the meeting room** *(canon)*.
She asks when the East comes back on. She argues for draining the Gin Lane branch before the cut. Eldgard overrules her; his triage is defensible.

**19. The Works, the corridor. After.**
> **ELDGARD:** You were right about the branch.
> **TAMARA:** Then why—
> **ELDGARD:** Because being right about one street is easy. You'll learn.

She sends her runner herself *(canon)*.

**20. Gin Lane, after the blast.**
She goes. Nobody sends her; she just goes. The street lifted, the houses gone, the green shell cracking around Byron. Nixon is there with the Guard, carrying the dead out on doors. She takes off her gloves and takes the other end of a door. They carry three people together without one word. When they set the third down, his hand covers hers on the wood for one second. Then the Guard sergeant calls his name.
*Exit:* the cost of being right too late, carried with her own hands, next to the man she's losing. No dialogue. It's their love scene.

**21. The Harding library. Night.**
One lamp. Alistair's book. She has been at it every night since Ep 4. Tonight the first line opens. Ada brings tea, sees which book is open, sets the cup down very carefully, and goes.
*Exit:* she can read the Hardings' secrets. Ada knows she can.

## Episode 7: The Girl Who Isn't

**22. Eldgard's study.**
The East is freezing. She comes with her father's coal and her own terms: coal to the East tonight, the wedding Saturday. Eldgard, who taught her that being right about one street is easy, hears her being right about a whole district, and agrees.

**23. The Lambrick carriage.**
She tells Hector it's done. "Harding asked for Saturday." Hector thinks Eldgard blinked. Eldgard thinks Hector did.
*Exit:* she has written her own marriage, and both fathers think it was their idea.

**24. The rented room. The fight.**
Nixon has heard who set the date.
> **NIXON:** You chose this.
> **TAMARA:** I chose the East getting coal tonight. The rest came with it.
> **NIXON:** The rest is me.

She doesn't answer. She takes his coat, the one she relined, off the hook and holds it out to him. He takes it, because it's freezing, and that's the worst part.

**25. Nathaniel's rooms, Harding House.**
His ruined hand is bandaged. He's raw from the Lost One's confession. She doesn't pretend:
> **TAMARA:** Neither of us is marrying for love. Let's at least not lie to each other.
> **NATHANIEL:** Is that a vow?
> **TAMARA:** It's the only one I mean.

*Exit:* she has made one honest promise in the season, to the man she isn't in love with.

## Episode 8: The Wedding

**26. The Harding library. Dawn.**
She finishes the book. She reads what the Hardings did to Hargrave: the frozen village, the hangings, the child they kept. She reads it twice. Then she cuts the Hargrave pages out with a penknife.

**27. The vestry, before the service.**
She gives Nixon the pages. *(canon)*
> **NIXON:** What is this?
> **TAMARA:** Who you are. *(she closes his hand over them)* Don't read them today.

He reads them in the chapel. She knew he would. It's love, because he deserves the truth, and strategy, because Nixon with this truth will break Eldgard. She doesn't know which matters more.

**28. The altar.**
Through her Works seat she has seen the transfer order: the Lost One went to the Works, not Newgate. As the vows begin she tells Nathaniel, under her breath. She keeps the pact. He says "I will" anyway (canon), and then he goes. **She lets her groom leave their wedding** to free the creature, knowing that when the light goes out, coal is king.

**29. The empty chapel, after.**
Nixon is the last one left. She asks him to stay: her Captain, inside her marriage, kept.
> **NIXON:** I've been kept my whole life.

He hands her back the empty ring box and walks out.
*Exit:* she has everything she negotiated for, and not him.

## Episode 9: The Break-out

**30. Eldgard's study. Before dawn.**
Before he goes down to the Works for the last time, Eldgard gives her the last page of Alistair's book: the beacon. "If they open it, you'll need to know what comes." He's naming his heir, and it isn't either son.

**31. The servants' stair. Morning.**
**She's the one who finds Ada,** the lamp smashed beside her. Then she sees Nixon on the landing above, with blood on his cuffs. She understands everything in one look. She sends the footman away, picks up the broken lamp, and tells the household that Mrs Hargrave fell. *(This is the aftermath Ada's death needs.)*
> **NIXON:** *(at the top of the stair)* Tamara—
> **TAMARA:** *(not looking up)* Go. Whatever you're doing, go now.

It's love: she saves him. It's power: she now holds the worst thing he's ever done. Both are true, forever.

**32. Harding House, the hall.**
Nathaniel comes for the keys and the Works plans. She gives him everything, except the beacon page in her pocket. She breaks the only vow she meant, and lets him go down there not knowing what he'll call.

## Episode 10: Lights Out

**33. The Lambrick depots, the East End. Night.**
London is dark. She opens the depots under her own name and gives the coal away by the list, family by family, on the streets her father paid for.

**34. The same street.**
Hector arrives too late.
> **HECTOR:** You took this from me.
> **TAMARA:** You taught me what grief costs. I only paid it forward.

**35. The final image.**
Across the frozen street, Nixon is leading the Guard for London. He's wearing the coat she relined. They see each other. Neither crosses.
