# The room pass: Episodes 4, 6, the front of 7, 9 and 10

**Claude's proposal. Not canon.** Episodes 1, 2, 3, 5 and 8 are left as written, and so is the back half of Ep 7 (from "You're not her"). Everything below is built to land on them.

---

## The room's rules for this pass

**Shape of an hour.** A teaser, four acts and a tag. Streaming doesn't need ad breaks, but the discipline still helps: every act ends on an *act-out* (a reversal, a threat or a choice), never on a resolution. Each act below is marked with its act-out.

**What each influence brings, in one line each.**
- **Brent Weeks:** power has a body cost (the nosebleed, the fingers), and loyalty is the blade that cuts deepest.
- **Sarah J. Maas:** found family (Alice, the Lost One, Jennie), longing delayed, and one earned moment where the heroine is plainly lethal.
- **Beau Willimon:** every scene is a negotiation, and the most powerful person in the room says the least.
- **George R. R. Martin:** lore comes through people's guilt, not exposition. Politics outlast the heroes. The warmest person is not safe.
- **Mike Truk (*The Lost Reavers*):** progression you can see. Alice visibly levels up through the season, one learned skill at a time, and the tech has rules.

**The four new frequencies you asked for.**
1. **The warm body: Jennie.** Funny without jokes. Raised by the Vykra, so she can't say a feeling out loud and does something useful instead. She makes pitiful choices. The audience loves her by Ep 6, which makes the riot in Ep 7 unbearable and the price in Ep 10 unbearable too.
2. **The Duke: tragic, then terrifying.** He's an astronomer, and almost impossible to find; he's at the old river Observatory after dark or nowhere. He holds the lore no Harding wrote down. His lie in Parliament kills his sister. In Ep 10 he makes the decision that buries the Hardings.
3. **Alice's aura moments.** Three moments where the audience goes *whoa*: Grise at knifepoint (Ep 6), Miriam's throw on the rooftop (Ep 7), and the knife fight with Josephine in the dark (Ep 10). One smaller one in Ep 9. Each one is a skill the show planted earlier.
4. **The price nobody saw coming.** Two prices, one for the hero and one for the audience (see Ep 10 and **Your calls** at the end).

**The world in use.** Crystal cores and collars, the Lanterns' leap-boosters, folding patrol wings, flash-nets and crystal bombs, the prison train and the glazed transfer tower, the Works' core guard and their ring-shields (Eldgard's ring is one), Nathaniel's gauntlet and cold-sink. There's one new piece of hardware, the vault-stair key, and it's only there because it solves a logic hole.

**New elements, kept to the minimum.**
- **The Duke's Observatory and its ledgers:** the 1780 star record, and his grandfather's notes from the Tyburn bench.
- **Byron's street:** the Harding butler was born on Gin Lane. That's why he's in the washhouse.
- **Jennie's hush money:** the reason Grise's man comes for her in the riot.
- **Ren takes Nathaniel's two fingers:** the wound is shown, and Ren comes back.
- **The vault-stair key:** Jamie's initiation stair from Ep 1, Josephine's way in.

**Cuts.**
- **The Ep 4 bullet "Tamara at the hearing":** it's covered by her track.
- **Tamara and Nixon's fight in Ep 7:** the Ep 8 ring box is the same break, done better. He already wears the coat from Ep 3, so nothing is lost.
- **Ep 7's crowded front half:** rebuilt as two acts in two places.

## Episode 4: The Hearing

**What the hour is about now:** Jamie opened the ornament himself. Why? That question is Nathaniel's engine from here to Ep 6. Around it, London learns the light is failing, and every lead is pushed to lie in public.

**Teaser.**
- **Cold open (canon):** Morgana teaches; Alistair: "You have my word."
- **Lambrick House, breakfast:** Tamara breaks the engineer's cipher in three lines. Hector folds the figures into his coat. **Act-out:** "Today the Hardings find out the lamps can be counted."

**Act One: three people looking for the same thing.**
- **Nixon's file.** The Frost Killer case is Nixon's. Frost "like gallowglass" sends him into the Newgate register, looking for anyone ever held over green frost. One name: Tobias Marsh, committed fifteen years ago for raving about "frost under Tyburn". *This is Nixon's errand to Newgate; it fixes the flag that he had no reason to go.*
- **Newgate (canon set piece).** The prison train up the chalk hill, the glazed tower, the relief guards gliding down on wings, the moving stair that every prisoner steps over on the beat. Marsh pinches his chin: "Hargrave." Nixon leaves not understanding.
- **The shard.** In the workshop, Nathaniel and Alice match the shard to a Works drawing: Jamie opened it himself. Nathaniel understands his Ep 2 lie hid the most important fact of the case. He shows the Lost One the exterior drawing and asks whether Jamie showed her this. She asks for time. He leaves the drawing with her.
- **Act-out:** Nathaniel, alone, says it out loud for the first time: "He let it out. *Why?*"

**Act Two: the hearing.** The big set piece.
- Westminster. Lamps in bronze crystal collars line the chamber. Hector lays out the smuggled figures, and the lamps dim on cue, a chamber full of faces turning up.
- The Harding seats: Nathaniel stands in for the family. Alice is two rows behind him as Josephine's girl, his shadow.
- **The Duke keeps his word:** a fault in the lines, temporary, being mended. He says it beautifully. He believes about half of it.
- **The card.** In the gallery Tamara slips Hector the card: *Ask the Duke how many days.* **Nobody sees it but Alice,** because she's a spy and watching hands is her job.
- Hector: "Your Grace, how many days did you tell your sister to lay in?" The Duke's silence is the loudest thing in the episode. The chamber applauds Hector; Tamara keeps her hands in her lap.
- **Act-out:** Alice leans to Nathaniel's ear: "The Lambrick girl wrote that question." Nathaniel looks up at a woman he's been promised to since lunch in Ep 1, and sees her for the first time. *This seeds the Ep 5 toast, and Alice doesn't yet know about the engagement.*

**Act Three: the cost of a public lie.**
- **The robing room.** Eldgard finds the Duke unpinning his chain of office. "You said it for Jamie." "I said it in front of the Lords, and they'll remember it longer than Jamie." "Will you say it again, if I need you?" The Duke, very gently: "Ask me when the lamps in my sister's street go out." He leaves by the river door. That night Eldgard's note comes back unopened. *The Duke is tragic, plus the plant for Ep 7.*
- **Ada and the spectacles (canon).** Nixon mentions the madman. Ada asks if he has his spectacles, wraps them, and asks him to deliver them by hand. Sunday, the crisp end of the joint.
- **The buttons (canon).** Alice reports to Josephine, fastens the pearl buttons and mentions the dyehouse stair and the blue. She tells Josephine about the Lambrick girl's card. Josephine files it.
- **Act-out:** Jennie at the laundry gate with Grise's terms. Bring your sister in to report, or Ajax gets your address.

**Act Four: the test.**
- **Jennie's terms.** She delivers the ultimatum in the worst way possible: "Grise says bring her in or Ajax gets your address. Also you've got lye on your face." Alice tells her she's a terrible messenger. "I'm a *good* messenger. It's a terrible message." *This is the first time we like Jennie.*
- **The orange.** In the drying loft before the test, Jennie hands the girl a stolen orange because she can't say anything else. The Lost One peels it in one long spiral, the way Miriam did on her birthday. Jennie has to leave the loft.
- **The hand-sign test (canon).** Daylight, a floor full of women, and Alice never leaves her sister's side. The sign. No answer. Grise smiles.
- **Jennie's first lie (canon).** Ordered to follow them home, she comes back: "Lost them in the fog." After the orange, we know why.
- **Grise sells to Lambrick (canon).**
- **Tag (canon):** Eldgard to Josephine. When the mother dies, they use the child.

## Episode 6: The Remembering

**What the hour is about now:** Nathaniel follows Jamie's last week and finds the reason. The reveal is something he works for, with the Duke as the key. Everyone else has a night that won't come back.

**Teaser.**
- **The Works meeting (canon, your scene).** Tamara is overruled about the Gin Lane branch.
- **The corridor (canon).** Eldgard hands her Alistair's book: "Can you?"
- **Act-out:** she turns the first page and the cipher is nothing she's seen before. She smiles.

**Act One: the safe return, and the tail.**
- **Breakfast at Harding House.** Nathaniel has been sleeping here since Ep 5. Eldgard is warm, and Nixon stands at the door. The tail is visible now, and polite. Neither friend mentions it.
- **The drawings.** *This replaces the unplotted "safe return".* Alice can't bring Nathaniel to the workshop, so she brings the workshop to him. She's in the house legitimately, as Josephine's girl. Upstairs, in the room where she waited in Ep 5, she gives him the Lost One's two drawings: the outside and the inside of the same wall. "She remembers being inside it." Their hands touch over the paper, longer than they need to.
- **Byron.** Nathaniel asks where Jamie went in his last week. Byron deflects, then he can't: "Master Jamie took the carriage to the Observatory. On the Tuesday."
- **Act-out:** Nathaniel looks at the drawings, then at the clock. The Observatory is only open after dark.

**Act Two: the Duke.**
- **Alice's report.** Josephine wants to know where Nathaniel goes. Alice gives her the detail she thinks is safe: the Observatory, the Duke. *This fixes the open beat.* Josephine goes very still. He's following Jamie's steps, which is exactly what she hired Alice to stop.
- **The Observatory, night.** A dome on the river, a brass orrery that turns by itself, the city's green glow on the underside of the clouds. The Duke, in a coat older than Nathaniel, says without turning round: "Your brother sat in that chair three days before he died."
- **The lore, told through guilt.**
  - **The star.** The Duke's great-grandfather's star ledger, 1780: *a star fell into the Harding woods and did not burn out. The woods were warm in December.*
  - **The bench.** His grandfather sat on the Tyburn bench that hanged the "rebels" from a village by those woods. They all said the same thing, a lady in the woods, and were hanged for saying it.
  - **The sketch.** A sketch from the ledger, of the thing that fell: a curved hull marked with ribs.
- "Jamie asked me what fell. I told him what I've told you. He went home and opened something. I've wondered ever since whether I killed him."
- **The trigger.** Nathaniel unfolds the Lost One's drawing of the inside wall. The hull curve in the 1780 sketch matches it rib for rib.
- **Act-out: imprint reveal #1.** It hits him in the Duke's chair: the whole 1873 night. The shadow, the eyes, the knowledge going into him, his father's lantern swinging to bury it. And, under it all, the machinery that holds a signal down. The Duke watches him come back. "He had that same look."

**Act Three: knives.**
- **Byron's truth.** Dawn. Nathaniel is back from the Observatory. "Byron. What's under the Works?" Thirty years of silence give out. "A woman. In a pod. Your father didn't make you clever, Master Nat. You were always enough." *The loyal butler chooses the boys he raised over the man who pays him. That's his whole arc, without a secret.*
- **Josephine and Grise (canon, sharpened).** Josephine comes to the laundry. "You were the one holding the knife. And you hired her sister." There's a witness to the bridge: Jennie. Josephine pays for her to be removed. Grise, who prefers cheap solutions, offers Jennie money to leave London that night.
- **Jennie takes the money.** It's a pitiful choice, and a human one. Then she doesn't leave. She goes to the Bounds room to say goodbye and can't. Instead she criticises Alice's knife grip, and ends up teaching her Miriam's one-handed throw: "Left foot *here*. She always cheated with the left foot." Alice can't land it. They laugh. Jennie still doesn't say goodbye. *This is the warmest scene in the episode, and it's also the setup for Ep 7.*
- **Alice at Grise's (aura moment #1).** That night Alice goes into Grise's ledger room the Vykra way: across the drying lines, around the bell wire, through the shutter. She's reading the bridge-night entry, which is in Grise's private marks and unreadable, when the lamp comes up. Grise is in the doorway. A heartbeat later Alice is behind her with the knife at her throat. Nobody saw her move.
  - Grise, at knifepoint, chooses cruelty: "Want to know what your sister paid to get in? Your stepfather. Sixteen. The river." Alice lets her go, because Grise has just won.
- **Act-out:** Alice walks home with it, and walks past the Bounds room to the workshop.

**Act Four: the last whole night.** The episode runs over two days: the Observatory is the first night, and this is the second.
- **Nathaniel slips the tail.** Over the roofs, the way he and Jamie used to. Nixon doesn't run after him; he walks. "You always went left at the chimneys." Nixon follows.
- **The night they stop circling (canon).** Alice confesses the contract: paid at first, never after. Nathaniel takes it like a blow and doesn't leave. The Lost One has cleared a place on the bench. Outside, Nixon stands in the snow, looking at a lit window. He decides.
- **The Harding library (canon).** The first line opens for Tamara. Ada sets the cup down very carefully.
- **Tag: Gin Lane washhouse (canon, your scene).** *Why Byron is there:* he was born on Gin Lane and goes home on his night off with the family's leftover bread. The East cut kills his own street. The master's ring saves the butler, and the master's order killed the butler's street. Byron is alive in green crystal with his eyes open.

## Episode 7, front half (up to "You're not her")

**Act One: the raid.**
- **Dawn.** Nixon's Lanterns come down on the workshop on leap-boosters, crystal collars blazing. Nathaniel gets the sisters out through the back.
- **Aura moment #2.** A Lantern lands on the roof above Alice, between her and the ladder. Miriam's throw, which she has failed her whole life and failed again last night with Jennie: left foot *here*. The Lantern goes off the roof into the coal heap. She stares at her own hand, exactly the way the Lost One did in the passage in Ep 2.
- **The flash-net (canon).** It drops over the Lost One. Nathaniel throws himself under it, her cold bursts to save him and takes his right hand, and her nose bleeds. From the roof, Nixon sees the Frost Killer freeze his best friend's hand.
- **Ren (new, short).** Nathaniel on Ren's table. Two fingers. Ren, flatly: "You'll learn to do it left-handed or you'll learn to ask for help." *The wound is on screen; the hand stays bandaged for the rest of the episode.*
- **Act-out:** the crowd has swallowed the Lost One.

**Act Two: Gin Lane.** One place, three stories.
- **Byron (canon).** The shell flakes away. Nell's bare feet. The rent tin. A relief clerk asks him for the names of the dead.
- **The Duke's sister.** The Duke comes into the frozen East in his own carriage, with no escort. He's looking for a name on the relief list. Byron, who has served him at Harding dinners for thirty years, finds it. She had laid in her coal. Then she heard her brother tell the Lords it would be mended in days, so she gave it to the street. *"She believed what I said in public."* He looks at the Harding notice blaming the Frost Killer, and walks away a different man. *This is the Ep 4 plant paying off, and the root of his Ep 10 decision.*
- **Byron's witness (canon).** The green came up through the floor before the girl arrived.
- **The riot (canon).** The Hardings blame the Frost Killer and the mob forms. Grise's man comes for Jennie, *because she took the money and stayed.* The Lost One, carried clear by the crowd, looks back, stops and comes back for her. Jennie has to take that hand. She can't look at her.
- **Tamara's terms (canon).** Coal tonight, the wedding on Saturday. "Your terms. Your carts."
- **Act-out:** Alice takes the girl back to the **Bounds room**, the only home left. *This fixes the "where is home" flag.*

From here it's your scenes. One insertion goes into your "That night":

- **Jennie's gut-wrench.** In the dark, Jennie is on the floor against the door and the girl has the chair. "Can I talk to her? Miriam. Just for a minute. You could just... be her." "I have what she remembered. I don't have her." "Then tell me one thing she remembered about me." The girl tells her: Miriam on the Narrows, laughing at Jennie for carrying a spare knife *and* a spare orange. Jennie laughs, then breaks. "She's gone, Jennie." By morning she has decided to tell Alice. *This is the motive for Ep 8's first scene.*

## Episode 9: The Break-out

**What the hour is about now:** they win their way in, and the truth beats them. Ep 9 ends in defeat, which is what gives Ep 10 something to do.

**Teaser.**
- **Cold open (canon):** 1873, the offspring's side.
- **The stair (canon):** Ada.
- **Tamara covers it (canon).** "Go. Whatever you're doing, go now."

**Act One: Newgate.** Your scenes, unchanged: the tower drops, Jennie gives her name again, and the hill face.
- **Act-out:** two men flying out of the chalk hill's shadow.

**Act Two: the way in.**
- **The boy's door (your scene).** Marsh stops the draw.
- **The dimming.** It's the first time all season the city goes dark by human hand. Across London the lamps sink to reserve, street by street: the Duke watching it from the Observatory dome, Tamara at a Harding House window, a hospital ward lit by candles.
- **The silt gallery.** It's the Hargrave boys' cleaning crawl, under the floor of the draw room, running toward the pod chamber.
- **The night porters (aura moment, small).** The Works' night porters are on Grise's books, because Grise sells to everyone. Alice gives them the Vykra hand-sign she failed to know in Ep 4. They look at the floor and open the gate. *That's a skill she earned.*
- **Act-out:** the pod chamber door. Inside, two pods. In one, Morgana, failing. The other is open and waiting. Strapped beside it with the second set of restraints from your Ep 8 tag is the Lost One.

**Act Three: the truth spoken aloud.**
- **Reveal #2 (canon).** Morgana to her child: both boys, both seeds. "I did what I was told." The Lost One looks at Alice: "Did I love you because I was made to?" Alice, cutting the straps: "Doesn't matter. I'm still here."
- **The lock.** Nathaniel's planted knowledge knows the lock, but his ruined hand can't work it alone. He's trying when the door opens.
- **Eldgard's confession (canon), with numbers.** He comes alone, unarmed, carrying the night ledger. Pneumatic slips are arriving as he speaks:
  - "Saint Bartholomew's: the children's ward is on candles. Four hours."
  - "Shadwell pumping station: stopped. The cellars on the river side are flooding."
- It sounds right, because it partly is. Every slip is a real death on a real clock.
- **Act-out:** Marsh, in the doorway, hears the numbers.

**Act Four: the reversal.**
- **Marsh's switch.** "I stopped it for an hour every cleaning day for twenty years. I'll not be the man who stops it for good." He winds the wheel back. The light comes roaring back through the floor. The man they broke out of prison to stop the machine restarts it. He's still the kept Hargrave.
- **Nixon's own Lanterns** come through the door with Eldgard's order: arrest the captain.
- **"Did you love me, or did you keep me?" "Yes." (canon).**
- Nixon holds the silt gallery so Nathaniel and Alice can get out, and is taken by his own men.
- Alice won't leave her. The Lost One, from the restraints: **"Nine is the one where we get up. Get up, Alice."** Alice goes.
- **Tag.** Eldgard closes the second pod. Across London every lamp flares brighter than it has in a century. In the ruins of Gin Lane people come out into the street and *cheer* the light. **London has never been so bright.**

## Episode 10: Lights Out

**What the hour is about now:** the season's question gets answered by four people at once: keeping someone, or loving them.

**Teaser.**
- **Cold open (canon):** Alistair closes the pod. The broken word.
- **Dawn in a blazing city.** Two lives are being drained now. Morgana is dying faster, and the Lost One's nose won't stop bleeding inside the glass.
- **Act-out:** the engineer reads the yield and goes pale. At this rate, both are dead by tonight.

**Act One: the Duke's decision.** This is the coffin.
- **The Observatory.** The Duke looks at the blazing city through the glass. "Light from a star is old news. You see what it was, not what it is."
- **The Lords, at noon.** He reads three documents into the record:
  - his grandfather's Tyburn warrants
  - the 1780 star ledger
  - his sister's last letter: *you said days, so I gave it away*
- He moves to revoke the Harding Charter and return the Guard to the Crown. Hector seconds. The Lords also grant Lambrick the city's emergency heat. *Willimon: the mildest man in London ends a century-old dynasty with a reading.*
- **The Guard cells.** The Duke releases Nixon himself: "The Guard needs a captain who knows what it is to be kept."
- **Act-out:** the freight route is sealed and the silt gallery has been flooded. There's no way back into the Works.

**Act Two: the way down.**
- **Alice and Josephine (aura moment #3).** There's one other way in: the private vault stair from Harding House, the stair Eldgard took Jamie down in Ep 1. Josephine holds its second key.
  - Alice goes to her with the knife she didn't use in Ep 8.
  - Josephine was Vykra before Alice was born. In the dark dressing room, two trained bodies fight: Josephine's old training against Alice's new one. It's ugly and fast. Alice wins with the throw.
  - Knife at the throat. "Did my sister see you coming?" "I chose you so I'd have to look at you." Josephine knew from the first day.
  - Alice spares her. Jennie's testimony is already on record; the law can have her.
  - Josephine gives her the key. "Jamie went down with one just like it."
- *This fixes the logic flag: the confrontation is now on the path to the pod, not a detour away from it.*
- **Act-out:** Nathaniel and Alice at the top of Jamie's stair. It's the shot from Ep 1, with different people.

**Act Three: the lock.**
- **The vault.** The core guard comes at them with ring-shields. Nixon's Guard, now the Crown's, arrives and holds the door against them: Lanterns against Lanterns.
- Eldgard is at the pods.
- **The lock (canon).** The planted knowledge, his ruined hand, and Alice's hand on it too. They open it together.
- **Price one: the hero's.** As the beacon releases, the knowledge goes out of Nathaniel the same way it came in, in 1873. The seed goes back. Next morning he will not be able to read his own drawings.
- **Morgana's vengeance.** She doesn't kill Eldgard. She *keeps* him. The emptied pod's last cold seals him in green crystal, alive, eyes open: the Byron shell, for ever. "Now you know."
- **Act-out:** London goes dark.

**Act Four: the first night.**
- **The dark kills (canon).** On screen. Eldgard was partly right.
- **The ship (canon).** Called by the beacon, it comes through the cloud and does real damage. Morgana boards it.
- **The depots (canon).** Tamara opens them under her own name. Hector: "You took this from me." "You taught me what grief costs."
- **The coat (canon).** Across the street, Nixon leads the Guard for London in her coat. Neither crosses. *This comes before the final image now, which fixes the double-ending flag.*
- **Price two: the audience's.** Dawn. Alice and the Lost One reach the dyehouse. Jennie is on the floor against the door, the way she sat on the Lost One's first night. Under her coat, three children from the stair, alive. Jennie isn't.
  - *Why it's right:* it's what a warm person does in a world that punishes warmth. It makes "Eldgard was partly right" cost the person we loved. And it's Alice's hand that opened the pod.
- **She stays (canon).** Alice offers her the ship. The Lost One looks up, then at Alice, and stays. Morgana lets the ship leave in silence.
- **Final image:** the Lost One's hand finds Alice's in the cold.

## Your calls: these change canon

1. **Jennie dies in Ep 10.** This is the biggest one. It's the price that makes the audience pay. If you'd rather keep her, Byron is the alternative: the butler who chose the boys dies in the first dark night.
2. **Nathaniel loses the planted genius** when the pod opens. It gives Season 2 an engineer who can't build.
3. **Ep 9 ends in defeat:** Marsh's switch, Nixon arrested by his own men, and the Lost One sealed in the second pod.
4. **The Duke revokes the Charter.** This makes "Lambrick wins" political as well as practical.
5. **Morgana keeps Eldgard** instead of killing him.
6. **Cut** Tamara and Nixon's Ep 7 fight.
7. **Insert** Jennie's "Can I talk to her?" into your Ep 7 "That night".
