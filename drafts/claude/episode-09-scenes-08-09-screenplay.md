# THE GREEN GALLOWS
## Episode 9: "The Break-out"
### Scenes 8 & 9 — Screenplay Draft

*Draft from a friend's pass, not canon. Checked against canon on 22 Nov: "Suction Core" and the cautery/laudanum 1873 (both cut) were replaced with the pod and the canon lantern. The scene breakdown in `episodes/episode-09.md` (scenes 8–9, "Both boys, both seeds" and "Yes") is the story of record.*

**CHARACTERS:**
- **ELDGARD HARDING (58):** Patriarch. Unarmed, carrying the night ledger. The terrifying calm of utilitarian power.
- **NATHANIEL HARDING (21):** The son who was hollowed out. Arm in a sling, holding a pry-bar left-handed.
- **NIXON HARGRAVE (23):** Lantern-Captain in rebellion. Greatcoat torn, Ada's fresh blood dried on his cuffs.
- **ALICE BOUNDS (20):** Knife in hand, sawing through leather restraints. Fierce, desperate.
- **THE LOST ONE (Immortal, wearing Miriam Bounds):** Strapped to the gurney, trembling as the alien telepathy tears through her host.
- **MORGANA (Ancient entity in the pod):** Emaciated, chained in green brine, her voice echoing in every skull.

```fountain
INT. THE POD CHAMBER, UNDER THE WORKS - SUNDAY NOON

A cathedral of iron, green verdigris, and sweating subterranean clay fifty feet beneath Tyburn.

In the center of the chamber, rising four stories high, stands THE POD: a cylinder of four-inch lead crystal filled with glowing, turbulent emerald fluid.

Inside floats MORGANA. 

Chained by iron collars at neck, waist, and ankles. Hundreds of needle-fine copper lines pierce her skin, siphoning luminous fluid into the city's pressurized mains. 

With the cleaning wheel locked below by Marsh, the pumps have stopped chugging. The green brine is still. For the first time in four hundred years, the fluid clears.

On a steel gurney thirty feet below the tank lies THE LOST ONE, buckled into heavy leather restraints.

ALICE kneels over the gurney, furiously sawing at the reinforced leather wrist strap with her skinning knife. Her knuckles are bleeding.

Nathaniel stands guard at the foot of the gurney, his ruined right hand strapped across his chest in a dark sling, an iron pry-bar gripped in his left fist.

NIXON stands ten paces back at the base of the iron gantry, his service revolver drawn, facing the upper catwalks where six CORE GUARDS hold their crystal pulse-rifles level.

Suddenly, a LOW, SUBSONIC VIBRATION hums through the floorplates.

Morgana’s eyes open behind the four-inch glass.

They do not look human. They are boundless, swirling nebulae of pure white auroral fire.

A voice blooms inside every skull in the room. Not sound traveling through air, but cold silver needles pressing directly against their temporal lobes.

MORGANA (V.O.)
(telepathic; ancient, melodic, terrible)
My child has done what she was born to do.

The Lost One arches her back against the steel table, a silent gasp tearing through her throat.

MORGANA (V.O.) (CONT'D)
Both boys. Both seeds planted in the dark sixteen winters ago. The elder brother taught to resist, so his bleeding fingers would break the brass... and the younger brother given the eye of geometry, so he would know how to turn the key.

Alice doesn't stop sawing. *Shhk. Shhk. Shhk.*

ALICE
(through gritted teeth)
Shut her out, Mim. Don't listen to her.

MORGANA (V.O.)
And the woman on the river bridge... the dying shell you chose to put on like a garment... do you believe your devotion was your own, little one?

The Lost One turns her cyan eyes toward Alice. 

Silver ichor weeps from her tear ducts, freezing instantly into diamond trails down her cheeks.

THE LOST ONE
(voice trembling with Miriam's timbre)
Alice...
(a choked, mechanical breath)
Did I love you because I was made to? Was my heart just a frequency she tuned?

Alice cuts through the left wrist buckle. The leather snaps. 

She grabs the Lost One’s cold, silver-stained face with both hands, forcing the girl to look at her.

ALICE
Look at me! Look at my eyes! I don't give a damn whose plan it was! Miriam gave you her last breath, and you came back for me! You’re mine, do you hear me? You're mine!

A heavy iron latch CLANKS above them.

The heavy vault security door swings open.

Down the iron gantry stair steps ELDGARD HARDING.

He wears no armor. He carries no weapon. Under his left arm, he holds the brass-bound leather volume of the Works night ledger. 

His face is carved from pale Berkshire granite.

ELDGARD
Hold your fire, Captain.

The Core Guards on the catwalks freeze, fingers off their triggers.

Eldgard reaches the bottom of the stair, stopping fifteen paces from Nathaniel. He doesn't look at the gun in Nixon’s hand. He looks at his surviving son.

ELDGARD (CONT'D)
Alistair built the lock to answer one mind, Nathaniel. An acoustic harmonic that no mortal craftsman could fathom. I put that mind in you when you were five years old.
(a beat; his voice drops)
I am sorry for how it was done.

Nathaniel’s knuckles turn white around his pry-bar. His chest heaves.

NATHANIEL
You swung a lantern over a five-year-old boy and told him he'd dreamed it. You erased my childhood so you could use me as a key.

ELDGARD
(unflinching, serene)
Yes. Because without that key, London freezes to the bone.

Eldgard steps forward, opening the night ledger. He taps the columned ink with a clean, manicured finger.

ELDGARD (CONT'D)
In 1840, before the gallowglass mains reached the river wards, sixty-four thousand children under the age of five died of freezing lung and cholera in this city every single January. The soot was so thick that horses choked in the Strand at noon.
(he looks at Nathaniel, then at Alice)
This entity—this miracle from the sky—gives us light that does not burn, heat that does not choke, and an empire that commands the globe.
Do you know what her price is, Nathaniel? One life every fifty years. One Harding life to calm her mind, and one immortal to bear the burden.

Eldgard closes the ledger with a soft, final *thud*.

ELDGARD (CONT'D)
At five o'clock tonight, the temperature across the Thames will reach twenty below zero. If the transfer is not completed, the children's ward at Saint Bartholomew's goes onto tallow candles before dusk. By midnight, the Shadwell drainage pumps seize, and two hundred thousand families in the river cellars drown in frozen sewage.
Show me a King, show me a Parliament, show me a Church that has ever bought civilization for three million souls at a cheaper price than House Harding.

The silence in the vault is suffocating.

Eldgard’s argument hangs in the cold air—monstrous, logical, and terrifyingly true.

Nathaniel stares at his father. His knees tremble. The horrific realization settles into his stomach: his father is not mad. He is a mathematician of slaughter.

Then, heavy footsteps ring on the iron plates.

NIXON steps out from the shadows of the gantry.

His dress uniform is torn at the collar. His eyes are hollow, bloodshot, burning with a madness of grief. On his cuffs, dark and sticky, is the fresh blood of Ada Hargrave.

Nixon raises his revolver, pointing it directly at Eldgard’s chest.

NIXON
And the Hargraves, Eldgard?
(his voice cracks, raw and ragged)
My mother, dying on the floorboards with a bowl of ice while you built your pipes? Marsh rotting forty years in the Newgate chalk? Ada... washing your linen until her knuckles turned to chalk?

Nixon takes two steps closer. The gun doesn't shake.

NIXON (CONT'D)
Did you love me, Eldgard?
(a tear cuts through the soot on Nixon's cheek)
Did you raise me at your right hand because I was family? Or did you just keep me as a spare when Jamie failed?

Eldgard looks into the barrel of the revolver. 

He looks up into the eyes of the boy he taught to ride, the boy he pinned with an officer’s crest, the boy who carried his secrets for five years.

Eldgard does not blink. He does not flinch.

ELDGARD
Yes.

A single syllable.

It hits the room with the force of an executioner's drop.

Nixon lowers the hammer of the gun by a fraction of an inch, his mouth open, hollowed out from the inside.

Nathaniel steps forward, stepping between Nixon and his father. He looks at Eldgard with the agony of a lifetime of neglect:

NATHANIEL
And me?
(his voice barely a whisper)
All those years at the far end of the dining table. Looking down the hall at Jamie. Looking at Nixon in your chair. Did you ever see a son, Father? Or did you just see the lock?

Eldgard looks at Nathaniel.

For the first time in sixty years, the mask of the patriarch slips. 

Eldgard’s mouth parts. His eyes soften with something ancient, vulnerable, and deeply human—the look of a dying man about to speak the truth.

Before the word can form on his lips—

**CRACK.**

A sound like an artillery shell detonating in an enclosed room.

Morgana’s flat, blue-skinned palm slams violently against the inside of the four-inch crystal cylinder.

An ultrasonic screech rips through the chamber, shattering the gallery lamps overhead in a cascade of white sparks.

Down the center of the four-inch lead crystal pane, A HAIRLINE FRACTURE explodes across the glass, racing from rim to base like a bolt of jagged lightning.

Green fluid begins to hiss through the fissure in high-pressure jets of toxic steam.

Eldgard jerks around, the truth dying in his throat, the patriarch instantly reasserting command:

ELDGARD (CONT'D)
(shouting to the guards)
Restart the draw! Now! Before she has the strength to finish it!

CUT TO BLACK.
```
