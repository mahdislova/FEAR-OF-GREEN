# The Green Gallows

A ten-episode drama. London, 1889. The whole city glows green, and the light is a living being kept in a freezer by a family that broke its word.

## The story of record

The current story lives in three folders and nowhere else:

- `canon/`: the world, the rules, the season shape, the timeline and who knows what when
- `characters/`: one file per character
- `episodes/`: one file per episode

A short version for newcomers is in `handoff/` (read `01-READ-FIRST.md`, then `02-STORY-BIBLE.md`, then `03-SEASON-AND-DARTBOARD.md`). A visual overview is `tracker/green-gallows-season-tracker.html`, generated from canon.

**Key facts of the current version** (if anything you read disagrees, it's out of date):
- **Jamie Harding dies in Episode 1.** The starving, half-mad offspring ravages him when he frees it; Nathaniel finds him bleeding out. There is no secret room and no coma.
- **Josephine Harding killed Miriam** on Blackwood Bridge. The audience learns it in Episode 2; Alice learns it in Episode 8, after Alice returns.
- **Nixon first appears in Episode 2,** back from the east to report village after village frozen in ice.
- **The Lost One killed Jamie** and doesn't remember it; she tells Nathaniel and Alice together in Episode 7. Nixon discovers Nathaniel hid the suspected Frost Killer in Episode 5 and hears confirmation before his Episode 8 raid.

## Not the story

`archive/` holds the old season and every superseded draft. It is kept for reference only. **Where it conflicts with canon, canon wins.**
