# Episode 3 — The Frost Killer

**Cold open:** Before dawn, three independent teams deliver Hector’s coal to one eastern siding. One wagon comes back with something more valuable than the freight.
**Change:** Nathaniel shelters the woman London suspects of killing Jamie. **POV:** Nixon and Nathaniel, with Alice and the Lost One inside the hunt.
- Jennie keeps her bridge account to herself. Alice takes the Lost One along to keep her promise to Grise. Moss recognizes Alice from the Narrows raid.
- Nixon and Tamara meet before his shift. She asks for time to tell Hector about them. The coal deliveries put Hector in touch with the Works engineer ahead of the hearing.
- Moss seizes Alice. The girl uses Miriam’s familiar throw, then stays in contact one beat too long. Moss dies. Alice witnesses the whole encounter.
- Nixon clears the market under the paper stars. Ajax abandons his search for the stolen cargo to pursue the girl who froze his man.
- Nathaniel opens his workshop and conceals the sisters from Nixon. Alice gives Grise a true but incomplete account. The girl’s first words are for Nathaniel.

## Scenes

### 1. The eastern siding

One of the carriers refuses to tip his load into the snow until someone signs for it. A Works engineer gets down on the wagon bed and shovels the first row himself so the frozen men at the furnace can get at it. He signs the receipt and asks the carrier to take a folded sheet directly to whoever sent the coal. The carrier puts it inside his shirt. His westbound wagon leaves empty.

### 2. Jennie on the threshold, morning

Jennie has stayed the night in the chair by the door. Alice brings her yesterday’s untouched cup and asks why she was frightened. Jennie admits she saw Miriam go over the bridge. Alice asks who else was there. Jennie says she ran. She did run; Alice hears the answer she needed to hear. The girl brings Jennie her coat when she stands up. Being helped by those hands is more than Jennie can bear. She leaves for her shift.

### 3. Before Nixon’s shift

Tamara meets him at the hour she gave him in the yard. She puts her arms around him as soon as he shuts the door. He offers to walk her back. She checks the street first. He asks whether the uniform is the trouble, or what a man earns in it. She loves him and would like her father to hear it from her. He agrees to give her the time. He walks out first, pleased enough to turn and wave to the blank upstairs window. She already knows what Hector has arranged.

### 4. Grise’s errand

Alice can reach the laundry by way of the covered market. When she puts on her coat the girl is already waiting at the door. At the laundry Alice leaves her in the yard in sight through the open door. Grise wants a first introduction to Nathaniel. Today he collects his Exhibition prototype from the market instrument mender. Find out whether he means to rebuild it. Alice can answer that much without inviting Grise into anyone’s bedroom. She takes the job money. The girl watches her put it away.

### 5. The mender’s stall

Nathaniel has brought the shard from Jamie’s study wrapped inside his instrument roll. He collects the repaired gauntlet. Once the mender’s customer leaves, he asks if she recognizes the shard. She knows the curved edge was made to fit a housing; the Works would have the drawing. It goes back into his roll. Across the aisle Alice watches Nathaniel linger after paying for the repair. She misses her easy chance to introduce herself while he’s alone.

### 6. Moss finds a thief

On their way down the aisle Alice ducks a low paper star and walks straight into the next. The lamp boy laughs from the ironwork. A trader pays him his closing penny to bring the lights down later. At the next crossing Alice says ‘wait’; the girl stops on the word, before Alice reaches for her. Moss sees Alice. She was there when his employer’s Narrows cargo went missing. He follows her out of sight of the mender. His orders were to watch for a buyer. He thinks catching a thief will do him more good.

### 7. The killing

Moss catches Alice by her coat and hauls her back into the passage. He wants the load or its price. Alice tells the girl to wait. This time she understands and moves anyway. She puts him on the floor as she did the boy at the quay. He still has Alice by the collar. The girl holds his wrist until his fingers open. When Alice can breathe again Moss is dead. Frost has run beneath his sleeve. The girl waits for him to sit up as the boy did yesterday. Alice has to take her away.

### 8. The second body

Nixon reaches the body with the market watch. A porter who carried Jamie out of the study recognizes the rime along the cuff; he tells his neighbour before Nixon can take him aside. By the next aisle the two deaths have one killer in the telling. Nixon has an account of a struggle, a dead man and a witness who saw the sisters leave. He orders his Lanterns to bring both in alive. Ajax identifies Moss. His runner takes word to Hector: a woman left the same frost they see on gallowglass fittings. Hector’s answer is to bring him the woman. The consignment can wait.

### 9. Closing time

Nixon finds two hundred people pushing at shut gates. He opens both. His Lanterns leap to the iron galleries and light the aisles from above. The crush eases. Ajax looks up from the sisters’ trail and finds Nixon looking straight at him across the glass roof. Two open gates pour shoppers out past Ajax’s men. He can still pick out the wet mark from the girl’s hand along the stall rail. Then the iron runs out and the trail goes with it.

### 10. A penny for the stars

Alice pays the lamp boy to call closing early. The stars come down around the shoppers’ heads. From the gallery Nixon loses sight of the women. Below, the girl understands Alice’s ‘walk’ and keeps pace beside her. Nathaniel, caught at the service exit by the crowd, sees them pass. He also sees Ajax step into their way at the street end. He opens the little gate into his workshop court. The girl has frost all over her hand. He holds the gate until both women are inside.

### 11. Nixon at the gate

Nixon knows that court; he grew up coming here. He asks Nathaniel which way the two women went. Behind the gate Alice has one hand on the girl’s coat, ready to run. Nathaniel tells him he saw the women pass the court and keep going. Nixon sends the patrol on. He has trusted the same friend twice in two days. Nathaniel hears the order carried down the line before he takes either woman inside.

### 12. One finger

The girl cannot uncurl her hand. Nathaniel offers the prototype to Alice, who waits for her sister’s assent before touching her. One finger first. The crystal takes the cold. Alice tells him Moss still had her by the throat when he died. Nathaniel wants to ask about Jamie. Looking at the mute girl waiting for permission to touch her own sister, he puts that question away for tonight.

### 13. What the coal bought

The carrier finds Tamara at the depot. The folded sheet contains the chief engineer’s actual yield figures and an offer to authenticate them privately. Men are freezing beside furnaces his employer records as running. He wants the figures heard in Parliament. Tamara brings Hector the sheet herself. Their coal is already keeping the furnaces going. Tomorrow Hector can ask why the city had to buy it. The engineer’s name stays off his public copy.

### 14. The broken chair

While Nathaniel sits with the girl, Alice slips out by the court’s other gate. She leaves a message at the laundry’s usual collection point: the gauntlet works. She leaves out whose hand was in it. When she returns, the only chair that doesn’t squeak is missing a leg. Alice tries it, lands in her sister’s lap and announces it takes two to sit in this house. The girl laughs first. Later Nathaniel comes back with blankets. She studies his face. ‘I know you.’ He stops in the doorway. Alice has waited since the river to hear her speak, and the first person she recognizes aloud is him.
