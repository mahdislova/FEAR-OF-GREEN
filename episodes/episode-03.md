# Episode 3 — The Frost Killer

**Cold open:** open (the 1873 fragment moved to Ep 2).

**Change:** Nathaniel takes a side. **POV:** Nixon and Nathaniel.
- Before his shift Nixon meets Tamara privately. She embraces him the moment the door closes. He wants to walk her home openly. He asks whether his pay and his place in the household make him someone she can only see here. She loves him. She asks for time to speak to Hector herself. She knows about the Harding arrangement and lets Nixon leave believing the timing is still theirs to choose.
- The Lost One kills a man who attacks Alice. Her cold, by touch. London names a Frost Killer, and blames Jamie's death on it.
- Nixon opens the Christmas market gates and puts the Lantern Guard overhead. Alice buys the sisters a way out under the descending paper stars. Ajax hunts too, for Lambrick, who has heard the killer leaves frost like gallowglass.
- Nathaniel finds the sisters and hides them in his workshop. His tech can manage her cold. First lie to Nixon.
- The only chair that does not squeak is missing a leg. Alice tries it, drops neatly into her sister’s lap and, after a beat, announces that it takes two to sit in this house. The Lost One laughs before Alice does. They spend a minute trying out the other furniture with elaborate caution. It is their first joke since the river.
- **Tag:** the Lost One studies Nathaniel's face: "I know you."

## Scenes

### Closing time at the covered market

Before the killing, Alice ducks a paper star and walks straight into the next one. Up in the roof a boy laughs. He tends the star lamps and takes a penny from each trader to lower the whole canopy at closing.

After the killing Nixon finds two hundred people pushing at shut gates. He opens both. His Lanterns leap to the iron galleries and light the aisles from above. The crush eases. Ajax looks up from the sisters’ trail and finds Nixon looking straight at him across the glass roof.

Alice shows the lamp boy a penny. He lowers the stars. From the gallery the lamps fill the frame. From underneath we can see two women walking slowly enough to keep the paper still. They gain one street. At the workshop door it is Nathaniel’s turn to decide.

### One finger

The Lost One cannot uncurl her hand. Nathaniel offers his prototype to Alice, who asks her sister before touching her. One finger first. The crystal takes the cold. In Episode 7 both women will recognize the device when Nathaniel reaches for it.
