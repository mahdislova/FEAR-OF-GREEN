# Episode 3 — The Frost Killer

**Cold open:** open (the 1873 fragment moved to Ep 2).

**Change:** Nathaniel takes a side. **POV:** Nixon and Nathaniel.
- Morning in the Bounds room. Still in disbelief, Jennie tells Alice to keep it a secret that Miriam is back. Alice asks her why. Jennie can't say, and leaves. Grise swore Miriam ran off and left her; Miriam is in Alice's bed. Grise lied.
- Alice leaves the girl with Ren and goes to the laundry alone. She plays the grieving initiate and takes her first envelope: Nathaniel Harding. "Any word of my sister?" "Still gone, love." Now Alice knows Grise is lying to her face, and Grise doesn't know she knows. She says nothing. She takes the job to stay inside long enough to learn what Grise had to do with the bridge.
- The girl slips away from Ren the way a child slips a sitter, pulled toward Alice. She finds her at the market, where Alice is stalking Nathaniel. Alice yelps, "Hey, what are you doing here?" The girl holds up her hand: "Food." At the market the girl follows a simple spoken instruction from Alice for the first time. Her first clear words come in the workshop tag.
- Before his shift Nixon meets Tamara privately. She embraces him the moment the door closes. He wants to walk her home openly. He asks whether his pay and his place in the household make him someone she can only see here. She loves him. The engagement letter is in her glove. When he asks about walking her home openly, her fingers go to it, and stay there. She asks for time to speak to Hector herself instead. She knows about the Harding arrangement and lets Nixon leave believing the timing is still theirs to choose.
- Lambrick's men attack Alice. The girl realises she has to protect her, and the cold breaks out of her: an ice outburst that kills several of Lambrick's men and some civilians, impaling bodies nine feet in the air. London names a Frost Killer, and blames Jamie's death on it.
- **Hector pays the widows.** At first light he stands in Gin Lane in the snow with Ajax and a strongbox and pays every family the ice touched, not only his own men's. The Hardings send no one. Tamara holds the ledger. "You're paying for the Hardings' monster." "I'm paying for the street's memory. In a month they'll need coal. They'll remember who came." "And if they ask why you came?" "Grief, my dear. It's the cheapest thing I'll ever buy."
- **Eldgard names the monster.** Nixon reports the market killings. Eldgard drafts the Guard's public statement himself on the family's paper: "The Frost Killer." "We don't know it's the same—" "The frost in my son's study. The frost at the market. Londoners need one name for what they're afraid of, Captain, or they'll find their own. Sign it as the officer in charge." Nixon signs: the first time his name goes under something he doesn't know is true. The name London uses is Eldgard's.
- Word reaches Grise from the street: Miriam Bounds is alive, and was at the market killing men with ice. Alice sat across from her that morning and said nothing. Grise realises Alice is playing her.
- Nixon opens the Christmas market gates and puts the Lantern Guard overhead. Alice buys the sisters a way out under the descending paper stars. Ajax hunts too, for Lambrick, who has heard the killer leaves frost like gallowglass.
- Nathaniel finds the sisters and hides them in his workshop. His tech can manage her cold.
- **The first lie to Nixon: the wrong street.** At the Guard post that night, Nixon gratefully shows Nathaniel the search map. Nathaniel reads it like an engineer and points the frost along the old drains toward Gin Lane. "That's where I'd look." Twenty men move to Gin Lane; Clerkenwell stays open. "I don't know what I'd do without you tonight." "Neither do I."
- The only chair that does not squeak is missing a leg. Alice tries it, drops neatly into her sister’s lap and, after a beat, announces that it takes two to sit in this house. The Lost One laughs before Alice does. They spend a minute trying out the other furniture with elaborate caution. It is their first joke since the river.
- **Tamara's track.**
  - *The rented room (canon, sharpened):* instead of telling Nixon about the engagement, she gives him his Guard greatcoat relined in grey wool. "I can sew. Nobody's ever asked me to." "Walk with me on Sunday." *(buttoning it on him)* "Give me a little time."
  - *Gin Lane, dawn (canon):* she writes each widow's name in the ledger as Hector pays.
  - *Her room, night:* she copies every name and address into a small book of her own. Nobody asked her to.
- **Tag:** the Lost One studies Nathaniel's face: "I know you."

## Scenes

### Closing time at the covered market

Before the killing, Alice ducks a paper star and walks straight into the next one. Up in the roof a boy laughs. He tends the star lamps and takes a penny from each trader to lower the whole canopy at closing.

After the killing Nixon finds two hundred people pushing at shut gates. He opens both. His Lanterns leap to the iron galleries and light the aisles from above. The crush eases. Ajax looks up from the sisters’ trail and finds Nixon looking straight at him across the glass roof.

Alice shows the lamp boy a penny. He lowers the stars. From the gallery the lamps fill the frame. From underneath we can see two women walking slowly enough to keep the paper still. They gain one street. At the workshop door it is Nathaniel’s turn to decide.

### One finger

The Lost One cannot uncurl her hand. Nathaniel offers his prototype to Alice, who asks her sister before touching her. One finger first. The crystal takes the cold. In Episode 7 both women will recognize the device when Nathaniel reaches for it.
