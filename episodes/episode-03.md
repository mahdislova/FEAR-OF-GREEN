# Episode 3 — The Frost Killer

**Clock:** THE LIGHT HAS 42 DAYS · 9 November 1889

**Cold open:** 1873. A child's room, green light at the window. A fragment.

**Change:** Nathaniel takes a side without knowing it.  
**Governing question:** Who do you protect when the law, your friend and your father all want the same girl?  
**POV:** Nixon and Nathaniel  
**Set piece:** The Christmas crystal market: the tree sings, the hunt, the rooftop escape  
**Heaviest moment:** Nathaniel builds a cold-sink for the Lost One's wrist with his hands knowing what his head doesn't.  
**Tag:** The Lost One studies Nathaniel's sleeping face: "I know you."

## Scenes

### 1. Watch the light

*Cold open · Harding House, a nursery. 1873. Night.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Setup

A child's bed under a sloped ceiling; a tin soldier on the sill. **NATHANIEL, FIVE**, wakes to green light on the wallpaper, moving like water. Something is at the window, on the inside of the glass. Not a face. A brightness, small, the size of a child, with the shape of one held only loosely, the way breath holds the shape of a word.

It comes to the bed. It puts something that might be a hand on his forehead. We hear a sound like a struck bell under water.

Then a man's silhouette in the doorway, a lantern swinging from his hand on its chain. Slowly. *Tick. Tick.* We never see his face.

> **MAN'S VOICE:** *(gentle, very slow)* Watch the light, Nathaniel. Watch it swing. It was a dream. It was only ever a dream.
>
> *(The boy's eyes follow the lantern. Left. Right. Left. Closing.)*

**Exit state:** Before: Nathaniel's 'nightmare' was a character note. After: the audience knows something visited him, and that someone made him forget.

**Craft:** A two-reading reveal (guide §4): the full meaning lands in Episode 6. Withholding the father's face lets the audience suspect before they know.

### 2. The stair

*Act One · Gin Lane, the Bounds stair. Night, five days after the funeral.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Turn

A man waits on the dark stair: **MOSS**, one of Ajax's, a big soft-spoken docker with a cosh up his sleeve and a description in his head: *small, hair cut with a knife, cut Mr Fink.* He takes Alice by the collar as she comes up and has her against the wall before she can draw.

The Lost One comes out of the room behind him. She puts her bare hand flat on the back of his neck. That's all.

Frost goes through him like a crack through a pond, *krrrrk*. His skin goes white, then blue, then glassy. He doesn't fall; he stops, a man-shaped statue, ice ferning up the wall behind him in the exact pattern of gallowglass. Alice slides out from under his hand. The Lost One looks at her own palm, appalled and fascinated.

> **MOSS:** Mr Fink sends his regards, love—
>
> **ALICE:** *(staring at the statue)* What did you do?
>
> **THE LOST ONE:** He was hurting you.
>
> **ALICE:** What did you *do*?
>
> **THE LOST ONE:** I felt too much.

**Exit state:** Before: the sisters were hiding a sickness. After: they're hiding a killing, and they have to leave Gin Lane tonight.

**Craft:** The first kill is self-defence for someone else: protection again, in its most dangerous form. A move with a reason and a cost (atlas §5): they lose their home in one scene.

### 3. FROST KILLER

*Act One · Gin Lane, the stair. Morning.* · Thread: Nixon & the kept Hargraves · Turn, Setup

The Lantern Guard in grey greatcoats on the stair; a crowd held back with rifles; a newsboy already shouting it. Nixon crouches by the frozen man. He's seen this frost before, on a study carpet, in the room where he was handed a ring of keys. He touches it with a gloved finger and the glove goes stiff.

Peg the mudlark pushes to the front: she saw a drowned lady sit up in the river last week, she says, in the same kind of ice. Nobody listens to her but Nixon.

> **NEWSBOY:** FROST KILLER! FROST KILLER HAS STRUCK AGAIN! *Same as the Harding heir!*
>
> **SERGEANT:** Newgate, sir? Or the Met?
>
> **NIXON:** Neither. Anything we find, anyone we take, comes to Harding House. My order.
>
> **SERGEANT:** Sir, that's not—
>
> **NIXON:** It's Lord Harding's order.

**Exit state:** Before: Jamie's death was a burglary. After: London has a monster and has linked it to the Harding heir, and Nixon is running a private hunt for his father.

**Craft:** The institution does work (atlas lesson 6): routing an arrest away from Newgate is illegal, and Nixon knows it. That's the first crack in his obedience.

### 4. Because it's his

*Act One · Lambrick House, the billiard room.* · Thread: Lambrick / coal · Turn

Lambrick in shirtsleeves at the table, the green baize lit by coal-gas, not gallowglass: the only room in Mayfair that is. Ajax at the window, angry for the first time we've seen. Moss was his.

> **AJAX:** Froze him on the stair like a joint in a cold-house.
>
> **LAMBRICK:** Frost. Like the Harding boy. *(he pots a ball, click)* And Harding's hunting it with his own Guard instead of the Met. Why would a man hunt a murderer privately, Ajax?
>
> **AJAX:** Because it's his.
>
> **LAMBRICK:** Find it first.

**Exit state:** Before: Lambrick wanted a scandal. After: he suspects the killer belongs to the Hardings, and he wants to own it.

**Craft:** Lambrick's greed curve, step one. Three factions now hunt one girl for three different reasons: incompatible objectives (guide §7).

### 5. The dead-house

*Act Two · St Giles dead-house. Afternoon.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Turn

Nathaniel talks his way in with a Works pass and an engineer's authority. Moss lies on a slab, thawing very slowly, the frost on him still faintly humming. Nathaniel holds the shard near the body and the two hum the same note. He can't breathe for a moment.

Across the room a girl in a borrowed black shawl claims to be the dead man's niece and is lying about it beautifully. Alice, checking what the Guard knows, and watching him for Grise. He recognises the flower-girl from the churchyard. She recognises his loupe as it goes into her pocket.

> **NATHANIEL:** You were at my brother's funeral.
>
> **ALICE:** Lot of people were.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** You weren't crying.
>
> **ALICE:** Nobody was but you.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** *(holding out his hand)* My loupe.
>
> **ALICE:** *(returning it, not blinking)* You dropped it.

**Exit state:** Before: the frost and the shard were separate mysteries. After: Nathaniel knows his brother and the dead docker were killed by the same thing, and he's met the girl twice.

**Craft:** Investigation and romance in the same scene, each complicating the other. She steals from him while lying to him; he notices both and is charmed anyway, which tells us who he is.

### 6. One long spiral

*Act Two · A condemned boarding house in Seven Dials. Evening.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Turn

Their new hiding place: a gutted room with the floorboards up, rain coming through a broken slate, a mattress Alice stole. The Lost One sits wrapped in two coats, frost creeping out from her across the floor whenever she's frightened.

She takes an orange from Alice's bag and peels it, slowly, in one long unbroken spiral, and holds the spiral out. An apology, though she doesn't know the word.

Alice laughs, then stops. Miriam never could peel an orange. Miriam tore them to bits.

> **ALICE:** When did you learn to do that?
>
> **THE LOST ONE:** *(honestly)* Just now.
>
> **ALICE:** *(taking it; choosing)* Don't ever do that again. To a person. Not ever.
>
> **THE LOST ONE:** Even if they're hurting you?
>
> **ALICE:** *(a long beat)* Then we run. We always run.

**Exit state:** Before: Alice could still explain everything as fever. After: she has noticed a thing Miriam couldn't do and chosen, again, not to see it.

**Craft:** Each denial costs more than the last, so the break in Episode 7 is cumulative. 'We always run' is the lie Alice believes: love as holding on, never standing in front.

### 7. Bring it home

*Act Two · Harding House, the servants' hall.* · Thread: Nixon & the kept Hargraves · Setup

Ada sews a torn Guard glove at the long servants' table; around her, the house's twenty lamps burn in their racks, waiting for dusk. Nixon sits with his tunic open, a bruise coming up on his collarbone from the morning's crowd.

> **NIXON:** The thing that killed Jamie. It killed a man in Seven Dials. Or Gin Lane. I don't know where it'll go next.
>
> **ADA:** Then you bring it to Master Eldgard, love. He'll know what to do. He always does.
>
> **NIXON:** You don't even know what it is.
>
> **ADA:** *(biting the thread)* I know who it belongs to.

**Exit state:** Before: Nixon hunted for duty. After: the woman who raised him has told him the killer *belongs* to the house, and he didn't hear it.

**Craft:** Weeks-style secret in plain sight: Ada has just told the truth to the one person who can't hear it. Audiences rewatching will scream.

### 8. The singing tree

*Act Three · Covent Garden: the Christmas crystal market. Dusk.* · Thread: Alice & the Lost One · Turn, Reveal

The Christmas crystal market: a forty-foot tree of cut gallowglass under the iron roof of the flower market, fed straight from the Ring, every branch hung with green baubles; stalls of glass angels and hot chestnuts, *pop-pop*; glim-sellers in the shadows of the arcade; the Lantern Hymn on a steam organ; Guard Wicks swinging overhead on tether-lines to light the high branches. The richest and poorest of London under one green roof.

The Lost One came because the tree is singing. Alice couldn't stop her. She walks through the crowd with her face up like a child, and puts her bare hand on the trunk.

The whole tree flares white-green and *sings*: a single huge note, *HMMMMMMMMMM*, that shatters every bauble in the market at once, a rain of green glass, and frost runs up the trunk to the star. A woman screams it: *FROST KILLER.*

> **THE LOST ONE:** *(hand on the tree, eyes full of light)* Mother?
>
> **ALICE:** *(dragging her)* Mim, *move*—

**Exit state:** Before: the Lost One was hidden. After: half of London has seen her face, and the Guard and Ajax's men are in the market.

**Craft:** Worldbuilding as set piece: the market shows the city's whole economy of light in one image, then breaks it. Her pull toward her mother gets them caught: a character choice, not bad luck.

### 9. The rooftop

*Act Three · Covent Garden: the arcade roofs.* · Thread: Nixon & the kept Hargraves · Turn

The chase, in phases that change the problem. Through the stalls: Alice clears a path with snap-glass, *KRAK*, green flashes, Ajax's men blinded. Up the arcade: Guard Wicks on leap-boosters, *kshhh-THOOM*, dropping onto the iron roof ahead of them. Across the glass roof of the flower market: it cracks under the Lost One's feet, frost spidering out, *krk-krk-krk*.

Nixon reaches the roof on a tether-line and lands between them and the edge. He levels his gauntlet. He has them. And then he sees the frost girl's face up close: young, terrified, holding the small one's hand.

Behind him, a flash-burst from nowhere, *FZZZT*, a white strobe, and every Guardsman on the roof goes blind. Nathaniel, on the next roof, his gauntlet still smoking. He grabs Alice's wrist.

> **NIXON:** *(gauntlet up)* Down. On your knees. Now.
>
> **NIXON:** *(blinded, roaring)* Who fired that? *WHO FIRED THAT?*
>
> **NATHANIEL:** *(to Alice)* This way. Trust me or don't, but this way.

**Exit state:** Before: Nathaniel was investigating the Frost Killer. After: he has blinded his best friend's men to save her.

**Craft:** The point where Nathaniel takes a side happens in one physical action, before he understands why. The season's first major choice made by reflex: the imprint, not the mind.

### 10. The mains

*Act Three · Under Covent Garden: the Ring mains, then up into Clerkenwell.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Turn

He takes them down a Works inspection hatch into the Ring mains: brick tunnels where the green conduits run under the city in pipes as thick as tree trunks, humming, warm. The Lost One stops dead and lays her cheek against a pipe, listening. Alice has to pull her on.

Nathaniel knows every junction without a map. He's never been in these tunnels in his life.

> **ALICE:** You know your way around down here.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** *(honestly confused)* I've seen the drawings.
>
> **THE LOST ONE:** *(hand on the pipe)* She's so tired.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** Who is?
>
> **ALICE:** Nobody. She's feverish. Keep walking.

**Exit state:** Before: they were strangers. After: he has seen the frost girl listen to the light, and Alice has lied to cover it.

**Craft:** Travel tests the relationship (atlas lesson 4): the route forces choices of trust. Geography as causality: the Ring is why an engineer can save them, and it's how the city's veins lead back to Tyburn.

### 11. The cold-sink

*Act Three · Nathaniel's workshop, Clerkenwell. Night.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Turn

The bell-foundry. Alice has a knife at his throat before the door is bolted. The Lost One stands in the middle of the floor, shaking, frost spreading from her feet across the boards in a widening ring, the tools on the nearest bench going white.

Nathaniel looks at the frost, and his hands move without him. Two gauntlet cores, a copper coil from the bench, a strap: he winds the coil in the strange spiral Pike asked him about, and fastens it around her wrist. The frost stops spreading. The ring on the floor begins, slowly, to melt.

Alice lowers the knife an inch.

> **NATHANIEL:** She's the Frost Killer.
>
> **ALICE:** She's my sister.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** My brother died like that man on the stair.
>
> **ALICE:** *(lying, and believing it)* She was with me that night.
>
> **ALICE:** Where'd you learn to do that?
>
> **NATHANIEL:** *(looking at his own hands)* I don't know.
>
> **ALICE:** You'll hand her to your father.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** My father had my brother buried before I could see his face. *(a beat)* You can stay. And you help me find who killed him.

**Exit state:** Before: Nathaniel was alone with a shard. After: he's sheltering the girl London is hunting, and Alice is inside the life she was paid to watch, invited.

**Craft:** Every character in the room holds a secret from the others (Weeks): Alice's contract, the Lost One's nature, Nathaniel's ignorance of his own hands. The deal is the season's spine in one exchange.

### 12. Six gauntlets

*Act Four · The roof of Harding House. Midnight.* · Thread: Nixon & the kept Hargraves · Turn

Their old roof: a flat of leads between chimneys where two boys used to smoke stolen cigars and plan their lives. Nixon is already there with a bottle. Nathaniel comes up through the skylight. They drink. It is almost the old days.

> **NIXON:** Someone flashed my men tonight with a Works gauntlet. Only six exist.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** Mine's in the case.
>
> **NIXON:** I know. I checked. *(a beat)* It was warm.
>
> *(Silence. Neither of them looks at the other.)*
>
> **NIXON:** Your father trusts me with things now.
>
> **NATHANIEL:** I noticed.
>
> **NIXON:** Nat. If I told you something you didn't want to know—
>
> **NATHANIEL:** Then don't.

**Exit state:** Before: they were brothers in everything but blood. After: each knows the other is lying, and each has decided not to ask.

**Craft:** Two men lying to each other in both directions is the spine's Episode 3 beat for this thread. Nixon nearly tells him about Jamie; Nathaniel's 'Then don't' costs him the truth for two more episodes, and it's his own fault.

### 13. Properly

*Act Four · The Shadwell laundry, the drying loft.* · Thread: The Vykra / the contract · Setup

Among the hanging sheets, Jennie stands very still. She was in the market tonight. She saw the face of the woman she put a knife in, alive, under a singing tree.

> **JENNIE:** I put the knife in her. I felt it go in. She went over.
>
> **GRISE:** Then you didn't do it properly.
>
> **JENNIE:** I did it *properly*.
>
> **GRISE:** *(pinning a sheet, unbothered)* Then the river's started giving things back, dear, and we'll need a bigger knife.

**Exit state:** Before: Jennie was sure. After: she's afraid of something for the first time in her life, and Grise doesn't care.

**Craft:** Seed Jennie's doubt in Episode 3 (canon requirement) so her turn in Episode 9 is a choice with roots.

### 14. I had her

*Act Four · The Harding Works, the core.* · Thread: The light / Morgana · Turn

The vault. The pod. The yield needle on the wall spiked at dusk, when the market tree sang, and fell again. Eldgard stands at the glass in his greatcoat. Morgana's eyes are open.

> **ELDGARD:** She was close tonight. You felt her. Call her home.
>
> **MORGANA:** *(in his head, very quiet)* You will never have her.
>
> **ELDGARD:** I had her for sixteen years.

**Exit state:** Before: Eldgard was waiting for the child to come close. After: he knows she's in the city and drawn to the light, and Morgana has drawn a line.

**Craft:** The antagonist's unmet need, stated plainly. Morgana's refusal is the first time she's spoken to Eldgard on screen. Rare words, every one costing someone.

### 15. I know you

*Tag · Nathaniel's workshop. Three in the morning.* · Thread: Nathaniel's hunt · Reveal, Setup

Alice asleep on the cot with her knife in her hand. Nathaniel asleep at his bench with his head on a drawing. The Lost One sits on the floor by him, the copper coil glinting at her wrist, and studies his face the way she studied the orange.

He opens his eyes. She doesn't look away. For a heartbeat, behind her, he sees green light moving on a nursery wall.

> **THE LOST ONE:** *(quiet, certain)* I know you.

**Exit state:** Before: she was a stranger he saved. After: she's someone who remembers him from somewhere he doesn't.

**Craft:** A tag that re-colours the whole episode (Weeks): every choice his hands made tonight was older than tonight.
