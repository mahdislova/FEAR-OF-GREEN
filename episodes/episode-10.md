# Episode 10 — Lights Out

**Cold open:** Alistair closes the pod on her. The broken word.

**Change:** the light ends. **POV:** Nathaniel and Alice.
- Morgana is dying. Eldgard moves to put the Lost One in the pod.
- Alice confronts Josephine and doesn't kill her.
- Nathaniel uses the knowledge planted in 1873 to open the pod. "Us" at the lock: both of them choose it.
- Morgana goes free. Her vengeance: Eldgard. London goes dark.
- The ship. Substantial damage across the city, then retreat. Morgana, too weak to fight, boards it and vows hell on this hellhole.
- In the dark, Lambrick's coal is king: Nathaniel's righteous act has handed London to his nemesis. Tamara opens the depots to the East for nothing.
- Nixon leads the Guard for London, not for the Hardings.
- Alice offers the Lost One the ship. The Lost One looks up at it, then back at Alice, and stays. Her hand finds Alice's in the cold.
