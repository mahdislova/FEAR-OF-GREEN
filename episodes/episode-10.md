# Episode 10 — Lights Out

**Cold open:** Alistair closes the pod on her. The broken word. Then he drops the pod's key into acid and has the ledger record that she gave her life freely.

**In short:** Nathaniel and Alice open the pod. Morgana goes free, London goes dark, the ship comes, and the Lost One stays.

**Change:** the light ends.

**Foreground:** Nathaniel and Alice open the pod; the light ends; the Lost One stays.

**Background:** Alice spares Josephine; Lambrick's coal and Tamara's coup; Nixon leads the Guard for London.

**Not in this episode:** Ajax, Grise, Jennie, Marsh, Byron, the chief engineer.

- Morgana is dying. Eldgard moves to put the Lost One in the pod.
- Alice confronts Josephine and doesn't kill her. Here Alice learns the last layer: Josephine knew who she was from the first day. "I chose you so I'd have to look at you." Alice's mercy lands harder because Josephine never spared herself.
- Nathaniel uses the knowledge planted in 1873 to open the pod. His ruined hand can't work the lock alone; it takes Alice's hand on it too. "Us" at the lock: both of them choose it, and he literally can't do it without her. Opening the pod releases the beacon Alistair silenced.
- Morgana goes free. Her vengeance: Eldgard. One touch and he turns to gallowglass where he stands. London goes dark.
- **Why Alice goes to Josephine:** Morgana leaves the Works with her child, and the Lost One walks beside her mother without looking back. Alice thinks she has lost her sister twice. With nothing left to keep, she goes to the woman who took the first one.
- **The answer to "You're mine" (Ep 9).** At the ship Alice doesn't hold on. She offers the Lost One the ship and means it. That's the season's question answered: the Lost One stays because she was free to leave. "I choose the cold with you."
- **Eldgard is partly right.** The dark kills on screen: people freeze in the first night without the light, because Nathaniel opened the pod. The audience should finish the season unsure he was right.

- Nixon leads the Guard for London, not for the Hardings.

## Scenes

### 1. The broken word (cold open)

c. 1780. The first vault under the Harding woods. Morgana lies in her own cryo pod, hooked to Alistair's lines. She taught him everything he asked. She looks up at him through the glass and waits for him to keep his word. He closes the lid.

His mechanic holds out the key and asks how they are to record her consent. Alistair drops the key into a flask of acid and watches it go green. "Write that she gave her life freely."

### 2. Before dusk

The pod chamber, minutes after the fracture. Morgana is dying; the yield needle on the gallery sinks toward nothing. Eldgard closes his ledger. Dusk is hours away and the engineers will come at dusk. He calls the core guard down: begin the transfer. Two attendants wheel the Lost One's frame toward the pod's feed. Nixon goes up the gallery stair into the guard.

### 3. Two hands

Nathaniel is at the lock. The planted knowledge knows every turn of it. His right hand can't hold the pressure, and his left can't find it. Alice leaves the straps, kneels beside him and puts her hand over his on the wheel. "Us." He couldn't do it without her, and neither of them is pretending otherwise. They turn it together.

Somewhere in the machine a signal Alistair held down for a hundred years comes free. It goes up through the city and out of it.

### 4. Her vengeance

The pod opens in a flood of green. Morgana steps out onto the plates, too weak to stand straight and still terrible to look at. Eldgard doesn't run. She crosses to him and lays her palm on his chest. Frost chimes through him. His heart, his blood, his eyes go to gallowglass, and he stays standing where he was: the last of the light.

### 5. Lights out

Across London the Ring dies street by street. Bridges, the Parliament towers, the hospitals, the docks. Three million people in the dark, and the cold comes in behind it.

### 6. She goes with her mother

Morgana gathers her child out of the broken restraints. The Lost One rises and goes with her, up the stair toward the night, beside her mother. She doesn't look back. Alice calls her name, Miriam's name, and then nothing. Nathaniel holds her arm until the footsteps are gone.

### 7. I chose you

Harding House by candlelight. Josephine is in the drawing room with Jamie's photograph, waiting. Alice comes in with her knife drawn. She asks why Josephine brought her into this house.

Josephine knew who she was from the first day. "I chose you so I'd have to look at you."

Alice stands over her a long time. She puts the knife away. Jennie's statement is on record; the law can have her. "You don't get to die clean, Mrs Harding. You stay in this dark house and remember her." She leaves Josephine alone with the candle.

### 8. The first night

The dark kills, and we watch it. A family on a stair in Shadwell who burned their last chair at midnight. The children's ward at Saint Bartholomew's, on candles, exactly as Eldgard said. People freeze in the first night without the light because Nathaniel opened the pod. Eldgard was partly right.

Through it Nixon leads the Guard with oil torches, carrying people up out of flooded cellars. For London, not for the Hardings.

### 9. The ship

Called by the beacon, the ship comes down through the cloud over Tyburn. The first thing anyone sees whole of it is its shadow. Its wake tears the spires off churches and brings chimneys down across Fleet Street. It settles over the old gallows field and lets down a ramp of white light. Morgana goes up it, and at the top she waits for her child.

### 10. The depots

The Lambrick depots in the East End. London is dark, and the coal has been moving on Tamara's orders since Ep 7. Now she opens every depot to the East for nothing, under her own name. A coup, not charity.

Hector arrives with men to lock the gates and finds them open and the carts rolling. "You took this from me." "You taught me what grief costs. I only paid it forward."

From the Harding terrace Nathaniel watches the Lambrick carts go by under the torches. His righteous act has handed London to his father's rival.

### 11. Across the street

Across the frozen street from the depots, Nixon leads the Guard for London, wearing the coat she relined. Tamara sees him. He sees her. Neither crosses.

### 12. She stays

Tyburn field in the snow. Alice comes through the drifts to the foot of the ramp. The Lost One is halfway up it. Her mother waits at the top.

Alice doesn't ask her to come down. She offers her the ship. "Go, if you want to. I'll know you chose."

The Lost One looks up at the ship, then back at Alice, and stays. "I choose the cold with you."

Hold on Morgana long enough for hope to give way to understanding. She watches her child settle beside Alice. She sees the decision and lets the ship depart in silence. The Lost One's hand finds Alice's in the cold.
