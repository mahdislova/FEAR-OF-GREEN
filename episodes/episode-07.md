# Episode 7 — The Girl Who Isn't

**Cold open:** The Bounds girls under the bed, counting. A belt buckle.

**In short:** The East freezes. The Lost One tells Alice that Miriam is dead and confesses Jamie's death to them both. Alice saves her from Nathaniel, then sends her away.

**Change:** Alice learns Miriam died, and she and Nathaniel learn together what the Lost One did to Jamie. Alice stops Nathaniel from taking her, then cannot yet stay with her.

**Foreground:** Alice and the Lost One: the truth about Miriam and Jamie, and the separation.

**Background:** The dawn raid; Byron at Gin Lane; the pod; Tamara's coal deal.

**Not in this episode:** Josephine, Ajax, Ada, Marsh, Morgana.

- **The raid (the cost of "Find her").** At dawn, the morning after Gin Lane, the Guard closes on the workshop: Nixon followed Nathaniel there. Nathaniel gets the sisters out through the back, into the frozen East End, with the Lanterns on the roofs above them. A chase through streets with no light. A Guard flash-net comes down over the Lost One; Nathaniel throws himself under it to tear it off her. Her cold bursts to save him, and takes his right hand. Her nose bleeds. Nixon, on the roof, sees the Frost Killer freeze his best friend's hand. **The cost:** Nathaniel loses two fingers of his working hand. The engineer can no longer build alone. In the chase the crowd separates the Lost One from the others, which is how she's in the riot.
- **Act One.** The East End is freezing. At Gin Lane bodies lie in the torn-open houses, across the road and on the washhouse racks. The shell around Byron flakes away. He recognizes Nell by her bare feet and finds the old man’s tin against his knee. A relief clerk asks him for the names of the dead. The Harding butler knows just one. He spends the morning finding people who can give him the others. When the Hardings blame the Frost Killer, he gives the survivors his own name as a witness: the green came up through the floor before the girl arrived. The Hardings blame the Frost Killer; mobs form. The massacre repeats, smaller. In the riot, Grise's man comes for Jennie. The crowd carries the Lost One clear of the danger. She looks back and sees Jennie pinned where the crowd has left her. Stay with Jennie long enough for her to see the girl reach safety, stop, and come back for her. Jennie watched Miriam go over the rail. Now she has to take that same hand through a fold of coat and let herself be pulled free. Jennie can't look at her.
- **The middle: the shared reveal.** Alice brings home what Grise told her about their stepfather. The Lost One confirms it from Miriam's memory, then tells Alice the truth: Miriam died at the bridge; the person before her entered Miriam's body and carries her memories. "You're not her." "Why do I love you so much?" Alice asks whether Miriam was alone at the end. The girl starts to answer, then sees Nathaniel in the doorway. She asks him to stay. What she can remember of Jamie belongs to him too. She tells them how he opened the ornament for her, how hungry she was, and what she did to the boy who let her out. Nathaniel waits for the part where somebody tried to help his brother. She has reached the end. Nathaniel asks whether Jamie was still calling for him when she left. She cannot tell him. When he reaches for the device, the girl tells him where the Hardings will put her if he gives her up: in her mother’s pod. He saw the way in as a boy in 1873. He remembers enough to be frightened of believing her. “Alice. Hold her still.”
- **The door.** The first time he used the device, he waited for the girl to offer one finger. This time Alice opens the door. “Go to Jennie. Tell her I sent you.” The girl picks up Alice’s coat as well as her own. For a second Alice lets her hold it. Then she takes it out of her hands and hangs it back on the peg. “I need you to go.” She goes alone. Nathaniel gets as far as Alice. To follow he would have to pull her out of the doorway. He hears the last footstep downstairs and lowers his hand.
- **After she goes.** “You could see what it was doing to her,” Nathaniel says. “Could you see what it was doing to me?” Alice says yes. “And you opened the door.” “I did.” Alice draws up a chair. “I can stay a bit.” He looks at the coat on the peg. She stayed for him, and right now he can’t bear to be grateful for it. “I’d rather you went.” She takes her coat. After she’s gone, he sits in the chair she pulled out.
- **That night.** Jennie opens her door expecting Alice. The girl stands there alone. “She said you’d let me in.” Jennie takes the wet coat. There’s only one chair. She gives it to the girl and sits on the floor against the door. At Harding House Nathaniel hears Tamara’s terms: the marriage on Saturday will secure coal for the East. “Tell Tamara I’ll be there. Has the first cart gone?” He waits for an answer about the cart before he goes upstairs.
- **The overnight step.** Before dawn Jennie sends word: the girl is bleeding from the nose and won't stop. It's the cost of the cold she used to save Jennie, and to save Nathaniel. Alice asks the messenger whether the girl wanted her sent for. He doesn’t know. She goes anyway.
- **Tamara's track.**
  - *Eldgard's study:* the East is freezing. She comes with her father's coal and her own terms: coal to the East tonight, the wedding Saturday. Eldgard agrees. Hector, told it's done, hands her the delivery: "Your terms. Your carts." From tonight the East's coal moves on her orders.
  - *The rented room, the fight:* "You chose this." "I chose the East getting coal tonight. The rest came with it." "The rest is me." She doesn't answer. She holds out the coat she relined. He takes it, because it's freezing.
