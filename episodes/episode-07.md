# Episode 7 — The Girl Who Isn't

**Cold open:** The Bounds girls under the bed, counting. A belt buckle.

**In short:** The East freezes. The Lost One tells Alice that Miriam is dead and confesses Jamie's death to them both. Alice saves her from Nathaniel, then sends her away.

**Change:** Alice learns Miriam died, and she and Nathaniel learn together what the Lost One did to Jamie. Alice stops Nathaniel from taking her, then cannot yet stay with her.

**Foreground:** Alice and the Lost One: the truth about Miriam and Jamie, and the separation.

**Background:** The dawn raid; Byron at Gin Lane; the pod; Tamara's coal deal.

**Not in this episode:** Josephine, Ajax, Ada, Marsh, Morgana.

- **The raid (the cost of "Find her").** At dawn, the morning after Gin Lane, the Guard closes on the workshop: Nixon followed Nathaniel there. Nathaniel gets the sisters out through the back, into the frozen East End, with the Lanterns on the roofs above them. A chase through streets with no light. A Guard flash-net comes down over the Lost One; Nathaniel throws himself under it to tear it off her. Her cold bursts to save him, and takes his right hand. Her nose bleeds. Nixon, on the roof, sees the Frost Killer freeze his best friend's hand. **The cost:** Nathaniel loses two fingers of his working hand. The engineer can no longer build alone. In the chase the crowd separates the Lost One from the others, which is how she's in the riot.
- **The throw (Alice's third aura moment).** On the roofs a Lantern on leap-boosters lands between Alice and the ladder. Miriam's one-handed throw, the move Alice has watched all her life and never landed (Ep 1), works for the first time. He goes off the roof into a coal heap, alive. She stares at her hand the way the girl did in the passage in Ep 2.

- **The middle: the shared reveal.** Alice brings home what Grise told her about their stepfather. ## Scenes

### 1. Count to fifty (cold open)

c. 1878, the Reach. Under an iron cot, seven-year-old Alice and ten-year-old Miriam lie flat in the dust. Boots cross the floor above them. A belt buckle clinks against the boards, inches from Alice's face. Miriam puts her hand over her sister's mouth and counts into her ear. "At fifty he's tired. At fifty we run." Alice counts with her. The boots stop at the bed.

### 2. Dawn

The workshop, the morning after Gin Lane. Sleet on the glass. Nathaniel has hardly slept; the 1873 night is still in his hands. Alice is at the window. A Lantern lands on the roof opposite. Then another. Nixon followed Nathaniel here.

Nathaniel gets the sisters out through the back, into the frozen East End. No lamps anywhere. The Lanterns move along the roofs above them.

### 3. The throw

A ladder up to the next row of roofs. Alice sends the others ahead. A Lantern drops out of the sleet on roaring boosters and lands between her and the ladder.

He reaches for her. She takes his wrist. Left foot *here*. Miriam's throw, the one Alice has failed her whole life. He goes over her hip and off the edge of the roof, into the coal heap below, swearing. Alive.

Alice looks at her own hand, exactly the way the girl from the river did in the passage.

### 4. The net

A yard behind the frozen washhouses. A Guard flash-net comes down over the Lost One and lights up. Nathaniel throws himself under it to tear it off her. She feels him burning. Her cold bursts to save him, and takes his right hand. Two fingers break against the frame like icicles. Her nose bleeds.

On the roof above, Nixon sees the Frost Killer freeze his best friend's hand.

The crowd comes out of the alleys at the noise. It pours between them. When it has passed, the Lost One is gone.

### 5. Ren

Ren's table. Two fingers of Nathaniel's working hand are gone. Ren binds what's left and doesn't soften it. "You'll learn to do it left-handed, or you'll learn to ask for help." Alice holds his other hand. He lets her.

### 6. The names

Gin Lane. Bodies lie in the torn-open houses, across the road and on the washhouse racks. The shell around Byron flakes away. He recognizes Nell by her bare feet and finds the old man's tin against his knee. A relief clerk asks him for the names of the dead. The Harding butler knows just one. He spends the morning finding people who can give him the others.

When the Harding notice goes up blaming the Frost Killer, he gives the survivors his own name as a witness: the green came up through the floor before the girl arrived. The clerk writes it down.

### 7. The riot

The frozen canal basin. The mob is out, hunting the Frost Killer, and the massacre repeats, smaller. In the crush Grise's man Garrow finds Jennie and pins her against the railings with a knife.

The crowd has carried the Lost One clear. She looks back and sees Jennie pinned where the crowd has left her. She stops. She comes back. She lays two fingers on Garrow's throat, and he goes stiff and white and falls.

She holds out her hand through a fold of her coat. Jennie watched Miriam go over the rail. Now she has to take that same hand and let herself be pulled free. She takes it. She can't look at her.

### 8. You're not her

The Bounds room. Alice brings home what Grise told her about their stepfather. The Lost One confirms it from Miriam's memory, then tells Alice the truth: Miriam died at the bridge; the one before her entered Miriam's body and carries her memories.

"You're not her."

"Why do I love you so much?"

Alice asks whether Miriam was alone at the end. The girl starts to answer, then sees Nathaniel in the doorway, his hand bound. She asks him to stay. What she can remember of Jamie belongs to him too. She tells them how he opened the ornament for her, how hungry she was, and what she did to the boy who let her out.

Nathaniel waits for the part where somebody tried to help his brother. She has reached the end. He asks whether Jamie was still calling for him when she left. She can't tell him.

He reaches left-handed for the device. She tells him where the Hardings will put her if he gives her up: in her mother's pod. He saw the way in as a boy, in 1873. He remembers enough to be frightened of believing her. "Alice. Hold her still."

### 9. The door

The first time he used the device, he waited for the girl to offer one finger. This time Alice opens the door. "Go to Jennie. Tell her I sent you." The girl picks up Alice's coat as well as her own. For a second Alice lets her hold it. Then she takes it out of her hands and hangs it back on the peg. "I need you to go."

She goes alone. Nathaniel gets as far as Alice. To follow he would have to pull her out of the doorway. He hears the last footstep downstairs and lowers his hand.

### 10. After she goes

"You could see what it was doing to her," Nathaniel says. "Could you see what it was doing to me?" Alice says yes. "And you opened the door." "I did." Alice draws up a chair. "I can stay a bit." He looks at the coat on the peg. She stayed for him, and right now he can't bear to be grateful for it. "I'd rather you went." She takes her coat. After she's gone, he sits in the chair she pulled out.

### 11. Her terms

Eldgard's study. The East is freezing. Tamara comes with her father's coal and her own terms: coal to the East tonight, the wedding on Saturday. Eldgard agrees. Hector, told it's done, hands her the delivery orders. "Your terms. Your carts." From tonight the East's coal moves on her orders.

### 12. The rest is me

The rented room. Nixon is waiting. "You chose this." "I chose the East getting coal tonight. The rest came with it." "The rest is me." She doesn't answer. His coat is still on the hook where he left it the night of the toast. She takes it down and holds it out. He takes it, because it's freezing.

### 13. That night

Jennie opens her door over the dyehouse expecting Alice. The girl is standing there alone. "She said you'd let me in." Jennie takes the wet coat. There's only one chair. She gives it to the girl and sits on the floor against the door.

At Harding House Nathaniel hears Tamara's terms: the marriage on Saturday will secure coal for the East. "Tell Tamara I'll be there. Has the first cart gone?" He waits for an answer about the cart before he goes upstairs.

### 14. The overnight step

Before dawn Jennie sends word: the girl is bleeding from the nose and it won't stop. It's the cost of the cold she used to save Jennie, and to save Nathaniel. Alice asks the messenger whether the girl wanted her sent for. He doesn't know. She goes anyway.
