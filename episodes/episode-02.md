# Episode 2 — The Wake

**Cold open:** Five-year-old Nathaniel gets out of bed. Something slips into his room. He sees eyes as limitless as the sky. A shriek wakes him.
 
**Change:** Alice finds her missing sister and discovers she needs to earn her trust. Nathaniel starts an investigation by lying to the friend in charge of it. **POV:** Alice.
- On the day of Jamie’s funeral the Lost One comes out of the river mute. Alice has spent the intervening days looking for Miriam.
- Letters from the darkened east interrupt the convoy. The Duke honours Jamie; the Lanterns lower their crystal lights. Nixon returns to Nathaniel under the mended umbrella. At the wake Tamara persuades Hector to move his coal east. Ajax sends Moss to the market after the stolen Narrows cargo.
- Two children and a frightened gang give Alice a trail. Miriam’s fighting reflex survives in a body the Lost One is still learning to use.
- Alice brings her home. Ren offers a provisional explanation for the silence and memory loss. The girl chooses Alice for company.
- Nathaniel keeps the shard. Nixon records his friend’s account and accepts Eldgard’s keys. Tamara and Nixon steal a minute together at the wake.
- Josephine burns her Vykra gloves. Grise sends Josephine’s paid assignment to Alice at home. Jennie arrives to sit with the girl and sees Miriam’s face.
- **Tag:** Jennie stops on the threshold. Alice takes her coat off.

## Scenes

### 1. Funeral convoy / river steps

Nathaniel wakes in the carriage taking him to Jamie’s funeral. The road narrows to a lane between mourners and families holding up letters from the east. A woman taps the carriage glass. Her brother writes that the village lamps went out on Tuesday. Has anybody here heard when they’ll come back? Nathaniel reaches for the catch. The Guard clears the wheels before he gets the window down. The woman takes off her hat as Jamie passes. Across town, the Lost One hauls herself out below the quay. Her wet skirts catch under one knee when she tries to stand. A ferryman offers a hand. She watches his mouth, a beat behind his words. When he repeats himself she flinches and moves away.

### 2. The Bounds room

Alice comes in from another night of looking. For one second she expects to see Miriam in bed. The clean shift she laid out the first night is still on the pillow. She crosses yesterday’s stretch of river off her list and heads downstream before the morning traders disperse.

### 3. The Duke at the funeral doors

The Duke has come as an old friend. At the doors a reporter asks if the eastern failures have reached London. He gives the family room to carry Jamie inside. From the lectern he remembers coming to dinner after his wife died. Eldgard had kept his usual place. Young Jamie sat beside him and ate the things he left on his plate so nobody would fuss over him. “It took me three Thursdays to catch him at it.” A few people laugh, Josephine among them. The Duke asks them to keep coming to one another’s tables in the weeks ahead. Outside, the woman with the letter is still waiting for an answer.

### 4. Buttons

Two children are waiting for the rain to ease under a market awning. The older one fastens the little one’s coat a button out and has to undo the lot. Both dissolve into giggles. The girl from the river tries the top button on her own coat. The little one points to the right hole. For a moment she stays where a stranger can see her.

### 5. The lowering / Nixon’s umbrella

At the graveside the Lanterns uncover their crystal cores. Each turns a cut collar around the green-white light. Bands of amber, blue and violet cross the wet coffin lid as it descends. The last band slips off the brass nameplate. All the shutters close together. The little click carries farther than the music.

Nathaniel stays while the chairs are folded. Rain stops hitting his collar. Over his head one rib of the umbrella is bound with white thread where he mended it himself. He shifts sideways to give Nixon his share of the cover before he even looks up. They stand there. When Nathaniel finally turns, Nixon folds him into a hug and gets them both wet.

### 6. The covered passage

Local boys see a soaking woman who cannot tell them whose coat she is wearing. One blocks the passage. Another catches her sleeve. Before she has found a word he is on the flags, winded, and she is standing exactly as Miriam would stand after a throw. She studies her hands. His friends start toward her. She runs. No frost; everyone gets back up.

### 7. The carriers at the wake

Three coal carriers are eating standing up in the service yard, hats under their arms. Each owns his horses. All three have come on their own time to ask Hector where they should send tomorrow’s loads. He keeps them waiting while Ajax shows him the broken tally from the smuggled Narrows consignment. Ajax wants the market receivers hauled in tonight. Tamara asks him which receiver he expects to open for business after the first arrest. Leave them open and whoever took the load will come looking for a buyer. Hector gives Ajax until tomorrow. Ajax sends Moss, who knows the Narrows thieves by sight, to the covered market.

Hector calls the carriers over. Tamara has heard which eastern towns are losing their light. At her suggestion Hector names one eastern siding for all three loads. One carrier points out that he’s promised his team elsewhere. Hector offers to cover the broken hire and asks what time the man can be at the siding. Six. The other two can make five. Hector settles their accounts here among the dirty plates. By the time the bells finish, three men with their own names painted on their wagons are carrying out the same order.

### 8. Alice finds her

The children remember the coat and its buttons. They point Alice to the passage. The boy on the ground insists she threw him one-handed. For the first time all day Alice looks relieved. She knows whom that sounds like. She finds the girl behind a shutter and starts scolding her for disappearing. The familiar face recoils when Alice reaches for her. Alice sits down on the wet flags. After a while the girl takes her sleeve. Alice walks her home that way.

### 9. Tea after the burial

Nathaniel and Nixon get a plate between them. Nathaniel remembers the three boys standing in a receiving line when they were too small to see over the guests’ bellies. Jamie taught the other two a squeeze on the shoulder: did you want him to get you out? Cover his hand and he’d stay where he was. The first time Nathaniel used it, Jamie solemnly told the Duke that Nixon was going to be sick. Nixon remembers the rest. “He made me go upstairs and do it.” Nathaniel laughs with food in his mouth. For a few seconds he gets to be Jamie’s little brother again.

Nixon asks if, before he goes, Nathaniel will take him through the night Jamie died. Nathaniel agrees. Eldgard catches Nixon as he gets up. Nixon names the last village where he ate a hot meal and the frozen settlements beyond it. The letters outside were telling the truth.

### 10. The Bounds room, afternoon

Dry clothes, a towel, too many questions. Alice first takes the silence for Miriam’s usual refusal to explain herself. Then she offers two shifts and asks which scratches. The girl patiently watches Alice’s mouth even after Alice has finished asking. Alice remembers the shift on the pillow. She helps her into it, fetches a coat and takes her straight to Ren.

### 11. Nixon and Tamara, service yard

Tamara is writing out the carriers’ new destinations. Nixon sets his cold tea beside her ledger. She has noticed his hands shaking since he came in from the east. She pours him a fresh cup, closes her fingers over his around it and waits until he can hold it steady. He asks if she’ll be here tomorrow. She gives him a time before his shift. At the sound of a returning carrier she lets go. “Your tea, Captain.”

### 12. Ren’s rooms

Ren, an underground healer, lets Alice do the talking until the girl reaches past her for a dry towel. Ren slides it within reach and waits for her to take it. A knock or fright could account for lost speech and missing memories, Ren says; pieces may come back in familiar company. Alice wants to know if Miriam recognizes her. When Alice stands to pay, the girl shifts along the bench to stay beside her. “She wants you here,” Ren says. Alice takes her home.

### 13. The parents / the study

Josephine unpins the mourning ribbon she tied to Jamie’s bedroom door. It leaves a little hole in the paint. She tries to rub it away. Eldgard holds out his palm for the pin. She gives him the ribbon too and stands with her forehead against his shoulder. Downstairs somebody laughs; for half a second she thinks it’s Jamie. “It’s Nat,” Eldgard says. They stay there listening. She asks him to find out how their boy is sleeping. Eldgard goes to speak to him.

He begins by asking when Jamie’s behaviour changed. Nathaniel tells him Jamie came up to the Exhibition roof to see him after the gauntlet failed. Eldgard wants to know where Jamie went next. Nathaniel snaps: did he ever once ask what Jamie did on that roof? “He went to see if you were all right,” Eldgard says. “I should have gone myself.” It takes the wind out of Nathaniel. Then his father asks again where Jamie went. Even now he needs the answer. Nathaniel walks out.

Back in the study Nathaniel follows Jamie’s last movements for himself. His father’s first question that night was whether Jamie had opened anything. He gets down beside the skirting and finds a shard the sweep missed. He pockets it as Nixon comes to take his promised account.

### 14. Nixon’s journal / Eldgard’s keys

Alone with Nixon, Nathaniel answers when his friend asks if Jamie was alive when he reached the study. Then Nixon opens his journal. Was anything disturbed? Nathaniel feels the shard in his pocket and says he saw nothing. Nixon enters it. Later Eldgard gives Nixon the keys and the authority to make inquiries for the house. His first account is already on the page.

### 15. Josephine commissions Grise

Nathaniel shuts the study door on his way out. From the landing Josephine sees where her conversation with Eldgard has sent their surviving son. At home that evening she burns the gloves she wore on the bridge. The Vykra knot shows as the lining catches. She goes to Grise in Shadwell with a fee. She wants someone close enough to Nathaniel to tell her where he goes and turn him aside if he follows Jamie’s steps. Grise chooses Alice. Her messenger brings the assignment to the Bounds room. Alice accepts and sends a separate request for Jennie to sit with Miriam while she goes to Grise.

### 16. Jennie at the door

Dusk. Alice has her coat halfway on when Jennie arrives. She is explaining what Ren said when she notices Jennie is still outside. From Jennie’s place in the doorway we see the face she last saw going over the rail. The girl edges toward Alice. Alice takes off her coat. All three stay there. The job can wait until morning.
