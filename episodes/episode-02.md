# Episode 2 — The Wake

**Cold open:** Five-year-old Nathaniel gets out of bed. Something slips into his room. He sees eyes as limitless as the sky. A shriek wakes him.
 
**Change:** Alice finds her missing sister and discovers she needs to earn her trust. Nathaniel starts an investigation by lying to the friend in charge of it. **POV:** Alice.
- On the day of Jamie’s funeral the Lost One comes out of the river mute. Alice has spent the intervening days looking for Miriam.
- Nixon returns with news of frozen villages. The Lambricks supply heat to the wake when the Harding fixtures fail to warm the room.
- Two children and a frightened gang give Alice a trail. Miriam’s fighting reflex survives in a body the Lost One is still learning to use.
- Alice brings her home. Ren offers a provisional explanation for the silence and memory loss. The girl chooses Alice for company.
- Nathaniel keeps the shard. Nixon records his friend’s account and accepts Eldgard’s keys. Tamara and Nixon steal a minute together at the wake.
- Josephine burns her Vykra gloves. Grise sends Josephine’s paid assignment to Alice at home. Jennie arrives to sit with the girl and sees Miriam’s face.
- **Tag:** Jennie stops on the threshold. Alice takes her coat off.

## Scenes

### 1. Funeral convoy / river steps

Nathaniel wakes in the carriage taking him to Jamie’s funeral. Across town, the Lost One hauls herself out below the quay. Her wet skirts catch under one knee when she tries to stand. A ferryman offers a hand. She watches his mouth, a beat behind his words. When he repeats himself she flinches and moves away.

### 2. The Bounds room

Alice comes in from another night of looking. For one second she expects to see Miriam in bed. The clean shift she laid out the first night is still on the pillow. She crosses yesterday’s stretch of river off her list and heads downstream before the morning traders disperse.

### 3. The funeral doors

Nixon catches the convoy at the doors. Nathaniel makes room beside him. Eldgard gets one look at the captain’s travelling clothes and asks how far east he went. Nixon names the last village where he could still get a hot meal. Beyond it, whole villages frozen. Then the doors open and he and Nathaniel have to carry their friend and brother inside. Hold the reserved umbrella beat at the arrival; its action remains for the writer.

### 4. Buttons

Two children are waiting for the rain to ease under a market awning. The older one fastens the little one’s coat a button out and has to undo the lot. Both dissolve into giggles. The girl from the river tries the top button on her own coat. The little one points to the right hole. For a moment she stays where a stranger can see her.

### 5. Jamie’s service

A packed house. The Duke attends. Nathaniel has come ready to speak about his brother. At the lectern he unfolds his paper, finds the story he meant to tell and smiles to himself. He reads it quietly enough that Nixon in the front row leans forward to catch the end. In the rear, a servant quietly leads an elderly mourner out of the cold to the adjoining wake room. Its green lamps burn bright. Its radiators are barely warm.

### 6. The covered passage

Local boys see a soaking woman who cannot tell them whose coat she is wearing. One blocks the passage. Another catches her sleeve. Before she has found a word he is on the flags, winded, and she is standing exactly as Miriam would stand after a throw. She studies her hands. His friends start toward her. She runs. No frost; everyone gets back up.

### 7. The wake kitchen

A Lambrick cart is in the service yard collecting the empty scuttles from an earlier delivery. Hector hears that the cold room is being cleared. He has the driver leave the return load meant for his own house. Tamara knows which hearth still draws and gets the staff to open that room first. By the time the mourners come through, the elderly guest has her shoes off at the grate. She asks the carter to turn them so the other sides dry. Hector has to wait for his man. Let the fire do the persuading.

### 8. Alice finds her

The children remember the coat and its buttons. They point Alice to the passage. The boy on the ground insists she threw him one-handed. For the first time all day Alice looks relieved. She knows whom that sounds like. She finds the girl behind a shutter and starts scolding her for disappearing. The familiar face recoils when Alice reaches for her. Alice sits down on the wet flags. After a while the girl takes her sleeve. Alice walks her home that way.

### 9. Jamie’s study

Back at the house, Nathaniel retraces the few steps between the door and the place Jamie lay. His father’s first question that night comes back to him: did Jamie open anything? A small shard catches against the skirting where it escaped the sweep. Nathaniel picks it up. Hearing Eldgard in the hall, he closes his hand over it.

### 10. The Bounds room, afternoon

Dry clothes, a towel, too many questions. Alice first takes the silence for Miriam’s usual refusal to explain herself. Then she offers two shifts and asks which scratches. The girl patiently watches Alice’s mouth even after Alice has finished asking. Alice remembers the shift on the pillow. She helps her into it, fetches a coat and takes her straight to Ren.

### 11. Nixon and Tamara, service yard

Tamara signs off the coal delivery. Nixon is returning the cup somebody put in his hands at the service; the tea has gone cold. Alone for the time it takes to pour him a fresh one, she asks whether he heard Nathaniel’s story. He admits he missed the last line. She repeats it in his ear. He laughs for the first time since he got back. She drinks from his cup before handing it over. When the driver returns she calls him Captain. Their argument about going public belongs to their next private meeting.

### 12. Ren’s rooms

Ren, an underground healer, lets Alice do the talking until the girl reaches past her for a dry towel. Ren slides it within reach and waits for her to take it. A knock or fright could account for lost speech and missing memories, Ren says; pieces may come back in familiar company. Alice wants to know if Miriam recognizes her. When Alice stands to pay, the girl shifts along the bench to stay beside her. “She wants you here,” Ren says. Alice takes her home.

### 13. Nixon’s journal / Eldgard’s keys

Alone with Nixon, Nathaniel answers when his friend asks if Jamie was alive when he reached the study. Then Nixon opens his journal. Was anything disturbed? Nathaniel feels the shard in his pocket and says he saw nothing. Nixon enters it. Later Eldgard gives Nixon the keys and the authority to make inquiries for the house. His first account is already on the page.

### 14. Two fires

The last guests are leaving the warm room. Upstairs Josephine burns the gloves she wore on the bridge. For a moment the stitching inside is visible: the Vykra knot. Down at Shadwell, Grise dispatches word of the job Josephine has paid for. Alice is wanted close to Nathaniel Harding. The messenger finds Alice at the Bounds room. She accepts the work at the door and sends back a separate request for Jennie to come to the room. She will go see Grise as soon as Jennie comes.

### 15. Jennie at the door

Dusk. Alice has her coat halfway on when Jennie arrives. She is explaining what Ren said when she notices Jennie is still outside. From Jennie’s place in the doorway we see the face she last saw going over the rail. The girl edges toward Alice. Alice takes off her coat. All three stay there. The job can wait until morning.
