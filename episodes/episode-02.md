# Episode 2 — The Wake

**Cold open:** Nathaniel as a boy hears noises and gets out of bed. Something creeps into his room: he sees a shadow, and eyes as limitless as the sky. They seem to pierce through him. Then a shriek. Cut to: it was a nightmare.

**Change:** two grieving siblings get missions. **POV:** Alice.
