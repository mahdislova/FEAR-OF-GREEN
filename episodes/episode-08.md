# Episode 8 — The Wedding

**Cold open:** Hargrave freezes in one night. A child survivor in Alistair's coat.

**Change:** the child is taken; everyone picks a side. **POV:** Nixon.
- Morning: after sending the Lost One away in Ep 7, Alice finds her again and chooses to stand with the person she now knows, rather than the sister she hoped to recover. "Nine is the one where we get up." The Lost One returns to Nathaniel's workshop with Alice, making it a target again. At the wedding Alice tells Nathaniel herself that his mother hired her to get close to him: paid at first, never after. This compounds the damage from their confrontation; it does not resolve it.
- **Josephine gives Eldgard the workshop,** from Alice's own reports. If the creature goes in the pod, the evidence of her crime disappears for ninety years.
- **Nixon's point of no return:** Nathaniel confirms to Nixon what the Lost One admitted about Jamie in Ep 7. Nixon leads the Guard raid on the workshop and takes her for Eldgard. He believes he is taking a killer, without knowing the intended replacement in the pod.
- The wedding. Nixon hands Tamara the ring properly. Tamara has cracked Alistair's book and gives Nixon the Hargrave pages.
- Nixon to Ada: "Did you know?" "Forgive them? Love, I forgave them before you were born."
- At the altar Nathaniel learns she's been taken, and says "I will" anyway, because the East is freezing. Once he learns Eldgard intends to put her in the pod, he leaves the wedding breakfast to get her. He has not excused Jamie's death: his choice is that grief cannot justify condemning a child to the captivity that is killing her mother.
- **Tag:** the Lost One in the Works, face to face with her mother's pod. Morgana: "My child."
