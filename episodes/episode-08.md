# Episode 8 — The Wedding

**Cold open:** Hargrave freezes in one night. A child survivor in Alistair's coat.

**Change:** the child is taken; everyone picks a side. **POV:** Nixon.
- Morning: after sending the Lost One away in Ep 7, Alice finds her again and chooses to stand with the person she now knows, rather than the sister she hoped to recover. "Nine is the one where we get up." Alice finds her with Jennie. Only after Alice has chosen her does Jennie tell her what she saw on the bridge. Josephine killed Miriam. “Miriam was the best of us.” At the wedding Josephine holds out her wrist as usual. Alice fastens two buttons and stops, looking at the hand that killed Miriam. Josephine finishes the cuff herself. She sees that Alice knows and tells her where Nathaniel is waiting alone. Alice goes to him with a chance to accuse Josephine and begins with her own part. She tells Nathaniel herself that his mother hired her to get close to him: paid at first, never after. This compounds the damage from their confrontation; it does not resolve it.
- Josephine supplies the lead that enables the arrest. Open: the arrest site and how Josephine discovers the Lost One’s whereabouts after she leaves the workshop.
- **Nixon's point of no return:** Nathaniel confirms to Nixon what the Lost One admitted about Jamie in Ep 7. Nixon takes her into Guard custody over Jamie’s death. Eldgard diverts the prisoner to the Works. Nixon did not arrest her knowing she would be put in the pod.
- The wedding. Nixon hands Tamara the ring properly. Tamara has cracked Alistair's book and gives Nixon the Hargrave pages.
- Nixon to Ada: "Did you know?" "Forgive them? Love, I forgave them before you were born."
- At the altar Nathaniel learns she's been taken, and says "I will" anyway, because the East is freezing. Once he learns Eldgard intends to put her in the pod, he leaves the wedding breakfast to get her. He has not excused Jamie's death: his choice is that grief cannot justify condemning a child to the captivity that is killing her mother.
- **Tag:** the Lost One in the Works, face to face with her mother's pod. Morgana: "My child."
