# Episode 4 — The Hearing

**Cold open:** The deal. Morgana teaches; Alistair: "You have my word."

**Change:** the light's failure goes public. **POV:** Lambrick, Tamara, Nathaniel.
- Lambrick attacks the Harding Charter in Parliament with yield figures smuggled out by the Works' chief engineer. The lamps flicker in the chamber on cue.
- Tamara at the hearing: the codebreaker, the engagement as a merger on both sides.
- Nixon visits Marsh in Newgate. The madman pinches his chin and whispers Hargrave. Nixon walks away not understanding. When he mentions the encounter to Ada, she asks if Marsh has his spectacles. Nixon tries to learn how she knows him. She wraps the glasses and explains that one arm is loose. Nixon must ask for the cell to be opened and give them to Marsh himself. He agrees. Ada asks whether he is coming on Sunday. He asks if she still keeps the crisp end of the joint for him. She tells him he can cut it himself this time. He promises to come. Ada has found a way to care for her brother in the very prison she helped put him in.
- Nathaniel and Alice match the shard to a Works drawing. Jamie could have opened the ornament himself. The Guard discounted that possibility on the strength of Nathaniel's statement. His silence now protects his own lie. At the workshop Nathaniel shows Miriam the exterior drawing and asks whether Jamie showed her the ornament. She asks for time. He leaves the drawing with her. The romance warms; Alice reports to Josephine and hates it. As they talk Alice fastens the pearl buttons at Josephine's wrist. Both treat it as an ordinary kindness.
- **Grise tests "Miriam".** Jennie fetches her to the laundry. Grise gives a Vykra hand-sign the real Miriam would answer without thinking. The Lost One doesn't. Grise smiles: it isn't Miriam, and it's worth more than anything she's ever sold. She sells the Frost Killer's whereabouts to Lambrick.
- **Tag:** Eldgard to Josephine: when the mother dies, they use the child. The audience knows the stakes before the characters do.
