# Episode 4 — The Hearing

**Cold open:** The deal. Morgana teaches; Alistair: "You have my word."

**Change:** the light's failure goes public. **POV:** Lambrick, Tamara, Nathaniel.
- Lambrick attacks the Harding Charter in Parliament with yield figures smuggled out by the Works' chief engineer. The lamps flicker in the chamber on cue.
- **The Duke keeps his word** and tells the Lords it's a fault in the lines. Hector asks him one question: "Your Grace, how many days did you tell your sister to lay in?" The Hardings' most respected witness is broken in public, and so is a friendship.
- Tamara at the hearing: the codebreaker, the engagement as a merger on both sides.
- The prison train climbs to Newgate, its cell galleries cut into a mountain face above the freight line. From the glazed transfer tower Nixon sees relief guards glide down to the valley on folding patrol wings. The moving stair sticks on the way to Marsh’s landing. Every prisoner lifts a foot at the same instant. Nixon visits Marsh in Newgate. The madman pinches his chin and whispers Hargrave. Nixon walks away not understanding. When he mentions the encounter to Ada, she asks if Marsh has his spectacles. Nixon tries to learn how she knows him. She wraps the glasses and explains that one arm is loose. Nixon must ask for the cell to be opened and give them to Marsh himself. He agrees. Ada asks whether he is coming on Sunday. He asks if she still keeps the crisp end of the joint for him. She tells him he can cut it himself this time. He promises to come. Ada has found a way to care for her brother in the very prison she helped put him in.
- Nathaniel and Alice match the shard to a Works drawing. Jamie could have opened the ornament himself. The Guard discounted that possibility on the strength of Nathaniel's statement. His silence now protects his own lie. At the workshop Nathaniel shows Miriam the exterior drawing and asks whether Jamie showed her the ornament. She asks for time. He leaves the drawing with her. The romance warms; Alice reports to Josephine and hates it. As they talk Alice fastens the pearl buttons at Josephine's wrist. Both treat it as an ordinary kindness. Josephine offers the wrist on purpose and holds perfectly still: she is letting her victim's sister touch her. A confession nobody hears.
- **Grise tests "Miriam".** Grise knows from the street that Alice has been playing her (Ep 3), and she holds the one thing Alice can't afford to lose: the contract. Through Jennie she sends Alice an ultimatum. Bring your sister in to report, or Nathaniel Harding learns who's paying the girl he's sheltering. If he learns that, he throws them out, and the Guard is waiting outside. Walking Miriam into Grise's room is the lesser danger. Alice sets the terms: daylight, the laundry floor full of women, and she doesn't leave her sister's side. Grise asks for a Vykra report and gets fragments and nonsense. Then she gives a Vykra hand-sign the real Miriam would answer without thinking. The Lost One doesn't. Alice is too new to know what the sign meant. Grise smiles: it isn't Miriam, and it's worth more than anything she's ever sold. She orders Jennie to follow the sisters home. Jennie comes back and says she lost them in the fog. It's her first lie to Grise. Grise sells what she has to Lambrick: a sighting, and the girl who walked them out.
- **Tag:** Eldgard to Josephine: when the mother dies, they use the child. The audience knows the stakes before the characters do.
