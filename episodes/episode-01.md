# Episode 1 — The Exhibition

**Cold open:** c. 1780. The crash in the Harding woods. Alistair's lantern finds a hand in the wreck. Somewhere in the hull a signal is pulsing, steady as a heartbeat. Alistair finds where it comes from and silences it.

**In short:** The night the secret escapes. Nathaniel is humiliated at the Exhibition; Jamie frees the child his family has kept, and it ravages him on its way out; Miriam dies at the bridge, and something takes her body.

**Change:** the secret gets out.

**Foreground:** Nathaniel's day (the chair, the misfire, the roof) and Jamie freeing the child.

**Background:** Miriam and Alice on the Narrows raid, ending at the bridge; Byron covers for Josephine; Ada; Tamara.

**Not in this episode:** Nixon, Hector, Ajax, Marsh, the chief engineer.

- Jamie's initiation, days earlier: the stair, the vault, Morgana in the pod. "Where is my child?"
- **The luncheon chair.** Nathaniel sits in the empty chair at his father's right; Eldgard moves him: "That's Nixon's chair, Nat." Nixon isn't in the episode. Eldgard has sent him east to see the failures for himself. Over the same lunch Josephine tells Nathaniel he is to marry Tamara Lambrick. Jamie: "Congratulations, Nat. You've been sold."
- **The gauntlet failure.** At the Exhibition at the Works, Nathaniel demonstrates his gauntlet prototype to impress a visiting professor. It misfires in front of the crowd and humiliates him in Eldgard's eyes. The coil inside it runs on a spiral he can't explain to himself.
- **The roof.** Jamie finds him on the roof: a quiet brother-to-brother moment. "Never lose the light within you." Then Jamie goes to free the child.
- Ada polishing lamps (seed her from Ep 1). On the servants' stair she meets Jamie going up to the study.
- On the Narrows raid Miriam drops a man with her one-handed throw, the move Alice has watched her whole life. (It pays off in Ep 2 when the girl from the river throws a boy the same way and Alice recognises it.) Jennie rides the raid with them.
- On the Narrows raid Miriam steals a book from the Lambrick consignment and hands it to Grise: Hector's private audit of the Ring under Gin Lane, proving its housings are fractured. The consignment is Harding glass with the Works crest chiselled off. There is more to the cargo than the Lambricks want to admit.
- **Grise's black wax.** A letter sealed in plain black wax asks Grise for an operative to make sure a son stays home. Grise sends Jennie after Miriam. (Josephine, unrevealed; she buys the rendezvous.)
- **Three berths.** With the Lambrick gold Miriam buys three berths on the dawn packet: herself, Jamie, Alice.
- **Byron covers for Josephine.** She is the Exhibition's hostess and must leave before midnight. Byron tells anyone who asks that the mistress went home with a headache. He suspects, and says nothing: loyalty means silence.
- **Half an orange.** It's Alice's birthday. Miriam: "Not tonight." Alice chooses to trust her, eats half the orange and leaves the other half on Miriam's pillow.
- Cross-cut: Jamie at Harding House breaks the ornament; the offspring, starved and half-mad, ravages him and goes out of the window. Miriam waits at the bridge. We see only her fall, and a figure in the shadows backing away (Josephine, unrevealed). Jennie watches from the stair. The river. Miriam's last thought is Alice: "Go back for my sister."
- **Closing image:** Miriam drowning, intercut with Nathaniel coming into the study where Jamie is bleeding out.
- **Tamara's track.**
  - *The Exhibition:* "very polite for forty years" with Nathaniel (canon). When the gauntlet misfires, two hundred people laugh. She doesn't.
- **Tag:** Nathaniel kneels by Jamie, bleeding out.

## Scenes

### 1. The furrow (cold open)

The Harding woods, c. 1780. Night, hard frost. A furrow a quarter of a mile long runs through the beeches, still steaming. At the end of it, half-buried in black earth, lies a hull the colour of a frozen pond, ribbed like a chest. It hums one low note that makes the teeth ache.

Alistair Harding comes down the furrow with a lantern. Three Hargrave woodcutters keep well back with their torches. One boy crosses himself.

The lantern finds a hand in the wreck: long-fingered, pale blue, frost feathering the nails. It moves. Alistair doesn't take it. Deeper in the hull something pulses, steady as a heartbeat. He climbs past the hand toward it. He finds where the signal comes from and stops it with his boot heel, and the woods go quiet.

Match cut: 1889. Emerald streetlamps over Mayfair. The camera drops through an iron grating into the pipes and comes up in Gin Lane, where every third lamp is dark.

### 2. The coil

Nathaniel's workshop, late morning. He fires the gauntlet's armature and it throws him into the coal bin. He climbs out and opens the casing. The coil inside is wound in a spiral he drew at three in the morning. It solves the heat bleed every engineer at the Works has fought for ten years. He tries to work out how he got there. He can't follow his own steps. Byron arrives to fetch him for luncheon and picks soot out of his hair on the stairs.

### 3. Nixon's chair

Harding House, the dining room. Eldgard, Josephine and Jamie are already at the table under the green chandelier. One chair at Eldgard's right is empty. Nathaniel sits in it.

"That's Nixon's chair, Nat."

Nixon is in the east, sent to see the failures for himself. Nathaniel moves down one place. The chair stays empty all through the grouse.

Josephine tells Nathaniel he is to marry Tamara Lambrick. It's settled; the families have agreed. Jamie raises his glass. "Congratulations, Nat. You've been sold." Josephine doesn't laugh. Eldgard does, once. Mid-course the chandelier dips for three seconds. Nobody at the table mentions it.

### 4. The ornament

Eldgard's study. On the desk sits the ornament: a palm-sized fist of pond-coloured glass caged in brass, humming like a trapped heart. Eldgard pours two drinks. Tonight the family stands up at the Exhibition, and tonight Jamie gives the speech. "You smile for them anyway." Jamie looks at the glass. "Does it know where it is?" Eldgard hands him his drink.

### 5. Three nights earlier

The Works vault, Jamie's twenty-fifth birthday. Eldgard leads him down the damp stair. Behind the glass of the pod floats Morgana, thin as a reed, held in green. Eldgard rests a hand on his son's shoulder. "Do you feel the quiet? That means you're home." Jamie says yes. Her eyes open. Her voice arrives inside his head, in his own language: *Where is my child?*

### 6. The Narrows raid

A riverside warehouse on the Narrows, afternoon. Rain through the skylight. Crates of core-cut gallowglass, the Works crest chiselled off every one, sit under Lambrick seals. Miriam crouches on the roof glass, counting under her breath. Jennie is beside her with the rope. Alice is supposed to be at the corner, keeping watch. She isn't.

Miriam drops through. Four men, then three. The fourth comes at her from behind and she throws him one-handed over her hip into the crates. Alice, in the rafters, has watched that throw her whole life and has never once made it work. In the safe: a Lambrick purse and a ledger with the Lambrick crest. Miriam takes both. When Alice asks who goes out of the window first, Miriam is already on the sill. "Me. Always me."

### 7. The laundry

The Shadwell laundry. Miriam hands Grise the ledger and keeps the purse. Grise turns the ledger's pages and smiles at what she reads. Then she looks at Alice the way she looks at good linen. "Your sister's quick." Miriam steps between them. "Keep your hands off her."

After they've gone, Grise opens a letter sealed in plain black wax. Someone wants a son kept at home and will pay to know where he's going tonight. Grise calls Jennie over from the tubs. "Follow Miriam."

### 8. Half an orange

The Bounds room, Gin Lane. Miriam dresses to go out and tosses Alice an orange from a street cart. "Happy birthday, Ali. Tomorrow you choose where." Alice asks where she's going. Miriam tells her she lost her temper in the warehouse, and that Alice shouldn't have been in the rafters. "Not tonight." Alice promises to wait.

### 9. Three berths

The Wapping packet office, dusk. Miriam pays for three berths on the dawn boat to Southampton with the Lambrick gold. The clerk writes the names. Across the street Jennie watches through the rain. When Miriam's cab pulls away, Jennie gives its driver a coin for the address.

### 10. A child in a box

A hired coach near Blackwood Bridge. Jamie climbs in out of the rain. Miriam shows him the tickets. He tells her what he's going to do. The house will be empty during the Exhibition. On his father's desk there is a glass box with something alive inside it. He's going to break it, let it go, and meet her on the bridge at midnight. She asks what it is. "A child," he says. "I think it's a child."

### 11. The Exhibition

The Works great hall, night. Two hundred guests and every lamp turned high. Josephine is the hostess. Tamara Lambrick is brought across to meet her future husband. They find they can be very polite for forty years. Jamie gives the family speech, smiling, and goes to the back of the hall.

Nathaniel takes the stage for the visiting professor. He fires the gauntlet. The coil coughs. He stumbles halfway across the floor. Two hundred people laugh. Tamara doesn't. Eldgard doesn't look at him.

Before midnight Josephine leaves. When guests ask, Byron says the mistress went home with a headache. He doesn't know that's a lie. He suspects it, and says nothing.

### 12. The roof

Nathaniel sits on the Works parapet with the dead gauntlet in his lap. Jamie finds him. For a while they don't talk. Then Jamie takes the gauntlet, turns it in the light, and gives it back. "Never lose the light within you." He squeezes Nathaniel's shoulder and goes downstairs to free the child.

### 13. The servants' stair

Harding House, late. Ada climbs the servants' stair with her rags and her taper, polishing the landing lamps. Jamie is coming up the other way with a spanner from the Works. She asks why he isn't at the Exhibition. He asks her, gently, to go to bed. She goes down a flight and stops. She watches him turn the corner toward his father's study.

### 14. The pillow

The Bounds room. Alice puts on her coat to follow Miriam and stops at the door. *Not tonight.* For the first time in her life she chooses to trust her sister. She takes her coat off. She peels the orange in one long strip, eats half, and leaves the other half on Miriam's pillow.

### 15. The ornament breaks

The study. Jamie locks the door and brings the spanner down on the brass cage. The glass splits with a shriek. Cold light pours out of it, starved and half-mad, and goes through him on its way to the window. Frost runs up his throat. The window bursts outward over Mayfair. Jamie goes down on the frosted rug.

At the Works every chandelier sinks to an ember. Under the hall floor, beneath the hum, Nathaniel hears a woman crying. Nobody else hears it. A runner reaches Eldgard. He leaves without a word.

### 16. Blackwood Bridge

Midnight. Rain. Miriam waits at the rail in her red mittens. A carriage stops at the far end. It isn't Jamie. Under the arch Jennie holds her breath on the stair.

We see a struggle in the dark, and then only Miriam's fall. A figure in the shadows backs away from the rail and is gone. Jennie doesn't move until the carriage has gone.

### 17. The river / the study

Under the Thames, Miriam sinks, blood rising from her side. A streak of cold light comes down through the black water and finds her too late to save her. Her lips move: *Go back for my sister.* The light goes into her. Her eyes open in the dark water.

Harding House. Nathaniel comes in with his gauntlet case and finds the hall full of constables. He puts his bare palm on the banister. It's ice. Byron comes down from the landing. "Sir. Don't go up." Nathaniel goes up. The study door is open. Jamie is on the rug, still breathing.

Cut between them: Miriam turning in the river, Nathaniel crossing the study. He kneels by his brother, bleeding out.
