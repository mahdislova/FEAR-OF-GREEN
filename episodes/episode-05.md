# Episode 5 — Open (midseason)

**Cold open:** None. Straight in.

**Change:** Nathaniel's secret shelter becomes a betrayal in Nixon's eyes. Nixon discovers he has hidden the suspected Frost Killer. **POV:** Nathaniel.
- Josephine summons Alice to wait upstairs and report where Nathaniel goes after dinner. The Lost One asks for the workshop to herself. Alice agrees. Nixon hears the public engagement toast. Nathaniel, unaware of Nixon and Tamara’s relationship, tells Alice upstairs afterwards.
- Alone, the Lost One sketches the inside of the object from Nathaniel’s exterior drawing. She folds her sketch away. She still cannot account for Jamie’s death.
- Nixon vouches for Nathaniel's account of the study at dinner. Afterwards Nathaniel shows him the shard voluntarily and admits helping the woman at the market. Nixon asks where she is. Nathaniel refuses. Nixon opens his journal to the first statement and asks him to start again from the moment he opened the study door. This time he writes down the question about Jamie too.
- Nathaniel stays at Harding House after giving the second statement. Going back to the workshop could lead Nixon to the sisters. Alice returns alone and explains his absence.

## Scenes

### 1. The workshop, before dinner

Alice has been sent for. She is still finding things to put within her sister’s reach when the Lost One asks for an evening to herself. Alice takes the hint. Once the door closes, the Lost One moves the chair back where she likes it and spreads Nathaniel’s drawing on the bench.

### 2. Upstairs at Harding House

Josephine receives Alice before the guests sit down. She expects Nathaniel to slip out after dinner. She wants to know where he goes. She tells him in passing that Alice is waiting upstairs. Both hear precisely as much of the arrangement as Josephine wants them to hear.

### 3. The toast

Nixon hears Nathaniel’s name and Tamara’s in the same sentence. Tamara is looking straight at him. He lets her see that he heard. He turns to Nathaniel first and squeezes his shoulder exactly as he would have done if the news had made him happy. Nathaniel covers Nixon’s hand with his own. For a moment Nixon has to stand there and let his friend be grateful. Then he offers Tamara his congratulations.

### 4. The drawing

Alone at the bench, the Lost One turns the sheet over. The reverse is blank. She takes a fresh sheet, lays it alongside and draws the curve she remembers facing her. Side by side, the sheets show opposite surfaces of the same wall. She folds hers small enough to keep. The exterior drawing stays in plain view.

### 5. The assurance

Hector brings up the inquiry while both families are still at table. “When Nathaniel gave his account, did he say anything in the study had been disturbed?” Nixon gives the answer Nathaniel gave him. “He said he saw nothing disturbed.” Hector accepts that and moves on. Nathaniel could correct it in front of everyone. He lets his friend’s answer stand.

### 6. Upstairs after dinner

Nathaniel finds Alice and starts telling her how wretched the announcement was. She has to ask what was announced. He tells her about Tamara. Alice asks when he first heard. He could say the families have only just made it public. Instead he gives her the day: lunch, before the Exhibition. She has been listening to him talk about Jamie for days, and that lunch has come up before. She puts his coat back on its peg. He asks her to wait while he finds Nixon.

### 7. The second statement

Nathaniel takes out the shard before Nixon can thank him for coming to say goodnight. He lets Nixon hold it. Once he has corrected the account of the study, Nixon asks about the market. Nathaniel owns his part in the woman’s escape. He refuses to tell Nixon where she is. Nixon opens the journal, finds the date of the first interview and starts on a clean page. “Was Jamie alive when you reached him?” Last time Nixon waited for the answer with his pen laid down. Nathaniel waits this time for him to finish writing.

### 8. The empty chair

Nathaniel tells Alice he is sleeping at Harding House. He has given Nixon reason to watch where he goes. Alice returns to the workshop alone. The Lost One has cleared a place on the bench for Nathaniel to sit beside her. Alice tells her why he stayed away. After a while the Lost One picks up the folded sheet and puts it back in her pocket.
