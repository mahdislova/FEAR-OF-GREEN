# Episode 5 — Open (midseason)

**Cold open:** None. Straight in.

**Change:** Nathaniel's secret shelter becomes a betrayal in Nixon's eyes. Nixon discovers he has hidden the suspected Frost Killer. **POV:** Nathaniel.
- At the Works, declining output costs a shift. Getting the men paid forces the engineer to take the loss to Eldgard, who requests district-cut estimates for tomorrow.
- Grise's sale brings Ajax to Shadwell. Jennie signs a truthful sighting at the Guard office to protect the sisters’ refuge. Nixon receives her account after delivering Ada’s spectacles to Marsh at Newgate.
- Josephine summons Alice to wait upstairs and report where Nathaniel goes after dinner. The Lost One asks for the workshop to herself. Alice agrees. Tamara offers to announce the engagement before Hector raises Jamie’s study. In return she gets a seat at the next morning’s Works meeting. Nixon hears her make the toast herself. Nathaniel, unaware of Nixon and Tamara’s relationship, tells Alice upstairs afterwards.
- Alone, the Lost One sketches the inside of the object from Nathaniel’s exterior drawing. She folds her sketch away. She still cannot account for Jamie’s death.
- Nixon vouches for Nathaniel's account of the study at dinner. Afterwards Nathaniel shows him the shard voluntarily and admits helping the woman at the market. Nixon asks where she is. Nathaniel refuses. Nixon opens his journal to the first statement and asks him to start again from the moment he opened the study door. This time he writes down the question about Jamie too.
- Nathaniel stays at Harding House after giving the second statement. Going back to the workshop could lead Nixon to the sisters. Alice returns alone and explains his absence.

## Scenes

### 1. The men on the night shift

At shift change in the Works, the chief engineer hands the incoming foreman a sheet with the figures from yesterday’s hearing. A second sheet, for actual use, has a lower figure. The foreman asks which one he is supposed to spend. A bank of workers stands waiting to start the presses. The engineer orders them left idle and signs for the lost shift himself. Before the foreman isolates the press he waits for the green to bleed away. An apprentice reaches too soon. The foreman shows him the split in the old crystal housing. On a press it costs a finger. Below Gin Lane a whole block shares one housing. The hearing was yesterday. Today people go home without wages.

### 2. What Grise sold

Ajax comes to the Shadwell laundry to collect on Hector’s purchase. Grise gives him exactly what she has: the Frost Killer came here yesterday, and Jennie fetched her. Ajax wants Jennie. Grise calls the girl over in front of the other women and tells her she may take the rest of the day off. Her own girls see what a customer’s money buys. Outside, Ajax offers Jennie a choice: take him to the woman, or give the Guard a signed account of where she saw her. He can use either.

### 3. A prisoner reads the paper

Before the family dinner Nixon makes the gaoler open Marsh’s cell and delivers Ada’s spectacles as promised. Marsh notices Works grit in the newspaper fold. As a boy he carried those same returns up from the draw room in a Hargrave servant’s apron. Nixon lends him the paper. With the glasses on, he can finally read yesterday’s hearing for himself. He asks Nixon to leave him the page with the district figures. Nixon does. Outside, the gaoler records the gift under Nixon’s name. A small visit has made their acquaintance a matter of record. Back at Harding House, Byron chips his ring carrying in a tray. Eldgard turns the old green stone toward Byron’s palm, the way it sat when he gave it to him. Byron wears it to finish the service.

### 4. The workshop, before dinner

Alice has been sent for. She is still finding things to put within her sister’s reach when the Lost One asks for an evening to herself. Alice takes the hint. Once the door closes, the Lost One moves the chair back where she likes it and spreads Nathaniel’s drawing on the bench.

### 5. Jennie takes the second offer

At the Guard office, Ajax waits while Jennie dictates a statement. She gives her own name, the laundry, the day, and the words 'I saw Miriam Bounds.' The desk officer asks if she saw the woman kill anyone. No. Ajax thought the choice of going to the Guard would frighten a Vykra into leading him to the girl. Jennie has chosen a public room where he has to accept the terms he offered. Her signature keeps him away from the sisters tonight and leaves a record Josephine would pay to bury. The officer sends a copy to the Lantern-Captain at Harding House.

### 6. Upstairs at Harding House

Josephine receives Alice before the guests sit down. She expects Nathaniel to slip out after dinner. She wants to know where he goes. She tells him in passing that Alice is waiting upstairs. Both hear precisely as much of the arrangement as Josephine wants them to hear.

Downstairs, Tamara catches Nixon on his way in. She has come to ask Hector to put off the announcement. Nixon sees her watching the door for her father. “Tomorrow will do,” he says. He gives her hand a brief squeeze and goes in to dinner.

In the little room off the hall, Hector is trying a question under his breath. “Before we go any further, Captain, when Nathaniel gave his account…” Tamara shuts the door. “If you put it like that, Eldgard will think the marriage depends on the answer. He’ll answer for the boy himself.” Hector asks how she would put it. “After the toast. Then you’re his father-in-law asking his friend what happened.” Hector reaches for the bell to cue the steward. She keeps her hand on it. “I’ll do it. I want a chair at the Works meeting tomorrow.”

“You read everything they send me.”

“I know. I’d like to hear what they leave out.”

Hector lets go of the bell. Through the open dining-room door Tamara can see Nixon settling into his place. She could still go over to him.

### 7. The toast

Tamara stands before the steward can call for quiet. Nathaniel is still chewing when she announces their engagement. Nixon looks up at the sound of her voice. She finishes looking straight at him. He turns to Nathaniel first and squeezes his shoulder exactly as he would have done if the news had made him happy. Nathaniel covers Nixon’s hand with his own. For a moment Nixon has to stand there and let his friend be grateful. When Nathaniel turns to accept the next hand, Nixon gets a moment beside her. “Did you speak to him?”

“Yes.”

Hector draws a chair up beside his for the conversation about the Works. Nixon sees him do it. “Congratulations, Tamara.”

She takes the chair.

### 8. Two drawings

Alone at the bench, the Lost One turns the sheet over. The reverse is blank. She takes a fresh sheet, lays it alongside and draws the curve she remembers facing her. Side by side, the sheets show opposite surfaces of the same wall. She folds hers small enough to keep. The exterior drawing stays in plain view.

### 9. A bill for the lights

The engineer brings Eldgard the idle-shift sheet for countersignature while the table is being cleared. He wants the men paid. Eldgard signs. For tomorrow he wants two estimates: the hours they can buy by stopping the Works, and the hours they can buy by cutting a residential district. The engineer must put both in writing. Outside the dining room he hears the footmen carrying in another course. He folds the signed wage sheet separately, to deliver to his men first. The district cut remains Eldgard’s decision in Episode 6.

### 10. The assurance

Hector brings up the inquiry while both families are still at table. “When Nathaniel gave his account, did he say anything in the study had been disturbed?” Nixon gives the answer Nathaniel gave him. “He said he saw nothing disturbed.” Hector accepts that and moves on. Nathaniel could correct it in front of everyone. He lets his friend’s answer stand.

### 11. Upstairs after dinner

Nathaniel finds Alice and starts telling her how wretched the announcement was. She has to ask what was announced. He tells her about Tamara. Alice asks when he first heard. He could say the families have only just made it public. Instead he gives her the day: lunch, before the Exhibition. She has been listening to him talk about Jamie for days, and that lunch has come up before. She puts his coat back on its peg. He asks her to wait while he finds Nixon.

### 12. A witness for sale

Ajax returns to Shadwell with Jennie. Grise wants to know what the girl signed. Jennie repeats her statement in front of the women who saw Grise hand her over. Grise cannot punish her for telling the customer precisely what he paid to learn without showing them all how little her protection is worth. She sends Jennie back to work. At the wash tubs the other women make room for her. Ajax leaves with a name to give Hector and no address.

### 13. The second statement

The copy from the Guard office is on Nixon’s desk when Nathaniel produces the shard. Nixon lets him finish. Then he unfolds Jennie’s statement and asks whether the woman she named was the woman Nathaniel helped at the market. Nathaniel owns his part in her escape. He refuses to give her whereabouts. Nixon now has two people willing to put their names to seeing the woman, and neither saw her kill Jamie. He opens his journal to a clean page. “Was Jamie alive when you reached him?” Last time he asked, his pen was down. Nathaniel waits for him to finish writing.

### 14. The empty chair

Nathaniel tells Alice he is sleeping at Harding House. He has given Nixon reason to watch where he goes. Alice returns to the workshop alone. The Lost One has cleared a place on the bench for Nathaniel to sit beside her. Alice tells her why he stayed away. After a while the Lost One picks up the folded sheet and puts it back in her pocket.
