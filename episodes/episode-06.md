# Episode 6 — The Remembering (aftermath of the Nixon break)

**Cold open:** None. Straight in.

**Change:** Nathaniel learns what his father did to him. **POV:** Nathaniel and Byron.
- The Lost One asks Alice to bring Nathaniel back when it is safe. She gives him both drawings. He now has evidence she knows the inside of the ornament. How they arrange a safe return remains to be broken.
- **Imprint reveal #1:** the full 1873 night. His genius was planted; his father erased him.
- Byron tells Nathaniel what the light is: a woman in a pod under the Works. "You were always enough."
- Nathaniel and Alice: the night they finally stop circling. **That night Alice tells him herself that his mother hired her to get close to him: paid at first, never after.** It costs her with him going into Ep 7.
- Josephine follows up on Alice’s reports through Grise. Grise has tested the returned Miriam herself. Grise reminds her she's been buying her own sentence since Episode 2: "You were the one holding the knife. And you hired her sister." Josephine connects the timing to the escape from the ornament: the sister she hired is living with the face of the girl she killed. She pays to silence Jennie. Open beat: the precise detail Alice believes safe to report.
- **Alice's hunt reaches Grise.** Grise, out of cruelty, tells her what Miriam paid to join the Vykra: their stepfather, drowned at sixteen. Alice walks home with it.
- **Tag:** Eldgard orders the East End cut to keep the West lit. The green left in the severed Gin Lane branch goes unstable. The washhouse block explodes. Byron survives inside the crystal shield cast by Eldgard’s ring.

## Scenes

### The Works meeting — morning after the toast

Hector has left the engineer’s figures at Tamara’s place. She reads them while Eldgard asks how many hours cutting the East will buy. The engineer gives him the number. Tamara has a finger beside the same figure on the page. “And when do they come back on?” The engineer waits for Eldgard. Eldgard asks for the next estimate. The engineer turns his page.

Last night she wanted to hear what went missing from the papers. By the time the meeting ends she understands that nobody has agreed when the East will get its light back. She keeps her copy of the figures. Eldgard makes the decision at the end of the episode.

### Gin Lane washhouse

The engineer has pencilled “fractured district housing” beside Gin Lane. Tamara asks if they can empty the branch before the cut. Yes, if Eldgard gives up another hour of West End supply. He keeps the West lit. Tamara sends word to clear the washhouse. Her runner reaches the near end of the street.

At the washhouse Byron helps a laundress named Nell lower the drying racks to make room for families coming in out of the cold. An old man has brought his rent tin. Nell makes Byron take it for safekeeping so the man will lie down. When the lamps die, Nell is halfway across the floor with a blanket. Green light comes back through the soles of her shoes.

The shot stays outside long enough to see light run the length of the block. The street lifts. Both rows of houses go with it. In the sudden quiet the rent tin rolls across the washhouse tiles and comes to rest against something green. A tight shell has crystallized around Byron. His eyes are open inside it. There is nowhere left for the runner to deliver the warning.
